## 4. What the deliverables say now (the inputs for the feature analysis)

**The approved answer:** the largest opportunity is **helping worn-out caregivers get relief**.
- Exhaustion is the #1 problem for both audiences.
- 39% of caregivers say a break would help, but only 13% have ever used respite (AARP/NAC 2025, PDF pp.26-27).
- Of 341 caregiver posts about respite, 128 got a break, 78 couldn't and 81 were still trying.
- No consumer app helps caregivers get relief. Burnout and meditation apps are almost never used.
- **The 4 blockers:** the cost, a parent who says no, paperwork and rules to qualify, and family who won't help.
- The opportunity is helping caregivers **reach** relief, not providing the care.

**By audience (use case 1):**

| Audience | What we found | Scenarios to support |
|---|---|---|
| Adult children | Can't get a break even when help exists. Cost, paperwork and a refusing parent block it, and siblings won't step in. | Find an affordable break they qualify for; get the parent to accept help; get relatives to cover time off; handle guilt and set limits |
| Spouses | Lose breaks too. Paperwork and rules (often the VA's) block them, and they turn to support groups more. | Find a support group or someone who understands; qualify for help, including VA programs; take a break knowing the partner is cared for; keep closeness |

**The 4 gaps** (competitive analysis, section 5):
1. No one handles a parent who refuses help or gets relatives to share the work.
2. Personal, ongoing guidance is locked behind an employer or a $150-an-hour bill.
3. Help with money and accounts works only if the parent prepared ahead of time.
4. Relief exists, but caregivers can't reach it.

**Competitor coverage of the top problems:**
- Exhaustion: partly (respite services and the VA, when families can get them)
- Dementia behavior: no
- The parent refusing help: no
- Money and accounts: partly (Everplans, Cariloop)
- Relatives not helping: partly (Caring Village, group texts)
- Choosing a place: partly (A Place for Mom)
