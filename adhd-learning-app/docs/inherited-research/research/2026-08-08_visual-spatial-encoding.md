## ⚠️ THE HEADLINE: the d = 0.88 anchor does not survive

**Source located:** Ondřej, J. (2025). *The method of loci in the context of psychological
research: A systematic review and meta-analysis.* British Journal of Psychology 116(4),
930–986. `10.1111/bjop.12799` · PMID 40457944.

The abstract does say d = 0.88. **The full text of the same paper says otherwise:**

| What the paper reports | Value |
|---|---|
| Abstract headline | d = 0.88, 95% CI [0.47, 1.25] |
| **Body — young adults, MoL vs rehearsal** (13 effect sizes, 936 participants) | **d = 0.42, 95% CI [0.00, 0.80]** ← lower bound touches zero |
| Studies at **high risk of bias** | **89.2%** |
| **GRADE certainty** | **"Very low"** |
| Publication bias | **BF = 6.41 × 10⁶** — "very strong evidence" |
| PET bias-correction estimate | **d = 0.00** |
| Heterogeneity | τ = 0.46 |

**Independent corroboration:** Twomey & Kroneisen (2021), *QJEP*,
`10.1177/1747021821993457` — 13 RCTs, **g = 0.65, 95% CI [0.45, 0.85]**. Medium, not large.

> **Action: downgrade from "High — rely on as-is" to "Moderate — directional."
> Plan against g ≈ 0.65, or d ≈ 0.4–0.5. Do not cite 0.88.**

## ⚠️ THE FINDING THAT THREATENS THE CORE PREMISE

**Alderson, Kasper, Hudec & Patros (2013).** *ADHD and working memory in adults: a
meta-analytic review.* *Neuropsychology*. PMID 23688211. **38 studies.**

| Domain | Deficit in ADHD adults |
|---|---|
| Verbal WM | **d ≈ 0.55** |
| **Visuospatial WM** | **d ≈ 0.49** |

**Both are moderately impaired, and by similar amounts.**

The design's thesis is that the verbal channel is *the specific bottleneck*, so encoding
should be routed through visual-spatial instead. **The meta-analytic data do not support a
visual-spatial strength in ADHD adults.** If anything visuospatial is very slightly less
impaired, but the gap is small and both are real deficits.

Method of Loci in particular is **visuospatial-WM-intensive during encoding**. Prescribing
it to a population with d ≈ 0.49 visuospatial WM deficit is defensible *only* if the
externalized nature of the method compensates for the internal deficit.
**No verified study tests this. Grade: Not covered.**

## ⚠️ The comparator problem — it explains most of the literature

**Nesbit & Adesope (2006).** *RER* 76(3). `10.3102/00346543076003413`. 67 effect sizes,
55 studies, 5,818 participants. **Full text obtained; tables extracted directly.**

| Comparison | d | k |
|---|---|---|
| Overall | 0.604 | 67 |
| Construct maps vs **lecture/discussion** (passive) | **0.742** | 10 |
| Construct maps vs **construct text or outline** (active) | **0.194** | 7 |
| Study maps vs **study outline or list** (active) | **0.278** | 10 |

The authors' own verbatim caution:

> *"We conjecture that many of the elevated effect sizes generated by these studies are due
> to lower effectiveness of the comparison treatment rather than to any particular benefit
> of mixed group and individual concept mapping."*

**Against a genuinely comparable active strategy, concept mapping's advantage collapses to
d ≈ 0.19–0.28.** Geography also drives it: Africa d = 1.446 (7 studies, nearly all one
Nigerian group); US/Canada d = 0.467; **Taiwan d = −0.003**.

> **Rule to adopt: every large effect size in this literature must be checked for whether
> the control condition was "listening to a lecture."**

## ⚠️ Retrieval practice beats concept mapping

**Karpicke & Blunt (2011).** *Science* 331(6018), 772–775. `10.1126/science.1199327` ·
PMID 21252317.

> *"Retrieval practice enhanced long-term learning above and beyond elaborative study with
> concept mapping by one and a half standard deviations."*

**d ≈ 1.0–1.5 favouring retrieval.** And critically: *"Even when the final test involved
using memory to construct a concept map, practicing retrieval during original learning
produced better performance."*

**Contradiction preserved:** Mintzes et al. (2011), *Science* 334(6055), 453,
`10.1126/science.1203698` — *"We question their findings on methodological and
epistemological grounds."* The objection is implementation fidelity: the mapping condition
was a single ~25-minute untrained session.

**Not adjudicated.** Defensible synthesis: Karpicke & Blunt is decisive against *naive,
one-shot, untrained* mapping as a substitute for retrieval practice. No adequately-powered
trial of *trained* mapping vs retrieval practice was located.

## ⚠️ The active ingredient is INTEGRATION, not the visual

**Leutner & Biele (2025).** *Without Integration, Everything Is Nothing: A Meta-Analysis of
the Effectiveness of Instructional Support for Drawing-to-Learn.* *Educ Psychol Rev*.
`10.1007/s10648-025-10067-7`. 14 studies, 16 comparisons, N = 1,213.

> *"Surprisingly, drawing support in general was not related to students' learning outcomes."*

| Condition | g |
|---|---|
| Support that does **NOT** facilitate verbal-pictorial integration | **−0.15** (negative) |
| Support that **DOES** facilitate integration | **+0.35** |
| Reducing extraneous load | n.s. |
| Increasing germane load | n.s. |

**Converging evidence:** Lawson & Mayer (2024), *Front Psychol* 15:1452385,
`10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1452385` — **Mayer's own lab**, two experiments:

| Contrast | Exp 1 (n=203) | Exp 2 (n=130, 1-week delay) |
|---|---|---|
| Summarizing vs control | **d = 0.48 ✓** | **d = 0.54 ✓** |
| **Drawing vs control** | **d = 0.23 n.s.** | **d = 0.11 n.s.** |

> *"There was no significant difference on the posttest for students who generated drawings
> compared to those who engaged in no generative learning activities."*

**Verbal summarizing beat drawing. Drawing did not beat doing nothing.** Their drawing
condition had no integration scaffold — exactly the g = −0.15 case.

> **If the system builds visual artifacts without forcing verbal-visual reconciliation,
> the evidence predicts a null-to-negative effect.**

## ⚠️ Handwriting may HURT ADHD learners

Two 2025 studies, same lab:

- **Shimko & James (2025)**, *Educational Psychology*, `10.1080/01443410.2025.2493257` —
  higher ADHD symptoms correlated with **slower handwriting speed and worse sustained
  attention**; note-taking of any kind beat none.
- **Shimko, Fyfe & James (2025)**, *Appl Cogn Psychol*, `10.1002/acp.70105` — verbatim:
  *"as inattentive ADHD symptoms increase, lecture learning **decreases** if notes were
  taken by hand and **increases** with tablet and laptop modalities."*

**This cuts directly against manual sketchnoting and hand-drawn mind mapping for exactly
the target population.** Proposed mechanism: handwriting's motor demands consume resources
ADHD learners cannot spare.
**Grade: Moderate — directional** (two studies, one lab, modest samples, classroom study
was correlational with self-selected modality).

## The one favourable signal — and it is an ABILITY moderator, not a style

Nesbit & Adesope (2006), Table 7 — aptitude-treatment interaction for *studying* maps:

| Learner profile | d | k |
|---|---|---|
| High ability | −0.133 (n.s.) | 5 |
| **Low ability** | **+0.404 ✓** | 5 |
| High verbal ability | −0.327 (n.s.) | 2 |
| **Low verbal ability** | **+0.436 ✓** | 2 |

If ADHD adults resemble the lower-verbal-throughput profile, this is the **most favourable
verified signal in the corpus** for the design's premise — and crucially it comes from an
**ability moderator**, not a "visual learner" style. That distinction is what keeps the
design out of learning-styles territory.

**Grade: Moderate — directional.** k = 2–5 per cell. Do not build load-bearing structure on it.

## What holds up

| Claim | Best number | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| MoL vs rehearsal | **g = 0.65** (Twomey & Kroneisen, 13 RCTs) | High |
| Concept mapping vs **passive** control | d ≈ 0.60–0.74 | High |
| Concept mapping vs **active** control | **d ≈ 0.19–0.28** | High |
| Constructing > studying maps | g = 0.72 vs 0.43 (Schroeder 2018, N=11,814) | High |
| Graphic organizers, learner-generated | g+ ≈ 0.59–0.63 | Moderate |
| Graphic organizers, instructor-provided (memory) | **g+ ≈ 0.70 — beats learner-generated** | Moderate |
| Drawing **with** integration | g = 0.35 | High |
| Drawing **without** integration | **g = −0.15** | High |
| Multimedia/dual coding overall | **g = 0.37, declining year over year** | High |
| **Text + static diagrams** | large, consistent across factual/inferential/transfer | High |
| ADHD adults visuospatial WM deficit | **d ≈ 0.49** | High |

**Cromley & Chen (2025)**, *Educ Res Rev* 49:100730, `10.1016/j.edurev.2025.100730` —
591 effects across Mayer's corpus. Overall **g = 0.37 with a small decline per year** (a
classic signature of early-literature inflation). The strongest, most consistent result in
the whole multimedia corpus is the humble one: **static text + diagrams**. No significant
effects for virtual reality.

## Not covered — do not extrapolate

| Topic | Status |
|---|---|
| Sketchnoting recall/comprehension | **No verified controlled study or meta-analysis exists** |
| Diagrammatic reasoning pooled effect size | **No meta-analysis located** |
| "Spatial schema construction" as a distinct method | **No evidence base under this label** |
| Any visual-spatial method RCT'd in **ADHD adults** | **Zero studies** |

The 2025 MoL meta-analysis **explicitly excluded psychiatric populations** — no ADHD or
neurodivergent samples at all.

---

## Three findings that should change the design

1. **The d = 0.88 anchor is the weakest number in the design, not the strongest.** Its own
   source rates the evidence "very low" and documents overwhelming publication bias.
2. **Comparator choice explains most of the apparent magnitude** across the entire
   visual-spatial literature — 0.19 vs 0.74 for the *same* intervention.
3. **The integration step is the active ingredient, not the visual.** Drawing without
   integration is g = −0.15. Retrieval (integrative) beats mapping. Summarizing
   (integrative) beat drawing (unscaffolded).
