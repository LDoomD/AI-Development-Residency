# Prior Art — AI/Adaptive Learning Systems for Neurodivergent Adults

> **RESEARCH TIER — NOT MERGED INTO THE DESIGN DOC.**
> **Date:** 2026-08-08 · **72 sources discarded** for failing verification.
> ⚠️ **Two were probable fabrications** produced by search summarizers — a "Smith & Taylor (2022)"
> Immersive Reader study and a "Hafiz" Microsoft-funded study. **Neither exists.**
> Verified via PubMed/PMC, DOI, Europe PMC, Semantic Scholar, **openFDA**, **SEC EDGAR**,
> **Companies House**, arXiv.

---

## The one-paragraph answer

**There is no verified intelligent tutoring system that was built for, and evaluated with,
neurodivergent adults as a diagnosed population. The category is essentially empty.**

What exists splits four ways: (1) cognitive training dressed as AI, whose adult evidence is
uncontrolled or null; (2) job-interview simulators for autistic adults — the only line with
real RCTs *and* real deployment; (3) commercial apps with real LLM use but zero clinical
claims, or clinical claims with zero evidence; (4) formative HCI studies of general-purpose
LLMs, n=3 to n=200, no learning outcomes.

> **The single strongest adult result in the entire corpus — adaptive dyslexia remediation,
> d = 0.70–0.96 — belongs to a research prototype with no commercial occupant.**

## ⭐ THE WHITESPACE

**Vender & Delfitto (2024)**, *Annals of Dyslexia* 75(1):42–70, PMC11954693.

RCT in **dyslexic adults 16–30**, 20 experimental / 18 control, 8 weeks × 3 sessions (~8h).

**The adaptive mechanism is embarrassingly simple:** on-screen stimulus persistence tapers
**1200 ms → 100 ms**, gated on >80% accuracy. No LLM. Closed-loop on performance.

| Outcome | Effect |
|---|---|
| Spelling | **d = 0.96** (p<.001) |
| Phonemic inversion | **d = 0.75** (p<.01) |
| Text reading | **d = 0.70** (p<.01) |
| Word reading | d = 0.46 (p<.01) |
| Nonword reading | n.s. (p=.079) |

These **exceed what any commercial product in this survey can demonstrate.**

**Negative-space confirmation:** a PubMed query for adult dyslexia + digital + randomized
returns **8 records total**; exactly **one other** is adaptive training for adult dyslexics
(Bertoni 2024, n=20). A separate Europe PMC sweep of 430 hits found **zero** adaptive
tutoring systems for adult dyslexia.

> **The adult-dyslexia ITS literature is functionally empty. Nobody sells this.**

**Grade: Moderate — directional.** Real RCT, real adults, real adaptivity — but n=38, single-lab.

## ⚠️ THE DESIGN WARNING — adaptivity without a floor amplifies the gap

**CSAL AutoTutor** (IES-funded adult literacy ITS). Fang et al. (2021), *IJAIED*,
**n = 252 adult learners.** Four behavioural clusters by accuracy × response time.

> **Verbatim: *"Gains in comprehension scores were highest for conscientious readers,
> lowest for struggling readers."***

**The ITS helped least the people it was built for.**

Two further points: participants were selected by **reading level, not diagnosis** — no CSAL
publication reports dyslexia, ADHD or autism status. And **there is no efficacy RCT of CSAL
AutoTutor**; only feasibility and pilot tests. The Center closed in 2022.

**Grade — "adaptive tutoring closes reading gaps in low-literacy adults": Not covered.**
**Grade — "adaptive tutoring differentially fails the lowest-skill learners": Moderate.**

## ⚠️ Do not chase objective cognitive transfer

| Study | n | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Bergmann et al. 2026**, *J Psychiatric Research*, PMID 42142506 | **77 adults with ADHD**, single-blind RCT, 12 wks | QoL improved; subjective attention/EF **d = 0.47–0.94**; but **"objective cognitive improvements were not observed"** and ADHD symptoms **not significant** |
| **Mawjee et al. 2015**, *PLoS One*, PMID 26397109 | **97** post-secondary students with ADHD, 3-arm RCT | **"only 2 of these findings remained robust after correcting for multiple comparisons"**; **"no evidence of any transfer effects"**; study **"failed to find robust evidence of benefits"** |

⚠️ **Same lab reversal:** Tannock's 2014 study (n=62) was positive; the larger, better-controlled
2015 study (n=97) reversed it. **Weight the null.**

⚠️ **Contradiction on which endpoint moves:** Bergmann found QoL moved but symptoms didn't;
a 2026 CBT meta-analysis (14 RCTs) found symptoms moved but **QoL didn't.**

**Grade — "adaptive cognitive training improves objective cognition in ADHD adults":
High — rely on as-is that it does NOT.**

## ⚠️ Almost nothing marketed as "AI" is actually adaptive

**Of ~25 products audited, exactly TWO adapt on a measured performance signal:**
Akili's SSME and Sincrolab's AiSA. Two more use LLMs for one-shot text transformation.
**The rest are UI, content libraries, or human services with a dashboard.**

| Product | What it actually is |
|---|---|
| **Floreo** | "Adaptivity" is **a human being** driving the lesson from an iPad in co-pilot mode |
| **Cognassist** | 30-min assessment → **human-authored strategy library** looked up by domain. Rule-based, not adaptive testing |
| **Brain.fm / Endel** | **Fully open-loop.** No sensor, no user-state input. Endel adapts to clock and GPS, not to whether you focused |
| **Goblin Tools** | LLM text transforms. The "spiciness" slider is **user-set, not inferred** |
| **Tiimo** | Own page puts adaptation in **future tense**: *"Soon, you will see even more intelligent prioritization…"* |
| **Nessy** | Static gamified phonics; adaptivity limited to placement and pass/repeat gating |
| **Lexplore** | Genuine ML (SVM on 168 gaze features) — but it's a **screener**. Adapts nothing. Trained on children |
| **Auticon / Daivergent / Focusmate / Amazing Marvin** | **No algorithm in the product at all** |

## ⚠️ The commercial category is financially unproven

**Akili Interactive — the flagship, and the flagship failure.**

Regulatory, verified in **openFDA** directly: `DEN200026` (EndeavorRx De Novo, 2020-06-15),
`K231337` (2023-12-13), **`K233496` (EndeavorOTC adults, 2024-06-14)**.

But the evidence:
- **Only true RCT** (STARS-ADHD, n=348, *Lancet Digital Health*): moved a computerized
  attention test (p=0.006) — and **all seven parent- and clinician-rated secondary endpoints
  failed** (adjusted p = 0.34–1.00)
- **The adult study has NO control group.** Single-arm, open-label, ~34% dropout. Practice
  effects and placebo cannot be separated. Akili funded and co-designed; multiple authors held equity

Financially: 2023 revenue **$1.7M against a $59.5M net loss**. Layoffs of ~⅓ (Jan 2023) then
**46% (Apr 2024)**. Nasdaq delisting notice. Sold to Virtual Therapeutics for **$34M** after a
**>$1B** SPAC valuation. SEC EDGAR confirms Form 15-12G filed 2024-07-12 — full deregistration.

> **EndeavorOTC won its adult FDA clearance two weeks after the company agreed to sell itself.**

**Elemy → Tilly:** $219M SoftBank Series B at **$1.15B valuation** (Oct 2021). July 2022 —
abruptly cut off in-person care to families **without notice**; staff laid off on brief video
calls. Feb 2024 — rebranded, **fired all remaining therapists**, pivoted to selling software
to the therapists it used to employ. **No algorithm ever. No studies ever.**

> **Two cautionary patterns: regulatory clearance did not create reimbursement (Akili), and
> capital did not create care (Elemy). Both were sold on adaptivity narratives that outran
> their evidence.**

## The one line that worked: VR interview training

**Smith et al.** (Northwestern → Michigan), simulator by SIMmersion. Authored branching +
scored feedback — **not** a learned student model, **not** an LLM.

| Study | n | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 2014 *JADD*, PMID 24803366 — **adults with ASD** | 26 | Single-blind RCT, live role-play interviews **p = 0.046**; 90% attendance |
| 2015 follow-up, PMID 25986176 | 23 | **OR = 7.82, p<.05** for attaining a competitive position |

**Left the lab:** 561 students, 47 schools, ~$100/trainee; dose-response identified
**nine completed virtual interviews** as the efficient dose.

⚠️ **Contradiction:** Ahn (2025) systematic review of VR/AR/MR social-skills training in ASD
(7 studies, 417 participants) concludes **"current evidence is insufficient to support
effectiveness."** Not adjudicated.

**Grade — adult efficacy: Moderate.** The only adult RCT is n=26 at p=0.046; the OR=7.82
rests on 23 people and is statistically fragile.

## LLM systems: entirely formative

**Nobody has shipped an LLM tutor with an adaptive learner model for this population.**

- **Jang et al., CHI '24** (CMU), n=11 autistic adults: GPT-4 vs a disguised human confederate
  on real workplace social questions. **Participants strongly preferred the LLM. An employment
  coach judged the LLM's advice questionable.** The finding is the *divergence*.
- **Carik et al., CHI '25**, survey of **200 autistic adults** + practitioners. Practitioners
  criticized LLM output as **"overly wordy, vague, and overwhelming."**
- **Noora**, *JADD* 2026, PMID 41934559 — **n = 3**, multiple-baseline. All three improved.

**Grade — "LLMs help neurodivergent adults with communication": Extrapolated.** Preference
data only, zero learning outcomes, consistent practitioner warnings that quality is worse
than users perceive.

## The human comparison class is unregulated

**Sibley et al. 2026**, *JAMA Network Open*, `10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.52407` — survey of
**481 US ADHD coaches**: **85% unlicensed · 89.3% no mental-health background · 91.5%
self-employed · 60.9% started during/after COVID.**

## What this means for building one

1. **The whitespace is real and specific.** Adaptive adult literacy/dyslexia remediation has
   the best measured effects in the corpus (d = 0.70–0.96) from the *simplest* mechanism —
   a performance-gated timing taper. **Nobody sells it.**
2. **Do not chase objective cognitive transfer.** Two independent RCTs say it doesn't happen.
   Design for the endpoint that *does* move — subjective function, quality of life, task
   completion — and measure it honestly.
3. **CSAL is the design warning.** An adaptive tutor produced the *smallest* gains for the
   lowest-skill learners. **Adaptivity without a floor amplifies the gap.**
4. **The evidence bar is on the floor.** A single properly controlled adult trial with a
   pre-registered primary outcome would put a product ahead of nearly every commercial
   competitor listed here.
