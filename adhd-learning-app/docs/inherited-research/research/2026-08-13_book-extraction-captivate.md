**Author:** Vanessa Van Edwards — self-described "recovering awkward person," founder of Science of People, a self-built "human behavior lab" (not an academic institution). No research credentials; her method is: find published studies → turn them into tactics → test on her student base and informal Twitter polls / lab "mini-experiments."

## Author's stated purpose

From the introduction, near-verbatim: "This book will teach you how people work… the people skills you never learned in school." Her frame is **behavior hacking** — a three-step loop she states explicitly: (1) find fascinating research, (2) create actionable real-life strategies, (3) test, tweak, and perfect. The book's promise is raising your "PQ" (interpersonal intelligence), with a before/after self-quiz. She insists throughout that every hack must be **authentic** — recurring refrain: never fake interest, never offer help you won't give, use attunement "only with those whom you truly want to build a deep connection with." Her closing thesis (via Dan Ariely): *you* are responsible for making every interaction captivating — no blaming boring counterparts.

## Framework — the underlying mental model

The book is organized by **time-depth of relationship**, which is itself the framework:

- **Part I — First five minutes** (Hacks 1–5): control the setting, nail the first impression, spark dopamine, make them feel good, find similarity.
- **Part II — First five hours** (Hacks 6–9): the **matrix** — a three-layer cipher you "solve" for each person: (1) Big Five personality traits, (2) appreciation language, (3) primary value. This is the book's core original contribution: a stacked people-reading system where each layer deepens prediction.
- **Part III — First five days** (Hacks 10–14): influence and durable connection — stories, ownership, vulnerability, de-escalation, attunement.

The engine underneath all 14 hacks, stated by her in ch. 14: the **reciprocity effect** — every hack works because social behavior is returned in kind (you smile → they smile; you're vulnerable → they're vulnerable; you're interested → they're interested).

## Claims to NOT repeat as fact

- **Ekman's 7 universal microexpressions** — the New Guinea universality work is real and historically important, but modern replication work (Barrett, Gendron et al.) has substantially contested strong universality claims. Teach the reads as *useful attentional prompts with honest uncertainty*, not as a lie-detection science. Never present microexpression reads as reliable deception detection.
- **Triple Threat posture claims** — the confidence-posture material is adjacent to power posing (Cuddy), whose hormonal claims failed replication. Visible hands and open posture are fine as social-signal common sense; drop any "your body chemistry changes" framing.
- **PQ statistics in the intro** ("high-PQ people earn $29,000 more," "90% of top performers," "42% happier") — sourced to EQ-industry marketing literature, not peer-reviewed PQ research. Do not repeat.
- **76% accurate first impressions / personality-from-face-shape** (ch. 7) — the face-perception studies show consensus in *judgments*, not accuracy of that magnitude; face-reading personality edges toward physiognomy. Skip.
- **5 Love Languages as science** — beloved framework, thin academic validation; use as shared vocabulary only.
- **Her lab's own numbers** (Twitter polls, Hot-or-Not photo coding, Fifty Shades of Awkward diaries, NYT viral coding) — informal internal experiments, not research. The tactics they decorate can stand on the underlying cited studies or as practitioner lore; the percentages cannot.
- **Emily/Bachelorette microexpression tables and similar TV analyses** — illustrative entertainment, not evidence.

## Compliance note specific to F&I

Van Edwards is the most explicitly ethics-forward author since Dreeke — she repeatedly conditions hacks on authenticity ("never offer help you can't give," attunement only for genuine connection, advice-asks only when real). The project's standing line still applies to the two sharp edges: **(1) behavioral reads (microexpressions, Big Five speed-reads, primary values) are rapport-and-service tools only — they must never touch credit, pricing, or approval decisions (Reg B fair lending), and (2) personality/value-tailored persuasion is compliant when it tailors *how* an honest recommendation is communicated, and non-compliant the moment it manufactures desire or steers product selection against the customer's interest.** Her price-flash-of-anger playbook (pause, explain the price rationale, invite questions) is a model CARS-friendly move: transparency in response to detected discomfort.

## Cross-book convergence (six books in)

- **The "because" justification** = Cialdini's Langer row, cited to the same study — second book anchoring on it; treat as High-evidence, library-standard.
- **NUT Job ≈ Voss's label→let-them-vent ≈ Schafer's anger cycle** — third independent de-escalation protocol with identical spine (name emotion → vent to physiological reset → only then problem-solve). De-escalation is now a 3-source convergence cluster like labeling was.
- **Franklin effect** appears in Schafer (ch. 3) and here (ch. 12), both citing Jecker & Landy; Cialdini's advice-asking-creates-unity is the same mechanism from a third angle.
- **Highlighter/Pygmalion labeling** converges with Voss ch. 3 (label positives to reinforce them) and Cialdini's "reputation to live up to" praise row.
- **Contempt-as-red-flag** (ch. 6) previews Gottman directly — she cites Gottman/Levenson's we-ness research in ch. 8. First bridge in the library toward the Relationships subject's planned Gottman material.
- **Thread Theory** = Schafer's similarity Law of Attraction + Cialdini's liking-via-similarity, operationalized into a search procedure (People/Context/Interests + Five Whys). Her unique contribution is the *procedure*, not the principle.
- **New unique contributions of this book:** the three-layer **matrix** (Big Five + appreciation language + primary value — the library's first stacked people-model), the **Personality Advantage pitch-adaptation lists** (most directly menu-applicable material), the **Story Stack** (first structured story inventory + anatomy), **Skill Solicitation** delegation, the **gremlin taxonomy**, and the **microexpression response playbook** (others read faces; she scripts the response).
