## Two homework pieces
1. A **research report (3 to 5 pages) and a slide deck** on one software layer.
2. A **PenTest report** on your Week 4 app. A PenTest means you act like a hacker, with permission, to find weak spots.

## Steps
- [ ] **1. [You]** Pick one layer: Presentation, Domain, Service, Data Source, or Database.
- [ ] **2. [Both]** Claude drafts the report and slide deck with the Security Research Report Template. The sections are: Introduction, Layer, Vulnerabilities, Challenges of Securing This Layer, How to Fix The Vulnerabilities. It covers what the layer does, the attacks that can happen there, and the OWASP rules for safe code. You check it.
- [ ] **3. [Claude]** Run a full PenTest of your Week 4 app with Claude Code and its agents, using the PenTest Report Template. **Test every layer.**
- [ ] **4. [Both]** Write the fixes the PenTest finds as requirements in Notion. Claude drafts them. You put them in Notion.
- [ ] **5. [Claude]** Save both reports in your repository, in a folder named `/security`.
- [ ] **6. [You]** Fill in the Submission Template yourself. No AI.
