# Overview

The world of engineering digital systems is wide and vast. For over 50 years, teams have worked to build and scale the ways that digital products live in the world. The Internet era in the 90’s and 2000’s forced teams to evolve how they offer digital experiences to people. Now, in 2026, the structure is evolving again. Artificial Intelligence systems are gaining speed in software lifecycles. Teams are utilizing AI in their development operations. People can code entire businesses in weeks.

The real question is: does that application survive when real people use it? Does it last? At the end of the day, the fundamentals of great systems engineering don’t go away. They become even more important when an AI can create an application in 1 day.

To make lasting software, teams must never abandon architectural principles. They must never abandon user experience principles. They must never abandon information security principles. This is the difference between “vibe coding” and actually engineering with AI tools. The engineering part.

Take a deep dive into everything that you nee to learn when engineering products using AI. The AI Development Residency is a rigorous 16 week training. You get 8 weeks of learning, being coached by an industry expert, and 8 weeks developing with AI tools on real projects. This training is offered by [Tech Fleet](http://techfleet.org), a public charity. [Tech Fleet](http://techfleet.org) is on the mission to build empowered team spaces in the world.

# Why Take This Training?

For UX designers and product folks, the lines of responsibility in the work world are blurring. More and more opportunities are arising for people who design to also develop; for people who manage products to also create. This residency teaches you how to build from 0 to 1. AI can help teams ship faster, but what ships still needs to be usable, secure, maintainable, and able to scale.

1. **Practice real cross-functional delivery - **This is not just a classroom class. After the first 8 weeks of instruction, you move into team project work (15–20 hours/week). It’s training for how modern teams actually operate: messy constraints, real business operations, fast iteration, client relationships, and shared responsibility for shipping something real.
2. **Build fast without cutting corners - **You’ll learn how to work with AI-assisted development while still protecting quality. The program draws a clear line between “vibe coding” and building products that survive real-world use. Good UX, solid architecture, and security are treated as required, not optional.
3. **Get the technical context that improves decisions - **The curriculum covers practical foundations that help UX/Product make better calls when roles blur. We cover how the web works, networks, data, security, system architecture, APIs/agents, and SEO/AEO. That means you know how to orchestrate everything with or without technical experts.
4. **Match where UX/Product roles are heading - **The skills mix classic product strengths with UX and technical strengths. It’s designed to help you operate in full cycle ”systems” roles.

## Weeks 1 - 8: Instructor Training

### Training Overview

During the first half of the residency, you train under an instructor in different areas, and have 8 coding challenges using AI tools to practice your craft.

This training will start when the class starts.

### Premium Residency Specialized Projects

If you register for the Premium Residency, you will work with the instructor to develop a separate project in a particular area. This is on top of the coding challenges. You will receive one-on-one coaching to make this project possible. Use this project and any other coding challenge to put on your resume and portfolio.

Topics we accept for specialized projects:

1. AI ethics
2. AI Design
3. AI Automation
4. Content management systems
5. Local AI models
6. Advanced information security
7. Advanced agentic workflows
8. Web performance
9. Advanced data engineering
10. Conversational design
11. Something you choose (the instructor must approve)

## Weeks 9 - 16: Team-Based AI Development Training

**We estimate the time period for this will start in January 2027.**

During the second half of the residency, you join a real cross-functional team with real clients and develop using AI tools. We will pay for Claude for you during this time.

During this time, residents join real project training teams that Tech Fleet sponsors and perform AI development activities with teams and clients. Residents will work directly with the residency instructor in group settings. Premium residents will receive extra one-on-one coaching hours. Residents work with the instructor to coordinate which projects they want to join. Tech Fleet will provide a list of options for residents to choose. Residents will join projects in development teams of up to 5 people per project.

The schedule for your involvement is based on the team’s and your own availability. Expect to spend 15 to 20 hours per week doing project work.
