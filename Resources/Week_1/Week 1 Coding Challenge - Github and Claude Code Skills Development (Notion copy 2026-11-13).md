# Summary

How does AI remember? Well, it doesn’t remember anything by default! If you want to get an AI model to do what you want it to do in proper ways, you will need to make it remember what to do, why, and how. This is where you as an AI Developer come in: you can create your own content called Skills to ensure your AI model remembers what, why, and how. This is fundamental to building enterprise-ready applications consistently.

# Objective

Build a brain for your Claude to understand how to properly do 5 things that are needed for strong enterprise-level development. You research what those are in Claude, you pick which 5 you want to focus on, and then you develop the skills content with Claude,. Drop it into Claude in the place where it goes so your AI model can actually use it.
