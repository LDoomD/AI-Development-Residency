## 4. What the deliverables say now

**The approved answer:** the largest opportunity is **helping worn-out caregivers get relief** (segments 11 and 12).
- **The biggest need:** exhaustion is the #1 problem for both audiences.
- **Big enough:** the adult day care market alone is $19.3B worldwide (2025).
- **The need isn't met:**
  - Of 341 posts about respite, 128 had gotten a break, 78 couldn't get one and 81 were still trying.
  - Nationally, 39% say a break would help, but only 13% have ever used respite (AARP/NAC 2025, PDF pp.26-27).
- **No one solves it:** there's no consumer app for getting relief, and caregivers almost never use burnout or meditation apps.
- **What stops caregivers:** the cost, a parent who says no, paperwork and rules to qualify, and family who won't help.
- **What it is not:** the care itself is a physical service. The opportunity is helping caregivers **reach** relief.

**By audience (use case 1):**
- **Adult children** can't get a break even when help exists. Cost, paperwork and a refusing parent block breaks, and siblings won't step in.
- **Spouses** lose breaks too. Paperwork and rules (often the VA's) block them, and they turn to support groups more often.

**Other verdicts that changed today** (market opportunity):

| Segment | Verdict now |
|---|---|
| Family coordination apps | The need is real, but apps don't solve it. (It was "Best fit," based on unsaved numbers.) |
| Digital vaults (the parent's accounts) | Clear need, hard to enter because of bank rules and security risk. (It was "Not a clear need.") |
| Respite and burnout support | "Core" in the relationship column, our biggest opportunity |
