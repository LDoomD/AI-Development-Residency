## 1. Purpose of this table

- **Plain words:** the table puts a dollar size on each market near the caregiver problem. For each one it shows how big it is today, how big it is expected to be, who the top companies are, where the number came from, what to be careful about, and how much to trust it.
- **How it differs from tables 1 and 2:** table 1 was a broad scan. Table 2 mapped who is out there and what problems they solve. This table sizes the segments and rates the evidence (owner's description, 2026-11-08).
- **What it can answer:** how big each segment is, which ones grow fastest, and how much confidence the numbers deserve.
- **What it cannot answer:** how much of any market is caregivers, or who pays. The notes say again and again that the numbers are not caregiver-specific.

## 2. How it connects to the Discovery milestone

Discovery means learning the "current state" of the market, user needs, current behavior, and competitor opportunities before planning a solution. This table answers: how big is the opportunity, which segments grow fastest, and how much to trust the numbers (owner's description, 2026-11-08).

## 5. The most important things this table shows (all **Proposed**)

1. **Only 1 of 16 rows is rated High, and it is not about caregivers.** Password Manager (three firms agree, $3.1B to $3.75B) is the High row. Its note says "Not caregiver-specific." Counts: Low 8, Medium 7, High 1.
2. **The two markets built for caregivers are both rated Low.** Caregiver App Market ($1.71B, firms disagree widely) and Digital Caregiver Platforms ($547.5M, only one firm). Both are small in dollars.
3. **Both Core segments are rated Medium.** Adult Day Care ($19.3B) and Mental Health Apps ($7.48B to $8.2B). Neither is measured for caregivers. The adult day number is global: the US-only figure is $7.6B.
4. **The biggest numbers are the least useful.** Elderly Care Services ($804.7B) and Telehealth ($27.1B to $126.1B) are the largest. Elderly Care is rated Low because it measures all senior care. Telehealth is rated Low because three firms disagree widely.
5. **Growth is fastest where evidence is weak or the topic is far from caregivers.** AI Medical Scribing 19.21% (Low), Password Manager 18.5% (High, not caregiver-specific), Digital Caregiver Platforms 16.9% (Low), Caregiver Apps 16% (Low).
