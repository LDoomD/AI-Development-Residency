## Use cases by audience (scenario-based)

### Active job seekers & career changers

**Problem: can't tell if they're qualified before applying,** so they apply blindly or self-reject.

- *When it happens:* scrolling listings and deciding whether to apply; after a string of rejections or silence; when a JD lists more requirements than they have; when unsure which roles are worth their time.
- *Business goals:* know before applying whether a role truly fits; stop wasting effort on wrong-fit roles; focus where they can realistically win; apply with confidence, not anxiety.
- *Future scenarios to support:* instantly show role-fit + what's missing; give a go / stretch / no-go call per posting; recommend which gaps to close; prioritize a shortlist of winnable roles.

**Problem: job titles don't reliably describe the actual job,** so they misjudge fit, seniority and pay.

- *When it happens:* comparing listings with the same title; when a 'manager' role is really an IC; deciding what level to target; level/salary negotiation.
- *Business goals:* see the real role behind the title; understand true seniority; avoid under-/over-applying; negotiate from accurate information.
- *Future scenarios to support:* standalone title-fit verdict (match / mismatch / stretch); title-inflation detection and true-seniority read; compare real scope to the user's level; flag level/comp context.

### New grads, students & bootcamp candidates

**Problem: can't decode what a JD is really asking for,** or whether they qualify.

- *When it happens:* a first-ever job search; right after graduation or a bootcamp; when a JD is full of jargon and '3–5 yrs experience'; when unsure what to put on a resume.
- *Business goals:* understand expectations in plain language; build confidence to apply; reduce self-selecting out of good roles; see realistic entry paths.
- *Future scenarios to support:* translate the JD into plain-language expectations; explain must-have vs nice-to-have; map coursework/projects to requirements; show realistic ways to qualify.

**Problem: interview prep is generic,** so they walk in unprepared for the specific role.

- *When it happens:* after landing an interview; the night before with generic question lists; never interviewed for this role type; when anxiety is highest.
- *Business goals:* prep for the actual role, not interviews 'in general'; reduce anxiety; perform at true ability; fast, focused prep.
- *Future scenarios to support:* generate role-specific likely questions; show what the JD will probe and why; practice + feedback tied to the role; a per-interview prep checklist.

### Mid-career professionals & career switchers

**Problem: can't tell how their experience maps onto a new title or industry.**

- *When it happens:* pivoting careers/industries; returning after a gap; eyeing a stretch role; translating transferable skills.
- *Business goals:* see how background maps to the role; reframe transferable experience; make confident pivot decisions; know real gaps vs reframable ones.
- *Future scenarios to support:* map existing experience to the role's expectations; surface transferable-skill matches; separate genuine gaps from reframable experience; guidance on positioning the pivot.

**Problem: title inflation / mismatch makes it hard to target the right level and pay.**

- *When it happens:* comparing roles across companies; weighing a lateral vs step up; salary/level negotiation; when down-leveling risk is high.
- *Business goals:* target the right seniority; avoid accidental down-leveling; negotiate from accurate level data; invest time only in right-level roles.
- *Future scenarios to support:* true-seniority read; title-inflation detection; level and compensation context; fit at the user's target level.

### Career centers, bootcamps & workforce programs (B2B)

**Problem: advisors can't deliver individualized, role-specific prep to every student at scale.**

- *When it happens:* peak placement season; large cohorts, limited advisor hours; reporting outcomes to stakeholders/funders; demand exceeds capacity.
- *Business goals:* scale 1:1 guidance to every student; improve placement rates; free advisor time for high-value coaching; measurable, trackable readiness.
- *Future scenarios to support:* automated per-student role-fit + prep; cohort readiness dashboards; advisor-assist flagging who needs help; outcome tracking (application → interview → offer).

**Problem: students apply to poorly-matched roles, get discouraged, and hurt placement metrics.**

- *When it happens:* early in the search; after repeated rejections; aiming too high or too low; when drop-off sets in.
- *Business goals:* improve match quality; reduce discouragement and drop-off; raise placement and employer satisfaction; stronger outcome data.
- *Future scenarios to support:* guide students to winnable roles; readiness-based shortlists; nudges to close the right gaps; track application → interview conversion by cohort.

## Motivations & goals

| Audience | What motivates them to act | Situational goals (while job finding) | Life goals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Active job seekers & career changers** | Urgency of income/stability; frustration with rejection & silence; fear of wasting time on wrong roles; desire for control; a hard deadline (job ending, relocation, visa) | Get interviews faster; focus on winnable roles; know they're qualified before applying; prep efficiently without burnout; cut wasted applications | Financial stability for family; career progression & higher earning power; meaningful work; work-life balance; control over their future |
| **New grads, students & bootcamp candidates** | Pressure to land a first job (debt, parents, peers, program stats); anxiety about a new process; wanting to prove their investment; fear of the unknown interview | Understand what employers expect; build confidence to apply/interview; get a first role; prep for specific interviews; translate education/projects into qualifications | Launch a career & gain independence; financial freedom; prove the degree/bootcamp paid off; long-term growth; build identity & standing in their field |
| **Mid-career professionals & career switchers** | Desire for change/growth or escape from stagnation/burnout; layoffs/instability; wanting better pay/title/purpose; pivot to stable/passion-aligned field; life changes | Reposition experience for a new field; target the right level; prep for higher-stakes interviews; make a confident pivot; know real vs reframable gaps | Fulfillment & purpose; financial advancement through transition; long-term employability; career aligned with life stage; autonomy & respect at the right seniority |
| **Career centers, bootcamps & workforce programs (B2B)** | Accountability for placement rates; employer/funding/accreditation pressure; limited staff vs rising demand; competition for enrollment; need to justify ROI | Scale individualized prep; raise placement & interview rates; improve match quality & reduce drop-off; measure & report readiness; free advisor time | Student success & economic mobility (mission); strong institutional reputation; sustainable funding & enrollment; lasting employer partnerships; long-term results |
