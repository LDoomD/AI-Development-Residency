## Resume / JD Matching & ATS Optimization

### Jobscan (Direct) · [jobscan.co](https://www.jobscan.co/)

- **Main features:** ATS Resume Scanner with match-rate report; 'see your missing skills' (hard/soft skills + keywords); AI Optimize (resume tailoring); AI Resume Builder; Cover Letter Generator; LinkedIn Optimization; Job Match; Auto Apply (new); Job Application Tracker; AI Interview Practice (new).
- **How they differentiate:** positions as the ATS authority. Claims to 'reverse-engineer' specific ATS (Greenhouse, Lever, Taleo, iCIMS) and tailor advice to each system's parsing rules.
- **Does well:** most mature, broadest all-in-one job-search suite; strong ATS credibility; used by hires at Apple/Google/Amazon; serves orgs (universities, nonprofits).
- **Does not do well:** interview prep and job-title fit are recent, shallow add-ons; match rate can encourage keyword-stuffing; paid gating of scans.
- **Customers say:** strong reputation for landing interviews ('3x more callbacks' claim); users value the ATS match rate; some find it pricey.
- **Gaps not filled:** a JD-driven 'what will be expected of me / am I a title fit' narrative. Output is keyword-match %, not role-readiness or interview coaching depth.
- **UVP:** the most trusted ATS resume optimizer. Know exactly how a specific ATS will read your resume before you apply.

### Teal (Direct) · [tealhq.com](https://www.tealhq.com/)

- **Main features:** AI Resume Builder; Job Tracker (Chrome extension, 40+ boards); Resume Keyword Scanner; Resume-Job Description Match (Match Score); ATS Resume Checker; Cover Letter Generator; AI Interview Practice; salary-negotiation guidance.
- **How they differentiate:** 'Career CRM'. One organized workspace (tracker + tailored resumes side by side with the JD); generous freemium.
- **Does well:** loved for organization and fast per-job tailoring; 4M+ users, 4.4+ rating; explicit JD Match Score.
- **Does not do well:** no real job-matching engine or auto-apply; no LinkedIn optimizer; weekly (\$13/wk) pricing frustrates some; interview practice is secondary.
- **Customers say:** praised as all-in-one 'CRM + resume + cover letter'; valued for reducing application tedium.
- **Gaps not filled:** deep interview-readiness coaching and role-expectation gap analysis. It tells you resume-to-JD fit, not how to prepare for the interview.
- **UVP:** organize your whole search and tailor a resume to every job from one CRM-style workspace.

### Rezi (Direct) · [rezi.ai](https://www.rezi.ai/)

- **Main features:** AI Resume Builder; AI Resume Agent; Resume Checker; Resume Keyword Scanner / Keyword Targeting (paste the JD); bullet and summary writers; Cover Letter Generator; AI Interview practice; Rezi job matches.
- **How they differentiate:** '#1 AI Resume Builder' (Forbes claim); automation-first, the AI Agent drafts the whole resume; 4.5M+ users.
- **Does well:** fast, highly automated resume creation; clean ATS output; JD keyword targeting; strong brand/SEO.
- **Does not do well:** resume-centric; title-fit analysis and interview prep are thin; limited tracking; no LinkedIn/auto-apply.
- **Customers say:** well reviewed as a quick ATS resume builder; Forbes 'top pick' positioning.
- **Gaps not filled:** a standalone 'does this title match the JD + how do I prep' advisor. Rezi stops at producing the document.
- **UVP:** build an ATS-proof resume in minutes with AI doing the heavy lifting.

## AI Interview Preparation & Mock Interviews

### Final Round AI (Direct, interview) · [finalroundai.com](https://www.finalroundai.com/)

- **Main features:** Interview CoPilot (real-time answers in live interviews); Practice Interview (8 scenarios incl. coding/behavioral/salary); auto Interview Debrief; Screen Help; Stealth Mode; JD parsing + prep checklist; AI Salary Calculator.
- **How they differentiate:** real-time, 'undetectable' in-interview assistance (hidden from screen share). A category our app does not touch.
- **Does well:** best-in-class live assistance + structured practice/debrief loop; 10M+ users; 4.9 on Product Hunt; SOC2/GDPR.
- **Does not do well:** controversial 'stealth' use raises ethics and integrity concerns; desktop-only; Pro required for live sessions; no resume builder or job matching.
- **Customers say:** praised for realistic mock interviews and real-time help ('11/10'); some cite high cost.
- **Gaps not filled:** pre-interview skills gap analysis and honest preparation. It optimizes in-the-moment answers more than long-term readiness.
- **UVP:** ace the interview itself with a private AI co-pilot that feeds you answers live, then debriefs you.

### Yoodli (Indirect) · [yoodli.ai](https://yoodli.ai/)

- **Main features:** AI roleplays / mock conversations with feedback; speech coaching (pace, filler words, clarity); Personas; Coach; Measure analytics. Interview coaching reaches job seekers mainly via partners (LHH, Spencer Stuart).
- **How they differentiate:** enterprise 'AI platform for human skills' (sales enablement, L&D); 'assist, not replace'; roleplay realism; backed by Google.
- **Does well:** excellent communication/speech feedback and realistic roleplay; enterprise-grade (SOC2, SSO); TIME Best Invention 2026.
- **Does not do well:** pivoted to B2B enablement, not a job-seeker product; no resume/JD/ATS/job-search features.
- **Customers say:** 4.8 on G2, 1M+ learners; praised by Google/Salesforce for realistic roleplays.
- **Gaps not filled:** a job-seeker-facing, JD-aware prep flow. Yoodli coaches delivery but is not built around a specific posting or the hiring funnel.
- **UVP:** build human/communication skills at scale through realistic AI roleplays.

### Big Interview (Direct, interview) · [biginterview.com](https://biginterview.com/)

- **Main features:** Learn to Interview (video curriculum); Interview Simulator; Video AI feedback; Big Resume (AI resume builder + course); large question bank + sample answers.
- **How they differentiate:** a 'training platform' combining curriculum with practice, often sold to universities and career centers.
- **Does well:** strong teaching and answer frameworks; good for beginners; institutional distribution; lifetime option.
- **Does not do well:** dated vs AI-native rivals; no JD scan, job matching, auto-apply, LinkedIn, or live copilot; resume is a lighter add-on.
- **Customers say:** widely praised by students and advisors for building real interview skills and confidence.
- **Gaps not filled:** JD-specific, personalized 'what this exact role expects' analysis. Content is generic interview training.
- **UVP:** a proven, step-by-step system to learn and practice interviewing, not just a tool.

## AI Career Coaching & Job-Search Platforms

### [Jobright.ai](http://Jobright.ai) (Direct) · [jobright.ai](https://jobright.ai/)

- **Main features:** Personalized AI Job Matches (8M+ jobs); 1-Click Application Autofill; Job-Specific Tailored Resume; Insider Referrals; Orion 24/7 AI Career Copilot; Interview Questions; AI Cover Letter; AI Job Tracker.
- **How they differentiate:** end-to-end 'AI job-search copilot' that automates the funnel from match to apply; featured by OpenAI.
- **Does well:** powerful matching + autofill at scale (claims 80% time saved, 3x interviews); networking/referrals; 3M+ users.
- **Does not do well:** breadth over depth; interview prep is a question list, not coaching; no LinkedIn optimizer or salary data; matching-quality complaints common in the category.
- **Customers say:** Trustpilot-reviewed; users cite tripled interview rate and big time savings.
- **Gaps not filled:** deep, honest role-readiness and interview coaching. It optimizes getting applications out, less so succeeding in the interview.
- **UVP:** put your job search on autopilot: matched jobs, tailored resume and autofill in under a minute.

### [Careerflow.ai](http://Careerflow.ai) (Direct) · [careerflow.ai](https://www.careerflow.ai/)

- **Main features:** Advanced AI LinkedIn Optimizer (flagship); ATS Score & Keyword Analyzer; AI Resume Builder; AI Cover Letter; AI Mock Interview (new) + Interview Analysis; Job Tracker; Application Autofill; coaching + salary-negotiation strategies.
- **How they differentiate:** LinkedIn-first optimization plus a broad toolkit and human-coach marketplace.
- **Does well:** strong LinkedIn optimization (a gap most rivals skip); broad toolkit incl. mock interview; coaching add-on.
- **Does not do well:** jack-of-all-trades; no strong job-matching feed; feature depth varies; interview analysis newer and less proven.
- **Customers say:** positioned as all-in-one 'career copilot'; valued for LinkedIn + resume improvements.
- **Gaps not filled:** a focused JD → role-fit → interview-prep workflow. Interview prep is one of many tabs.
- **UVP:** optimize your LinkedIn and run your whole job search from one AI copilot.

### Huntr (Direct) · [huntr.co](https://huntr.co/)

- **Main features:** Job Tracker (flagship); Advanced Job Match Score (compares JD keywords + qualifications + responsibilities to your resume and scores coverage gaps); Resume Keyword Scanner; JD Keyword Finder; AI Resume Builder + Tailor; Resume Checker/Review; AI Cover Letters; Autofill; Contact + Interview Trackers; Interview Question Generator.
- **How they differentiate:** best-in-class tracking plus the most granular JD-vs-resume gap analysis (keywords AND responsibilities/qualifications).
- **Does well:** closest to our core idea on paper; explicitly extracts JD responsibilities/qualifications and flags what your resume doesn't cover; 500k+ users; 4.9 extension.
- **Does not do well:** prep is only a question generator + tracker (no mock interview or coaching); no live copilot; no job-matching feed; no true LinkedIn optimizer.
- **Customers say:** praised for organization and fast tailored resumes; strong Chrome-extension reviews.
- **Gaps not filled:** turning the gap analysis into interview preparation and title-fit judgment. It shows resume coverage gaps, not how to prepare.
- **UVP:** organize your search and tailor every resume with an accurate JD match score.
