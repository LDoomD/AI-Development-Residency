# Qualitative research findings (Reddit and forums)

Discovery research on what family caregivers describe in their own words. Problems only, no solutions. Written 2026-11-01.

## Method

- **Sources:** a NotebookLM notebook of about 93 public pages: Reddit threads (r/CaregiverSupport, r/dementia, r/AgingParents, r/eldercare and others) and a few forums (AgingCare, ALZConnected, Carer Gateway). Each page is a snapshot of the post and, for some, its comments.
- **How it was read:** the user selected the sources in NotebookLM, in batches of 10 to 23, and ran a prompt asking for each source's main problem and one exact quote. The user pasted the output to Claude, which removed repeats by title and link, grouped the sources by theme, and counted them.
- **Type of research:** secondary qualitative research (existing posts, not interviews). The theme counts are a light numbers layer, not a statistical sample.

## What caregivers describe

About 86 distinct sources, one main theme each. Counts are rough.

| Theme | Sources |
|---|---|
| Choosing, placing and paying for care (home care vs memory care vs facility) | 21 |
| Burnout, guilt and exhaustion | 21 |
| Updates, coordination, siblings and long-distance care | 15 |
| Medications (tracking, refills, many prescriptions) | 11 |
| Insurance, benefits and money | 5 |
| Parents refusing help | 3 |
| New diagnosis, "where do I start" and grief | 3 |
| Single mentions (wandering, scams, phones, rides) | 7 |

## Strongest quote for each theme

Word for word, as returned by NotebookLM.

- **Choosing care:** "A case manager handed us a printed list of 10+ rehab facilities and told us we needed to decide by end of day."
- **Burnout:** "Now after so many years I am stuck caring for a woman I do not like. I don't know who I am. I feel lost. Angry. Guilty, and trapped. I have no way out.Suffocating."
- **Updates:** "I am weary from texting everyone separately and relaying all the nuances of what the last person handled." Another: "everything ends up scattered between texts and calendars."
- **Siblings:** "They will text me things like "thank you for all you do," but they never actually step in or offer real help."
- **Long distance:** "I'm really struggling with all the unexpected long distance admin coordination (everything from occasional meals, to domestic travel, to diabetes monitoring, to actual health scares)."
- **Medications:** "Between managing their schedule, refills, and making sure I don't miss potential drug interactions, it can feel very very overwhelming at times."
- **Insurance:** "The company gets to halt the claim once a lawsuit is filed. Then they stall/delay until my mother dies."

## What people already do

- **Medications:** pill boxes, Dixie cups, spreadsheets, whiteboards, PillPack, automatic dispensers, Medisafe, shared Google files. This is the most-described area.
- **Updates:** group texts, CaringBridge, WhatsApp. Several posters say group texts are too noisy.
- **Checking on a parent from far away:** door and motion sensors, Apple Watch, AirTags, cameras (some parents object to being watched), daily check-in calls.
- **Existing apps:** the market is crowded. At least 8 vendors pitched their own apps inside the threads.
- **One reported gap:** a medication app that handles a caregiver tracking both their own meds and a parent's, with shared visibility. This is one poster's claim. It has not been checked whether that poster is a caregiver or a builder.

## Limits

- Reddit and a few forums only, as snapshots. People who post online skew toward distress and toward asking for tools.
- Counts are rough: one main theme per source, grouped partly by Gemini. One early batch of 20 had no titles in its output, so it could not be checked for repeats. The true distinct count may be a little under 86.
- Some quote rows did not match their summaries. Only quotes that could be read as stated are used above.
- Claude cannot open Reddit, so quotes were not checked against the live posts. Typos kept in quotes suggest the text was copied, not paraphrased.
- Stuck prescription renewals barely appear (one post, from a patient).
- Usernames are left out on purpose.
