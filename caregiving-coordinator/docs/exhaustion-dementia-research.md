# Exhaustion and dementia behavior: demand, market size, and who already serves it (2026-11-02)

Why this was done: the audience work showed exhaustion and dementia behavior as the most common problems (about 41% and 25% to 33% of our forum threads), but the ten competitor rows and the market research did not cover them. This looks only at those two problems. It does not choose a problem. No solutions or features are proposed.

Evidence states: **documented** = read in a saved primary source, page given; **reported** = a paid-report summary, not checked; **not re-checked** = quote not re-verified (see limits). PDF page numbers are PDF pages, not printed pages.

## 4. What this shows (reading, mine; not a decision)

1. **Services exist for both problems.** The earlier statement "no tool covers these" was true only for our ten competitor rows. Outside them there is a free nonprofit helpline, a respite locator, employer-paid coaching, a Medicare benefit and a paid companion service.
2. **Who pays differs from our audiences.** Most of what exists is free (nonprofit), paid by an employer or health plan, or paid by Medicare. In what we checked, the only family-paid price we could verify is Papa at $30 an hour; the other family-paid price (Positive Approach to Care) was not re-checked.
3. **The gap AARP names is use, not supply.** 39% say respite would help and 13% use it, with trust, shame, quality worries and application hassle named as reasons (from other studies the report cites).
4. **Dementia behavior help exists as information and training** (helpline, GUIDE caregiver training, courses). Our forum threads show caregivers still writing about it in the moment, which these pages do not address; the pages say nothing about what happens when a caregiver is in the middle of a hard call.
5. **Spousal dementia caregivers face more burden over time** per the Facts and Figures (PDF p.55: "Spousal dementia caregivers are more likely than non-spousal dementia caregivers to experience increased burden over time"). That bears on Audience 2.

## 5. Limits

- Ten offerings were chosen by the research agent from a list I gave it; this is not all of the market.
- Market size figures are for care services, from free report pages.
- "Who pays" is unknown for five offerings because the pages saved do not say.
- Forums were not crawled further in this pass; the 117 caregiving-only threads we already have were not added to.
- Nobody was asked whether they would use or pay for any of this.
