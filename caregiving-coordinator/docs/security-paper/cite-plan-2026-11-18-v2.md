## Corrections the paper must follow
- **BLS:** use the 2025 to 2035 edition (median pay $129,180 in May 2025, 21% growth). It is a projection.
- **ZAP:** left OWASP in 2023; call it "ZAP by Checkmarx," never an OWASP project.
- **Next.js:** five middleware CVEs are confirmed; do not cite a sixth.
- **ISC2 2025 study:** gives no workforce size or gap number (p. 3). It is a self-reported survey.
- **OWASP Top 10:** cite the 2025 list. There is no web "Top 10 2026."
- **Saltzer and Schroeder:** cite by section name; the saved copy has no journal pages. Confirm volume and pages on IEEE's page before the bibliography.
- **SP 800-12r1:** the document says "eight major elements," not "eight core principles."
- **Page numbers in the notes are printed page numbers,** not PDF page numbers.
