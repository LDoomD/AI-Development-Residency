# Possible market segments (FigJam template section)

Written 2026-11-01. Template text: "Market segments are smaller groups of people within a big market who have similar interests, needs, or characteristics... The market can include for-profit or nonprofit organizations trying to sustain their business and solving the same problems. Define the possible segments that the organization fits into."

The "organization" here is a product for family caregivers who coordinate care for an aging or seriously ill relative. Segments are listed by the problem they address, not by a product to build. This is a list of possible segments. It is not a ranking, and no segment has been chosen.

Evidence: **Researched** means searched and cross-referenced on 2026-11-01 (details, sources and limits in `docs/market-research-prompt2-results.md`). **Earlier file** means taken from `docs/market-research-segments.md`, not re-verified in this pass.

## Limits

- Segments 1 to 10 were researched. Segments 11 to 14 come only from the earlier file, with sources I haven't re-checked.
- Sector (for-profit or nonprofit) is verified only where noted. For most rows I did not look it up.
- Two segments I found have no market size of their own: 4 (referral alone) and 8 (patient-side notes).
- Not covered: group texts, spreadsheets and WhatsApp, which caregivers use as workarounds today. They are indirect competition, not a segment of organizations.
- This list does not say which segment is best. That is the market opportunity step.
