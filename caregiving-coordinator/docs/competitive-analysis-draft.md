## Draft questions to answer about each competitor (problems only, no solution framing)

From the template's 11 column titles:
1. Is it a direct or indirect competitor, and in which market segment?
2. What is its website?
3. What are its main features and capabilities (as the company states them)?
4. How does it differentiate itself (the company's own words)?
5. What does it do well (from review or forum quotes, with the review id)?
6. What does it not do well (from review or forum quotes, with the review id)?
7. What do customers say about it (exact quotes, with source)?
8. What market gaps has it NOT filled (the problems in our evidence it does not address)?
9. What is its unique value proposition (the company's own claim, quoted from its saved page)?

Added for this project:
10. Which candidate problem does it relate to (1 scattered information, 2 medication list, 3 what the doctor said, 4 access to accounts, 8 monitoring and safety)?
11. Who is the customer and who pays (family, employer, institution)?
12. What does it cost, as shown on its own site or store listing?
13. Can more than one family member see the same information? What does the company say, and what do reviewers say?

Evidence rules: every cell shows its source (link, saved file in this repo, date saved). Company claims are labelled as company claims. "What they do well / not well" use only review or forum quotes with their ids. Anything not found is written as NOT FOUND.
