WHAT CHANGED, IN SHORT
AUDIENCES: still two. Audience 1 (adult child caring for a parent) is backed more strongly: 65% of Reddit caregiver posts are about a parent. Audience 2 (spouse or partner) has only {n2} Reddit posts, so its changes are a signal, not proof.

AUDIENCE 1
• Motivations: the top two are the same: keeping the parent safe and comfortable first, avoiding blame or guilt second. Love, loyalty and promises moves up from #4 to #3. New: honoring the parent's wishes and making the time left count (5), and relieving the other parent who is the main caregiver (4).
• Situational goals: the order changed. "Understand what is happening and what to do" moves from #4 to #1 (71 of {c1} posts). Money and legal affairs stays #3 but is close behind (46). Finding a place drops from #2 to #5. New: getting the parent to accept care and look after their own health (16), handling the parent's behavior (7), and technology for the parent (7).
• Life goals: the order flips. "Their own health and sanity" moves from #3 to #1 (72 posts), and "time and breaks" drops to #3. New: protecting their own marriage, children and family (9).
• Use cases: all 9 hold. Two candidates are open for the owner to add: the parent refuses help (29 main, 63 mentioned), and getting into a parent's accounts (added 2026-11-03). Money, choosing a place, doctors and safety come up more often on Reddit.

AUDIENCE 2 (small sample)
• Motivations: love, loyalty and promises moves to #1 (15 of {n2}).
• Situational goals: understanding what is happening and what to do shows up first (9 of {n2}). It isn't on this audience's board yet.
• Life goals: same order, and their own health and sanity is still first. New and large: companionship, intimacy and someone who understands (11 of {n2}).
• Use cases: exhaustion is still top. Dementia behavior is much lower on Reddit, because the forum spouses came mostly from a dementia community.
