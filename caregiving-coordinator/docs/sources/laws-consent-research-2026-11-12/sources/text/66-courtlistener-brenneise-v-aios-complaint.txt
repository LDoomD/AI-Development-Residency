8          1.    This class action lawsuit addresses Defendants’ improper, unauthorized,
9    and illegal disclosure of users’ personally identifiable information (“PII”) and
10   protected health information (“PHI”) (collectively referred to as “Private
11   Information”) to third-party advertising platforms and data analytics companies
12   without those users’ knowledge or consent.
13         2.    Defendant Aios, Inc., d/b/a Fella Health and d/b/a Delilah (“Aios”) owns,
14   maintains, and controls two websites: fellahealth.com and joindelilah.com, which are
15   substantially identical websites targeted at men and women, respectively, interested
16   in weight loss (collectively, the “Websites”).
17         3.    Defendant Fella Medical Group, P.A. (“Fella Medical”) is a Florida
18   professional corporation that provides medical services to users of the Websites,
19   including prescribing GLP-1 medications. Through the Websites and Fella Medical’s
20   telehealth platform, users solicit approval of GLP-1 regimens and access to GLP-1
21   products.
22         4.    Given their solicitation, acquisition and retention of sensitive medical
23   data and provision of healthcare services, Defendants have an obligation to protect
24   the privacy and confidentiality of their user’s Private Information in their possession.
25         5.    This obligation is not merely ethical—it is mandated by federal and state
26   law, including the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996
27   (“HIPAA“).
