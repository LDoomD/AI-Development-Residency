## The two biggest problems

1. **"Family or friends won't help" (93) was never a reader code.** It is not in the reader label list (`verify_codes.py`, `BAR`). All 93 come from a keyword rule in `count_results.py` (`OTHER`) applied to free-text "other:" barriers. The rule matches `son `, `husband`, `spouse`, `no help` and similar. That catches text like "no in-person group found" ("per**son** "), "not possible, reason not given" ("rea**son** "), "groups geared to older spouse caregivers", "facility declined combative husband" and "mother-in-law refuses outside help". About 17 of the 93 are clearly not "family won't help", leaving about 76. That moves it from 3rd to 4th, behind eligibility rules (86). In Audience 2, about 5 of 12 are the cared-for husband or spouse, not family refusing to help. So "family won't help leads Audience 2's barriers" does not hold.
2. **Several provider counts use exact-string matching** and miss variants. With variants counted: VA 38 (stated 28), Medicaid and waivers about 32 (stated "about 16"), Area Agency on Aging 14 (stated 10), hospice 24 (stated 20), Alzheimer's Association 9 (stated 6). Medicaid would rank 2nd, not 4th.
