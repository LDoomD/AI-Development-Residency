##### [26] id=agingcare-499812-COMMENT6 name=Care.com src=agingcare len=668
There's no such thing as 30 minutes of care. Certainly not from any homecare agency. I own a homecare agency and we have a minimum of two hours shifts at least twice a week for a new client to even be considered.

You may find a private caregiver who will come for a half hour, but you'll be paying them for a full hour. No one is going to come for half an hour. It's not worth the gas they'd use in the car to get to you.

Put up a profile on care.com and offer a flat rate for what you want done. Let it be known that this is all you want and that the person will not be working hourly. If the job takes them 20 minutes to get done or two hours, the pay is the same.
