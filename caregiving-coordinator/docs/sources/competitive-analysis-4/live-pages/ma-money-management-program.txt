Money Management Program

Helping adults aged 60 and older who have difficulty managing their personal finances.

About

Powered by the Executive Office of Aging & Independence and local Aging Services Access Points (ASAPs), the Money Management Program is run by program staff and volunteers who assist older adults who are having difficulty managing their personal finances. Support may include help creating budgets and paying bills on time, as well as providing information on how to communicate with creditors. The goal is to help people manage their everyday financial affairs and stay as independent as possible in their communities.

Eligibility & Cost

The Massachusetts Money Management Program is a free program for income eligible individuals aged 60 and older.

Contact

Connect with your local Money Management Program (https://money-management-program.mass-aging-disability.org/)
