# Agile Alliance: INVEST (extract)

Source: https://agilealliance.org/glossary/invest/ (read 2026-11-06 with Firecrawl; page last modified 2024-08-15). Extract of the definition and origins only.

- INVEST is "a widely accepted set of criteria, or checklist, to assess the quality of a user story. If the story fails to meet one of these criteria, the team may want to reword it."
- Independent (of all others); Negotiable (not a specific contract for features); Valuable (or vertical); Estimable (to a good approximation); Small (so as to fit within an iteration); Testable (in principle, even if there isn't a test for it yet).
- Origin: 2003, Bill Wake's article. Also recommended in Mike Cohn's "User Stories Applied" (2004), chapter 2.
