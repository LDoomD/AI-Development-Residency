# Bill Wake: INVEST in Good Stories, and SMART Tasks (extract)

Source: https://xp123.com/invest-in-good-stories-and-smart-tasks/ (read 2026-11-06 with Firecrawl; the author says he wrote it in April and August 2003). Short extracts and paraphrase.

- **Independent:** stories should not overlap and should be schedulable in any order. The author adds that this can't always be achieved.
- **Negotiable:** "not an explicit contract for features"; a good story captures the essence, not the details.
- **Valuable:** valuable to the customer. When splitting, slice vertically through the layers (like a multi-layer cake), because a full database layer has little value without a presentation layer.
- **Estimable:** just enough to rank and schedule. If unknowns block an estimate, split off a time-boxed "spike" to learn enough, and the rest implements the feature.
- **Small:** stories typically represent "at most a few person-weeks worth of work"; some teams limit them to a few person-days. Smaller stories get more accurate estimates.
- **Testable:** writing a story is an implicit promise: "I understand what I want well enough that I could write a test for it." If nobody can say how to test it, the story may be unclear or not valuable.
- **SMART tasks** (for the technical tasks under a story): Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-boxed.
