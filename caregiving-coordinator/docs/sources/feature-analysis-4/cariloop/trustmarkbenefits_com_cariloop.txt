 Expand Your Care Solutions 
 Trustmark is an industry leader in helping protect employees from the financial challenges of long-term care. Now, we're taking the next step by offering employees access to tools, resources, and coaching for working caregivers provided by Cariloop ® . This benefit can be used when caring for a loved one or when navigating their long-term care journey.
The result is a more comprehensive care solution that combines life insurance, benefits for care, and guidance for families experiencing the challenge of care.
 Expanding the Scope of Care
 One of the things that makes Trustmark’s life and care solutions so valuable is that it offers two-in-one protection. Now, we can offer a third way to protect employees:
 Life insurance 
To help protect the future for our policyholders' families
 Care benefits 
To help offset the growing costs of care
 Care support 
To help guide employees through managing care today and tomorrow
 Industry-leading coverage 
With expanded life and care protection
 What Is Cariloop?
Cariloop is a caregiver support platform that provides tools, resources, and guidance to help employees manage their caregiving responsibilities. It is available as an added-value benefit with Trustmark life and care solutions. Access to Cariloop includes*:
 Digital access for adult/elder care services
 1 month of coaching per calendar year from a dedicated licensed coach
 Discounted Cariloop rates are available for Trustmark customers who would like to offer Cariloop for all employees
 Why Caregiver Support Matters
 Caring for a loved one is increasingly common – and complex – impacting both employees and employers.
 The result? Increased stress, reduced productivity, and higher turnover.
 The solution? Offering benefits that support employees through these challenging times.
