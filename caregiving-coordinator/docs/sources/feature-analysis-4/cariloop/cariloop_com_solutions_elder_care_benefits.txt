 Why Elder Care Support Matters to Your Business
 With one in five employees caring for an aging loved one, working caregivers need caregiving support to stay productive at work. 
 What the data shows:
 An estimated 37.1 million Americans provide unpaid elder care. 
 Caregivers spend an average of 20 hours a week or more providing care. 
 Nearly 1 in 4 employed caregivers report absenteeism due to care responsibilities. 
 What this means for your business:
 Last-minute absences when caregiving emergencies arise 
 Burnout that affects energy and performance 
 Losing experienced, mid-career employees 
 Constant stress that impacts engagement and well-being
