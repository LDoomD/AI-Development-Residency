S1. Riegel et al. 2024, Circulation: Heart Failure. "Health Coaching Improves Outcomes of Informal Caregivers of Adults With Chronic Heart Failure: A Randomized Controlled Trial." PMID 38904103, DOI 10.1161/CIRCHEARTFAILURE.123.011475. Abstract excerpt: "We conducted a randomized controlled trial testing the efficacy of a virtual health coaching…" (abstract cut off in search result; result direction not read here).
S2. Chenoweth et al. 2016, BMC Health Services Research. "Coaching in self-efficacy improves care responses, health and well-being in dementia carers: a pre/post-test/follow-up study." PMID 27146060. Abstract: "Participants receiving coaching reported non-significant improvements in most areas of self-efficacy for caring" and "Group coaching had greater success in helping carers to achieve their goals".  (n = 16 individual coaching, 32 group, 43 usual support; authors say "need to be interpreted cautiously".)
S3. Tremont et al. 2015, Alzheimer's & Dementia. "Psychosocial telephone intervention for dementia caregivers: A randomized, controlled trial." PMID 25074341. Abstract: "significantly improved caregiver depressive symptoms (P = .003; 27% net improvement)".
S4. Han et al. 2020, JAMDA. COMPASS national family caregiver support program (Korea), RCT. PMID 32620359. Abstract: "significant improvements in burden ... depression ... and health risk behaviors ... for the experimental group".
S5. Terracciano et al. 2020, JAMDA. Powerful Tools for Caregivers RCT. PMID 31866419. Abstract: "PTC reduced caregiver burden (d = -0.48) and depressive symptoms (d = -0.53)".
S6. Gassoumis et al. 2024, J Am Geriatrics Society. COACH ("Comprehensive Older Adult and Caregiver Help"). PMID 37791406. Title states it "prevents elder mistreatment". Abstract excerpt read: "The primary outcome was caregiver-reported EM; additional proximal outcomes were caregiver burden, quality-of-life, anxiety, and depression."
S7. Griffin et al. 2024, J Pain and Symptom Management. Transitional palliative care for family caregivers, RCT. PMID 39111586. Abstract: "No differences in caregiver burden were observed."  (a null result; shows not every program works)
S8. Jutkowitz et al. 2026, JAMDA 27(7). "Transforming Caregiving Research Into Practice: Evaluation of the CareWell Program That Pairs Coaches With an Evidence-Based Web Application." DOI 10.1016/j.jamda.2026.106186. URL https://www.jamda.com/article/S1525-8610(26)00076-9/abstract . Abstract: "Sixteen (62%) of 26 caregivers completed pre and post surveys." and "Satisfaction with the program was high, with an average score of 4.7 of 5." Authors work at Plans4Care (the program owner); small, no control group.
S9. Mooney et al. 2024, Cancer. Automated remote monitoring and coaching, hospice cancer caregivers, multisite RCT. PMID 38009953. (Abstract excerpt read mentions outcomes "Caregiver Burden at weeks 1 and 8"; direction not read.)
S10. Tsoi et al. 2026, JMIR protocol. e-Coaching for family carers, RCT protocol, results not out. (Protocol only; not an outcome.)
