Asked Jan 2024 by "strugglinson" (adult child; stubborn father in assisted living). 10 answers. Answers copied verbatim (trimmed with [...]); id = date/time stamp on page.
[H1, 01/30/2024 15:57] I partnered with a Geriatric Care Manager for my mother [...] The Geriatric Care Manager was GREAT! He had been in the business for over 20 years, was a retired RN, so, he not only went to medical appts with her (after she signed off on the consent form) and relayed all the appt info to me; he was on call 24/7 if we were out of town and would handle any and all crisis and problems. It was SUCH a relief and help. BUT, and here is the BUT, he was expensive - even back in 2018, the hourly charge was $175/hr [...] This could add up quickly to over $700 for one 'visit'. Then covid happened and I had to terminate the relationship because I just couldn't afford it any more.
[H2, 01/23/2024 11:59] I live on the opposite coast from my brother, which I know is unusual. I am his fiduciary, DPOA, Trustee, you name it [...] I hired a GCM 2 years ago, and she has been invaluable in visiting him, helping me set up an Alexa device for drop-in video visits [...] She will also go through his mail with him, (then forwards it to me) has helped with many tedious details. I couldn't manage without her help. She charges $155/hour which typically amounts to $1000-2000/mo depending on how many doctor visits etc.
[H3, 01/23/2024 10:07] I have worked with a geriatric care manager for the last three years. First with my sister who has early stage dementia, and now with my husband who has middle to late stage dementia. She charges $125/hour, and the average cost is about $600/month, and it's worth it; she is very helpful.
[H4, 01/18/2024 23:02] My husband's family hired one when MILs finances were getting low and they wanted to get her in a ood Medicaid facility before applying. / A neighbor used one to help her sister find a facility that could accept her with morbid obesity with a hoyer lift that could support her weight. [...] moving sis across the the border in another state yet much closer to home had more choices.  (second-hand: in-laws and a neighbor; NOT counted)
[H5, 01/17/2024 08:11] I hired a GCM six months ago to support me and my 88-yr-old dad through/following another hospitalization. [...] The GCM has also been a helping sounding board for me, especially when additional hospitalizations have come and decisions about rehab stays, etc. have needed to be made. She and her agency have relationships with care providers across our area and have seen a lot of different situations, so I value their perspective. [...] Dad can be quite difficult, non-compliant and argumentative at times. [...] I've been worried that asking the GCM to take on more, and be that buffer, would just end up making it more difficult
