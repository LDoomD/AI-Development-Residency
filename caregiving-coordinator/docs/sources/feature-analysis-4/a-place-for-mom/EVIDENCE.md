## Advisor help: ongoing or until move-in?
Sources disagree (see F8 rows). APFM is paid when a family moves in ("A Place for Mom is paid by our senior living network communities when a family we refer chooses to move into a community," about page). Real users: BBB and Reddit report nonstop sales calls and, in one case, "when you actually need their help will not respond" (Reddit r/AgingParents, p1d55k9e, in feature-analysis-4/a-place-for-mom/reddit_posts_mentioning_a_place_for_mom_2026-11-06.txt). One BBB 5-star says the advisor gave advice on coping with a spouse's move. I did not find any page promising coaching after move-in.

## Real-user reports
- BBB: A+ rating, 109 complaints closed in 3 years, 272 reviews at 3.95 stars (competitors-4/review-sites/bbb_2026-11-04.txt).
- Reddit (saved archive, 33 posts mention APFM, file above): spam calls and email for years, "commission based." One thread says a US Senate probe was announced (p1dj429j).
- Trustpilot and Consumer Affairs: Trustpilot disallows crawlers; Consumer Affairs not fetched.
