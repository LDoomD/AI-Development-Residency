 Program of Comprehensive Assistance for Family Caregivers (PCAFC)
 PCAFC - Legal and Financial Planning Services 
 Secure Your Future, Honor Their Care 
 Caring for a Veteran can come with complex legal and financial planning questions. 
 The Program of Comprehensive Assistance for Family Caregivers (PCAFC) includes legal and financial planning services to support the unique needs of injured Veterans and their Primary Family Caregivers. 
 Services available Include: 
    • One-on-one support from a certified concierge. 
    • Personalized legal and financial planning guidance. 
    • Referrals to trusted attorneys. 
    • Educational tools tailored to you. 
    • 24/7 phone center at 1-833-422-7382. 
 You may benefit if you: 
    • Aren’t sure where to start with legal or financial planning concerns
   • Want help managing finances
   • Need guidance finding the right legal resources 
 How it works: 
 1. Connect with a concierge 
2. Receive personal support and answers 
3. Utilize resources as needed 
 Examples of covered services at no cost to you. 
 LEGAL SERVICES 
 FINANICIAL PLANNING SERVICES 
 Simple will 
 Household budgeting 
 Advance directives
 Debt and credit management
 Power of attorney 
 Retirement planning/education
 Education on items such as advanced directives, simple wills, estate planning, guardianship
 College expense planning/student loan management review
 90-day financial coaching
 Insurance review/education 
 Ready to take the next steps? 
 Find your local CSP Team or call the 24/7 Legal and Financial Planning services call center anytime at 1-833-422-7382 . The Caregiver Support Program is here to support you as you navigate these challenging topics.
