 Resources for Enhancing All Caregivers Health (REACH) VA Program 
Caregiving can be challenging.  The REACH VA (PDF )  program provides support for caregivers:
 OVERVIEW 
Caregivers will develop skills in problem solving, positive thinking, and stress management. The REACH VA Program has been shown to decrease caregiver depression, burden, and frustrations, and reduce the number of challenging problems reported. REACH VA sessions are customized for each caregiver. The material is tailored to caregivers based on assessments of where they need the most help.
 Support and Services 
 One-on-One Coaching 
Caregivers are paired with a trained and certified REACH VA Program Coach, who provides individualized guidance. Each caregiver usually receives four sessions over 2-3 months, with sessions extendable as needed. Sessions can be conducted face-to-face, via telephone, or through telehealth video conferencing. 
 Telephone Support Groups 
Optional six (or more) session group calls teach stress management, positive thinking, and problem-solving techniques. Sessions provide peer support and allow caregivers to learn strategies applicable to their own caregiving challenges.
 Caregiver Notebook 
Each caregiver receives a notebook containing strategies and information to help manage stress, mood, and problem-solving tailored to specific conditions of their care recipient
 Skill-Building 
REACH VA teaches cognitive reframing, positive thinking, and practical techniques for caring for Veterans while maintaining caregiver well-being
 Customized Approach 
Sessions are personalized based on the specific needs and challenges of each caregiver, with assessments guiding the focus of coaching.
 Benefits: 
 Participation in REACH VA has shown to:
   • Decrease caregiver burden, depression, and frustration.
   • Improve coping skills and caregiver emotional well-being.
   • Reduce the number of challenging problems in Veteran care.
   • Shorten caregiving “on-duty” hours, allowing better self-care for caregivers
 Eligibility: 
 Caregivers must be enrolled in VA’s Caregiver Support Program and provide care for a Veteran.
The program targets caregivers of Veterans with dementia, SCI/D, MS, ALS, PTSD, or post-9/11 spouses, but may be available to any caregiver enrolled in the VA system. 
REACH VA provides structured, evidence-based support that empowers caregivers to effectively manage their duties while maintaining their own health and well-being. The program offers flexible delivery options to accommodate individual schedules and caregiver needs.
