## Differs from Figma
- Figma F4 'No: nothing works unless the veteran agrees': half right. The veteran must agree, but VA also gives tips for a dementia patient resisting bathing and medication. LIMITED.
- Figma F5 'Partly: up to 2 backup caregivers': only naming backups in the 70% program; no tasks or tracking. VERIFY.
- Figma F6 'Partly: legal and financial planning': it is planning and referrals, not account access. VERIFY, leans NO.
- Figma 'Strict rules: 70%': true for the comprehensive program only. The general program (PGCSS) needs no rating, no application, and is free.
- The earlier saved VA page www_caregiver_va_gov_support_support_services_asp is a 404 error page, not content.
- F9 behavior: found and saved the VA dementia behavior page (caregiver.va.gov Tips_by_Diagnosis/Dementia.asp).
