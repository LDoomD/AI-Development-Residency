 G 

 GrammyM Oct 2016 

 Took my husband (since he did not know where he was going) to adult day care once. He liked it, but would not go back saying he likes staying at home. The Day Center was wonderful. Greeted with smiles, acceptance and hospitality. Cost $70 a day from 7am to 5pm. Extremely well organized. Included lunch, in-between snacks, activity games, light exercise programs, posted menu's and television and board games. Also, has an enclosed outdoor patio with lots of flowers, shrubs and a garden. Guests can sit, read, enjoy the sun in peace and quiet. Love it. But, hubby does not like to go anywhere and even with strong insistence from his doctors to give me a break, nope, nothing...he will not budge. I can only evaluate the adult day center near me---it is superb and very affordable. If I had dementia or Alzheimer's, I would love spending time there! I would like to point out before closing, the day care center is ran more efficiently than the $3,000 to $5,000 a month assisted living homes I visited! Yes, I would take a loved one to day care in a heartbeat!
 10/15/2016 11:30:14

 N 

 Nojoy3 Aug 2015 

 Started Mom in an Adult Day Center in June. The cost is $50.00 for 4 hours and she goes once a week every Tuesday. It's been very positive. She is enjoying it, although she was reluctant to go at first. The staff are great and it allows her more socialization. It has allowed me time to get things done ie shopping, house cleaning etc without having to take Mom. It's amazing how much I can get done when on my own. Now that I've caught up on some of the need to do tasks I find myself spending those four hours just relaxing and it's wonderful.
I also wish we would have started the Adult Day Center sooner.

 Oh, my gosh. Helpful? More like a Godsend.

Both mom AND I enjoyed it immensely. Wish we'd started doing it sooner.
