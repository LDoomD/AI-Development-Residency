 Millions of People Feel Just Like You
 With more than 5 million people living with Alzheimer’s in the US, there are millions of people caring for a loved one, who also feel stressed, tired, and overwhelmed. They are looking for a way to provide the best care possible and keep their loved one safe, while still making time to care for themselves.
 The Caring Village Mobile App and Dashboard Can Help You:
 Get the Help You Need Quickly
 Create a trusted care team and use the calendar and to do lists, so others can help you take your loved one to appointments, run errands, or provide companion care. 
 Ensure Your Loved One Is Safe
 Create a daily care plan and upload documents with your safety tips for others to access when filling in for you, so you can ensure your loved one stays out of harms way.
 Worry Less
 Stay engaged and in contact at all times with those watching your loved one by using the Wellness Journal, instant notifications, and secure messaging.
 Take a Much Needed Break for Yourself
 Ask others to volunteer their time to help you watch your loved one, so you can find time to recharge your batteries.

 See how Mary uses Caring Village to get the support she needs to make sure her dad is taken care of
 Mary gets the respite care she needs from her immediate family, so she can take care of herself – whether that is exercising, taking a nap, or spending time with her children.
 Mary tracks all of Dad’s medications to ensure he takes his meds at the right time. She also has easy access to the list of meds when she takes him to the doctor.
 Mary keeps everyone updated on Dad’s mood and demeanor- which can change on a daily basis. Secure in-app messaging makes it easy for her to correspond with her care team.
