Caring Village: Simplify Caregiving, Support Your Loved Ones
Caring Village is the all-in-one caregiving app designed to help families organize care, stay connected, and support each other with confidence. Whether you’re caring for an elderly parent, a spouse, or a loved one with health challenges, our platform combines compassion with smart technology to make caregiving easier and less stressful.
Why Caring Village?
Caregiving can be overwhelming — multiple schedules, medications, documents, and constant communication. Caring Village brings it all together in one secure, family-focused platform. With advanced tools built by and for caregivers, you’ll save time, reduce stress, and spend more energy on what matters most: your loved one.
Key Features
✔ Julia – Your AI Caregiving Assistant
Meet Julia, your AI-powered virtual caregiving assistant. Available anytime, Julia provides advice, answers questions, and helps families make informed decisions along the caregiving journey.
✔ Advanced Medication Management
Never miss a dose. Set detailed medication reminders, track adherence, and share updates with family members.
✔ Centralized Shared Calendar
Keep everyone aligned with a shared care calendar. Sync with external calendars to avoid missed appointments.
✔ Printable Care Calendar
Easily export and print calendars for offline use or to share with doctors and family members.
✔ To-Do Task Listings
Create and assign tasks. Family members can “volunteer” for responsibilities, ensuring caregiving is shared and supported.
✔ Expert-Curated Checklists
Access checklists created by caregiving experts to help guide you through complex situations — from daily routines to emergency planning.
✔ Customizable Care Plans with Reminders
Design care plans tailored to your loved one’s unique needs, with flexible reminders to stay on track.
✔ Wellness Journal & Social Sharing
Track moods, symptoms, and milestones. Share photos and updates in the Wellness Journal, where family members can like, comment, and stay emotionally connected.
✔ Wellness Journal Export
Easily export entries to share insights with healthcare providers and specialists.
✔ Customizable Document Folders
Store and organize medical records, insurance forms, and legal documents in one secure hub.
✔ Expanded Storage Capacity
Enjoy more space for files, updates, and records as your caregiving needs grow.
Built for Families
Caring Village is more than an app — it’s a caregiving ecosystem. Whether your family lives in the same home or across the country, you’ll have the tools to stay connected, divide responsibilities, and share support.
Who Is Caring Village For?
Family caregivers supporting aging parents
Spouses and loved ones managing chronic conditions
Families coordinating across multiple households
Anyone who wants peace of mind while organizing care
Why Families Trust Caring Village
Compassionate design: Built from real caregiving experiences
Tech-forward tools: From AI-powered support to smart scheduling
Peace of mind: Spend less time juggling tasks and more time caring
Join thousands of families transforming how they manage caregiving.
