 FAQ
 Questions Agencies Ask Before They Start
 Anything else about your agency’s setup goes straight to the Caring Village team.
 Ask the Team 
 Does Caring Village replace our scheduling or billing software?It runs alongside it. Keep scheduling, EVV, billing and payroll in the software you already use.
Caring Village is where each client’s family, your caregivers and outside providers stay in sync.
They share the wellness journal, medications, calendar, care plans, messages and documents.
 What counts as a village?One village is one client and their circle of care.
Community covers up to 10 villages, Network up to 25 and Hub up to 50.
If you serve more than 50 clients, the team will size a plan for you.
 Who can see a client’s information?Only the members of that client’s village. Each person gets their own access.
Information shared in a village is visible only to its members.
Caring Village protects data with bank-level 256-bit encryption.
 Do family members and caregivers pay to join?No. Joining a village as a member is free, and members are included in your plan.
Community allows up to 50 members per village, and Network and Hub allow up to 100.
They can use the iPhone or Android app, or the web.
 How is a Professional plan billed?Annually. Community is $499.99 a year, Network is $999.99 and Hub is $1,499.99.
Every plan includes every premium feature, so tiers differ by capacity only.
 How long does setup take?Days, not quarters. In week one you stand up a village per client with the care plan, medication list and key documents.
Then you invite the circle of care, and caregivers start logging notes and meds from the field.
There is no data migration or IT project.
 Can Caring Village sync with our calendars?Yes. Caring Village currently connects with Google Calendar only, not Outlook or Apple calendars.
Every plan also includes a printable care calendar.
 How do we get started?Create a free account, then choose Community, Network or Hub in My Subscription.
For a custom plan or a walkthrough, talk with the Caring Village team.
 Can we export our records?Yes. You can export Medication Logs, Care Plan Reports, the Wellness Journal and Calendar.
Documents you upload stay available to download from each client’s village.
What help do we get setting up?The Caring Village team helps you size your plan and answers setup questions directly.
Families and caregivers join from an email invite, and joining a village is free for them.
