Within Caring Village, you can control access to sensitive information by giving members the appropriate roles.
Administrators can perform any action inside the village.
Inner Circle members can view and update all areas of the village, but can't change village settings or edit member roles.
Friends can help provide daily updates and take on events and to dos, but they can't see any of your private checklist information.
Members who are Revoked are unable to even view your village in their list.
Role table: Administrator, Inner Circle: view and update checklists, events and to dos, wellness journal, medication lists, documents. Friend: events and to dos, wellness journal only.
