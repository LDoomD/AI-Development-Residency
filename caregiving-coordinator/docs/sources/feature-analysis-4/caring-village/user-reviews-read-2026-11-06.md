## a-1387 | 4 stars | 2020-09-01
I am a geriatric care manager and I discovered this app when I was trying to juggle communication among 30+ family members for one of my clients. I since have started several more “villages” for other clients and use most of the available options. Overall I love this app. I have the following observations to share in hopes it helps people make a decision about using this and also in hopes the developers are reading reviews Pros:  -ability to care plan and have staff document daily in the wellness journal to monitor compliance and communicate with family.  -ability to message multiple people at once -family and client ability to remain updated on status and keep all involved in the loop consistently  -family ability to ask questions, send messages, add items to the calendar, etc. it promotes transparency among family, client and care team -ability to use calendar to schedule staff and appointments. Also able to assign staff to tasks and ask for volunteer from family at times there is no staff in the home Cons: -friends can see most of what the inner circle sees. This poses a privacy issue for those not given authorization to see medical and clinical details. I have had to minimize information shared and can’t fully use the app in one of my cases -inability to archive closed cases for future reference. They can only be deleted -the care plan is only good for one day then disappears. From an agency perspective this limits our ability to do quality assurance and track trends -notifications to email only link to the site but not the actual action it is notifying about so it takes extra time to locate what has been added -inability to retract a message or edit -support is quick to respond and helpful. However when I asked to whom I could direct my recommendations to I got no response. I’m hoping this review may get into the proper hands to address some of these suggestions for improvements  Despite the “cons”,  have adopted used of this app for all of my clients receiving in-home care and feel it meets most of our needs.
