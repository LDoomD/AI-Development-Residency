 Questions Families Ask Before They Start
 Straight answers to what caregivers weigh up before bringing their family onto a new app.
 Still deciding? Start with the Free plan. It takes a few minutes and no credit card.
 Try Caring Village Free No credit card required
 Or ask the team a question
 We already have a family group text. Why switch?
 Group texts are great for quick hellos, but the important details scroll away: which pill changed, who is driving on Tuesday, what the doctor said. In Caring Village each thing has a home. Updates go in the journal, meds in the medication list, visits on the shared calendar, and every task gets a named owner, so anyone can find it later.
“It’s so much better than just texting each other.”
Lakeside Maine, App Store
 Will my family actually use it? Some of us aren’t tech-savvy.
 Invited family members join free, and Caring Village runs on iPhone, Android and any web browser, so nobody needs a new device. Relatives who only want news can simply read the updates, while the people doing the work post and check things off.
“Even my 78-year-old client figured it out, and that says everything.”
Linda H., caregiver and nurse
 Who can see my mom’s health information?
 Only the people you invite into her village. Each person has their own login and a role, so you decide who sees what. Caring Village uses bank-level encryption and encrypted messaging, and your family’s health and caregiving information is never sold or shared with third parties.
 Is it really free? What’s the catch?
 The Free plan is free forever, with no credit card: one village for up to 2 members, a shared calendar, messaging and basic to-dos. Larger families move to Circle (5 members per village) or Village (50), which remove ads and add care plans, calendar sync and document folders. Every price is listed above, and you choose your plan inside the app.
 Does it work with the calendars we already use?
 Yes. On Circle and Village, calendar integration syncs care schedules with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook, so visits and appointments show up where everyone already looks. You can also print a care calendar for the fridge.
 Can paid caregivers and doctors join our village too?
 Yes. Invite aides, nurses, care managers and other professionals alongside family. Paid caregivers can post visit notes and check off medications from their phones, so the family sees what happened without having to call and ask.
“One thing it does is it prevents, or it decreases the amount of times that my husband and I might bother the staff.”
Irene, family caregiver
 Is it only for aging parents?
 It fits any care journey. Families use Caring Village for a parent with dementia, a spouse after a stroke, a child with special needs and a loved one going through cancer treatment. If more than one person is helping, a village keeps everyone on the same page.
“If you want to keep track of ANY care journey for a loved one this is so easy to use.”
Emeaston3, App Store
 What if we get stuck?
 You’ll get help from real people. The Caring Village team answers questions directly, and Julia, the built-in AI assistant, answers caregiving questions any time of day.
“Their customer service sent me a 4-minute custom video addressing me by name with how to solve my problem.”
Jason Rawlins, Google Play
