 Frequently Asked Questions 
 Everything you need to know about our plans.
 Can I try Caring Village before paying?
 Absolutely! Our Free plan gives you full access to basic features with 1 village and up to 2 members. You can upgrade to a premium plan at any time from within the app.
 How do I choose my plan?
 After creating your free account, go to My Subscription in the app. From there you can select and manage your plan. You won't be charged until you choose a paid plan.
 Can I switch plans later?
 Yes! You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time from the My Subscription section in the app.
 What payment methods do you accept?
 We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and digital payment methods through our secure payment processor.
 Is my data secure?
 Yes. Caring Village uses bank-level encryption and strict privacy controls. Your family's health and caregiving information is never sold or shared with third parties.
