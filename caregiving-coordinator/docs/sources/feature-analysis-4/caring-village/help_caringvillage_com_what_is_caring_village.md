Put simply, Caring village makes caregiving for loved ones easier.
We provide a collection of articles, checklists, a marketplace, and a personalized caregiving dashboard. We also offer Android and iOS mobile applications that let you coordinate care inside your dashboard from anywhere.
Our platforms allows you to invite family, friends, and neighbors to join in all of the daily activities you face as a caregiver. This helps relieve some of the burden on you and keeps everyone in the village connected to the person they care about.
