## Differs from Figma
- F6: Figma says 'Yes, if the parent fills it in'. I agree it is LIMITED to prepared parents, and I add three limits Figma lacks: Premium plan needed to add deputies, death must be reported on the web, and there is a waiting period. If the parent did not fill it in, the product shows nothing (NO).
- F5: Figma says 'Partly: relatives see chosen sections'. That is viewing, not tasks. The help center says only advisors get edit access, so I rate NO.
- F8: Figma says 'No: stores papers only, no care advice'. Agrees. Source is Everplans' own disclaimer.
- F1 to F4, F9, F10: Figma says No. I can prove only silence, so VERIFY (leans NO).
- Figma says 'The parent pays'. Also true: advisors and partners (enterprise page) can supply cobranded vaults; the help center has a page for 'an Everplans subscription received through a partner'. So some users pay nothing (LIMITED to clients of partner firms).
