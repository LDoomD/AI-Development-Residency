Digital Legacy Planning & Vault Guides

Evaheld and Everplans both help families prepare important information before illness, incapacity or death makes it harder to find. That is where the similarity ends.

Everplans is primarily a structured digital archive for documents, accounts, policies, preferences and instructions. Evaheld combines estate organisation with online will and legal-document creation, advance care planning, emergency health access, family collaboration and multimedia story preservation.

This comparison also examines My Wonderful Life, Safewill, Trust & Will and Storyworth. They are frequently treated as interchangeable “legacy planning resources” services, but each solves a different part of the problem. The right choice depends on whether you need a legal document, a practical family handover, a care plan, a funeral plan, a memoir or one connected system containing several of those elements.

Quick Answer

There is no honest universal winner because these products do not perform the same job.

Evaheld is the best fit for people who want one connected place for legal documents, estate information, care wishes, emergency details, family stories and controlled sharing.

Everplans is the better fit when the main goal is a structured archive of practical information and instructions for trusted people.

My Wonderful Life is designed primarily for planning a funeral or memorial.

Safewill is a focused online will and estate-service provider for Australia and New Zealand.

Trust & Will is designed around US will-based and trust-based estate plans.

Storyworth is a guided memoir service built around regular prompts and a printed book.

Evaheld has the widest functional scope in this comparison, but breadth is only valuable when the additional functions are relevant. Someone who wants only a physical memoir may prefer Storyworth. Someone in the United States who needs a trust-based estate plan may prefer Trust & Will or a specialist attorney. Someone who wants a will, care plan, emergency information and living family archive in one place is more closely aligned with Evaheld.

How This Comparison Was Assessed

The comparison uses six practical criteria:

Legal-document capability: whether the platform creates wills or related legal documents, rather than only storing completed files.

Practical estate organisation: whether executors and trusted people can find assets, accounts, policies, contacts and current document information.

Health and incapacity planning: whether the platform supports advance care planning, decision-makers and information needed while the person is alive.

Story and memory preservation: whether it supports written stories, photographs, voice, video, family contributions or printed outputs.

Access and delivery controls: whether different people can receive different information now, later or after death.

Geographic and legal fit: whether the product is designed for particular countries or legal systems.

Provider features were checked against the official information published by Everplans, My Wonderful Life, Safewill, Trust & Will, and Storyworth. Features and prices can change, so readers should confirm current eligibility and terms before relying on any comparison.

Can Evaheld Create a Will?

Yes. Evaheld is an online will creator and legal-document creator. It is inaccurate to describe it as only a storage service.

Evaheld’s estate planning and legal-document workspace supports will creation and connects the document to the practical records that an executor may later need: assets, liabilities, insurance, adviser contacts, digital accounts, document locations, trusted people and family instructions.

However, document creation and legal validity are not identical.

A will generally needs to satisfy the law that applies to the person making it. Requirements can include testamentary capacity, intention, correct signing and eligible witnesses. A document produced online does not become legally effective merely because it has been generated or stored. The user must complete the required execution steps.

Evaheld is not a law firm and does not replace personalised legal advice. Professional advice is particularly important for trusts, business succession, overseas assets, blended families, vulnerable beneficiaries, complex tax matters or likely disputes.

The accurate position is:

Evaheld creates wills and other legal documents through its available product pathways.

Users must follow the signing, witnessing and other requirements applying to them.

Legal advice may still be required where the estate or family situation is complex.

Evaheld continues beyond the document by organising the information and access that a will usually does not contain.

That last point is significant. A will can appoint an executor and distribute assets, but it normally does not contain passwords, medical details, household instructions, a pet’s routine, the story behind a sentimental object or a message for a future grandchild. Evaheld’s complete digital legacy vault is designed to keep those distinct forms of information organised without pretending they all have the same legal effect.

Evaheld vs Everplans

Everplans is a structured digital archive. Users work through practical categories such as personal information, financial accounts, insurance, property, online accounts, documents and end-of-life preferences. They can appoint trusted “Deputies” and control access to selected information, including information intended to become available after death.

That is a useful service. Families frequently know that a document or account exists without knowing where it is, whether it is current or who should be contacted. Everplans is built to reduce that administrative confusion.

Evaheld covers much of the same organisational need but extends into areas that Everplans does not position as its primary purpose:

online will and legal-document creation;

advance care planning;

a Dynamic Emergency QR Card;

private and shared family Rooms;

Content Requests for collecting information from relatives or advisers;

guided memoir, biography and legacy-letter creation;

voice, video, photographs and uploaded documents;

immediate, milestone, future-dated and posthumous delivery settings;

Charli, an AI guide that helps users decide what to complete next.

The practical distinction is that Everplans is primarily an archive for information another person may need. Evaheld is a broader planning and participation system. It helps users create information and legal documents, collect contributions, manage care wishes and preserve the person behind the paperwork.

Choose Everplans when a detailed, checklist-led archive and Deputy access are the main requirements. Choose Evaheld when the family also needs legal creation, health planning, emergency access or multimedia legacy functions.

Evaheld vs My Wonderful Life

My Wonderful Life has a focused purpose: helping people plan a funeral or memorial that reflects how they lived. Users can record preferences and inspiration so family members are not forced to make every decision while grieving.

That focus is valuable, but it is narrower than Evaheld’s. A funeral plan does not create a will, organise an estate, record an advance care directive, manage passwords or preserve a continuing family archive.

Evaheld can hold funeral preferences within a wider plan containing legal documents, executor information, care wishes, emergency contacts, family stories and private messages. Access can be separated so a person involved in funeral arrangements does not automatically receive unrelated financial, health or personal material.

Evaheld supports a provider’s clinical or operational system. Its role is the person-controlled family layer: advance care directives, emergency access, wishes, selected records, relationships, stories and information the owner chooses to share.

Choose My Wonderful Life when funeral planning is the defined task. Choose Evaheld when funeral wishes are one component of a broader family, estate and care plan.

Evaheld vs Safewill

Safewill is a focused online will and estate-service provider for Australia and New Zealand. Its will pathway uses a guided process, and its Australian service describes legal-team review and related probate support.

Evaheld also creates wills, but its proposition is wider. The will sits inside an estate-planning system that can include executors, guardianship decisions, assets, liabilities, policies, passwords, digital accounts, document status and trusted access. Its guidance on estate planning mistakes families should avoid reflects the practical reality that a signed will can still be difficult to use if nobody can find the original, identify the estate or understand which records are current.

Safewill may be the more direct option for someone seeking a specialised AU/NZ will process and related legal services. Evaheld may be the better fit for someone who wants to create the will and organise the health, practical and personal information around it in the same platform.

Neither option removes the need for tailored legal advice when the circumstances are complex.

Evaheld vs Trust & Will

Trust & Will is a US estate-planning platform. Its published plans include will-based and trust-based options, with documents such as a last will and testament, living will, power of attorney and HIPAA authorisation available in relevant plans. It also offers membership and attorney-support options.

For a US household whose main objective is creating a revocable living trust and associated transfer documents, Trust & Will has a specialised advantage. Evaheld should not be presented as a substitute for US trust structuring or jurisdiction-specific tax and legal advice.

Evaheld’s advantage is its broader family scope. It connects legal documents and estate organisation to care wishes, emergency information, family collaboration, voice, video, life stories and controlled delivery.

The two platforms can therefore be alternatives or complements. A US household may use Trust & Will or an attorney for a trust-based estate plan and Evaheld for the wider care and legacy system. Another household may find that Evaheld’s will-creation and estate-organisation tools cover its straightforward requirements without adding a separate platform.

Evaheld vs Storyworth

Storyworth is designed around a defined creative outcome. The storyteller receives regular questions, answers them in writing or through available voice options, adds photographs and produces a hardcover memoir.

Evaheld’s family story and legacy vault also supports guided memoirs and printed life stories, but the archive is designed to remain active. Users can create written stories, voice recordings, videos, legacy letters, photographs, recipes, family history and milestone messages. They can invite selected people to contribute inside a Room without exposing the rest of the vault.

Storyworth is the more focused choice when the family specifically wants a prompt-led book project. Evaheld is the more integrated choice when stories need to sit beside legal documents, care wishes, family access and future messages.

An ethical will template and prompts can also help users preserve values and life lessons without confusing an ethical will with a legally operative will. The legal document governs the estate; the personal document carries meaning that an asset schedule cannot.

Health, Care and Emergencies

Health and incapacity planning are major points of difference because a will generally operates after death. It does not ordinarily govern treatment while a person is alive.

Evaheld’s health and care planning system supports advance care planning, care wishes, decision-maker information and selected emergency details. Its Dynamic Emergency QR Card is intended to surface limited critical information—such as allergies, medications, conditions and emergency contacts—without opening the whole vault.

Evaheld can also support users in creating jurisdiction-relevant advance care documents. Whether a particular document is legally effective depends on local law and correct execution. Requirements may involve capacity, prescribed forms, signatures, witnesses, certification or substitute decision-maker appointments. Evaheld should not be described as guaranteeing legal validity in every jurisdiction regardless of what the user does.

The advance directive and living will comparison explains why terminology and legal pathways differ even when the underlying objective is similar.

Everplans can organise health-related information. Trust & Will provides US healthcare documents in relevant estate plans. Neither advertises the same combination of family care context, a dynamic emergency QR pathway and multimedia legacy inside one connected vault. My Wonderful Life, Safewill and Storyworth are not primarily designed for ongoing family care coordination.

For families facing dementia or degenerative illness, the wider combination matters. The work may involve documenting decisions while capacity remains, clarifying authority, recording care wishes, keeping emergency information current and preserving the person’s stories and voice.

Digital Accounts Families Often Overlook

Digital legacy planning extends well beyond email and social media. A useful inventory should identify any service that controls money, property, access, identity, commitments or private information.

Commonly overlooked categories include:

password managers and account-recovery methods;

cloud storage, photo libraries and device backups;

subscription services with recurring payments;

business platforms and domain registrations;

travel, accommodation and marketplace accounts;

home-access systems and active bookings;

productivity, journal and personal-planning tools;

cryptocurrency exchanges and digital wallets;

loyalty points and stored-value accounts.

Personal-planning platforms can contain sensitive material too. A digital legacy plan should explain whether the account should be preserved, exported, transferred or closed, while protecting material that was never intended for family-wide access.

Evaheld’s digital legacy planning checklist helps separate urgent access information from private memories and ordinary account administration. The goal is not to copy every account into one vault. It is to ensure trusted people know what exists, why it matters and what authorised action may eventually be required.

FAQs about how to choose between Evaheld vs Everplans and alternatives

Is Evaheld an online will creator?

Yes. Evaheld offers online will and legal-document creation through its available product pathways. It is not merely a document-storage platform. Users must still complete the signing, witnessing and other execution requirements that apply to them.

Is Evaheld a law firm?

No. Evaheld provides document-creation and planning technology, not personalised legal representation. Creating a document through software does not prevent a user from seeking legal advice when their circumstances require it.

What is the biggest difference between Evaheld and Everplans?

Everplans is primarily a structured archive for practical information and trusted access. Evaheld includes organisation and controlled sharing but adds will creation, advance care planning, emergency QR information, multimedia stories, family Rooms, Content Requests and future delivery.

Can Evaheld create a legally valid advance care directive?

Evaheld can support creation of jurisdiction-relevant advance care documents. Legal effect depends on the applicable law and correct execution, which may include capacity, prescribed wording, signatures, witnesses or certification. Users should follow the requirements displayed for their location and obtain professional advice when uncertain.

Is Evaheld better than Safewill?

Evaheld has a broader product scope. Safewill is more specialised around AU/NZ wills and related estate services. The better fit depends on whether the user wants a dedicated will pathway or an integrated legal, care, emergency and legacy system.

Is Evaheld or Storyworth better for a family memoir?

Storyworth is purpose-built around prompts and a printed memoir. Evaheld also supports guided life stories and printed output, while adding voice, video, family Rooms, future messages and the wider legal and care vault. Choose according to whether the desired outcome is a defined book project or a continuing multimedia family archive.

Final Assessment

Evaheld and Everplans overlap in practical organisation, but they should not be presented as equivalent filing systems.

Everplans is a credible choice for people who want a detailed archive and controlled access for trusted Deputies. Evaheld combines that broader organisational objective with online will creation, estate readiness, advance care planning, emergency health access, family collaboration and story preservation.

My Wonderful Life, Safewill, Trust & Will and Storyworth each have more specialised use cases. Their narrower focus can be an advantage when the person needs only that particular outcome.

Evaheld is most differentiated when a family does not want legal, practical, health and emotional planning scattered across unrelated services. Its three pillars keep those forms of information separate enough to preserve their purpose but connected enough to give loved ones a coherent plan:

Essentials: wills, legal documents, assets, passwords, executor information and trusted access.

Health & Care: advance care planning, decision-makers, care wishes and selected emergency information.

Story & Legacy: written stories, family history, photographs, voice, video, books, letters and future messages.

The current founding offers make that combined system available for A$197 one-off for two separate Premium Lifetime Vaults or A$129 one-off for one Premium Lifetime Vault, subject to the live offer terms.

The right platform is ultimately the one that matches the family’s real task. For one printed memoir, choose a memoir specialist. For a US trust, choose an appropriate US trust service or attorney. For a structured practical archive, Everplans remains a serious option. For a connected system spanning wills, care, emergencies and family legacy, Evaheld offers the most relevant combination of features in this comparison.
