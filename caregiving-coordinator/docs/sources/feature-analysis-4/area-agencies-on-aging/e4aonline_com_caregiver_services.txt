 Support for Family Caregivers in East Arkansas
 Caring for a loved one is a lot to take on. EAAAA's Family Caregiver Support Program offers respite, support groups, training, and resources to help family caregivers in 12 East Arkansas counties.

 Who Qualifies
 EAAAA's Family Caregiver Support Program serves caregivers in these 12 East Arkansas counties: Clay, Craighead, Crittenden, Cross, Greene, Lawrence, Lee, Mississippi, Phillips, Poinsett, Randolph, and St. Francis.
 Our team will help you get connected to services and make sure you have what you need. Call 800-467-3278 or send us a message to get started.
 We Focus on Caregivers Needs
 At East Arkansas Area Agency on Aging, we understand the importance of providing care to a loved one and the toll it can take.  Our Family Caregiver Support Program focuses on the CAREGIVER’S needs.  Through various resources, education and support groups, we foster a bridge of support, and provide a personal relationship to our caregivers which empowers them to cope throughout their Caregiving Journey.

 Support
 Our respite services provide temporary relief for family caregivers, allowing them to take a well-deserved break from their caregiving duties. Our support groups offer a safe and supportive space for seniors and their loved ones to connect with others who are experiencing similar challenges and share their experiences and insights. Support groups meet both in person and virtually to better accommodate your needs and lifestyle.  
 Our ombudsman services help to protect the rights of seniors and ensure that they are receiving the high-quality care they deserve within a long term care facility. Whether you need temporary relief, support, long term care needs or advocacy, our team is here to help.

 Resources
 Our legal assistance services provide seniors and their families with the information and support they need to navigate the legal challenges that often come with aging. Our caregiver resources offer valuable tips, tools, and support to help family caregivers provide the best possible care for their loved ones. And our family mediation services can help families to resolve conflicts and improve communication, promoting greater harmony and understanding within the family. 
 We are here to help seniors and their families face the challenges of aging with confidence and support.
 Education
 At our agency, we believe that knowledge is power. That's why we offer a range of information, education, and training services to help senior caregivers provide the best possible care for their loved ones. Whether you are new to caregiving or have been providing care for years, we have resources to help you. 
 Our educational workshops and seminars cover a wide range of topics, from understanding the aging process and managing chronic conditions, to financial planning and legal issues. Our training programs can help caregivers to develop the skills and confidence they need to provide high-quality care. 
 We are here to support and empower caregivers every step of the way.
