 Programs Overview
 High Country Area Agency on Aging provides resources and support to strengthen caregivers through two avenues: the Family Caregiver Support Program and the Grandparents Raising Grandchildren Program. By supporting families, we help avoid or delay costly institutional care and promote independence at home.

 Who Is a Caregiver?
 A caregiver is anyone who provides ongoing support to a loved one. You may be a caregiver if you:
 Assist with personal care such as bathing, dressing, eating, or mobility.
 Help with shopping, finances, medical appointments, or household tasks.
 Coordinate healthcare or services for a loved one, even from a distance.
 Oversee the care of a loved one living in a facility.
 Eligibility
 Family Caregiver Support Program 
You may qualify for support if you:
 Live in Alleghany, Ashe, Avery, Mitchell, Watauga, Wilkes, or Yancey County.
 Are 18+ and provide unpaid care for:
 An adult age 60+ who needs help with at least two daily activities, such as bathing, dressing, eating, walking, transferring, or toileting.
 Someone living with dementia or severe memory loss (no age limit).
 Grandparents Raising Grandchildren Program 
You may qualify for support if you:
 Live in Alleghany, Ashe, Avery, Mitchell, Watauga, Wilkes, or Yancey County
 Are 55+ and provide unpaid care for:
 A relative child age 18 or younger, or
 An adult age 18–59 with a developmental disability.
 Have the care recipient living with you as their primary caregiver.
 Legal custody/guardianship is preferred but not required (foster families eligible).
 Program Services
 Respite
 Caregiver directed vouchers, based upon availability and eligibility, to supplement Project CARE and the Lifespan Respite Program .
 Support Groups
 Welcoming spaces across the region to share experiences, gain encouragement, and connect with others.
 Case Management & In-Home Assessments
 Home visits, consultations, emergency planning, and safety evaluations.
 Education
 Workshops cover practical caregiving topics, dementia awareness (including Dementia Friends USA training), and resources for caregivers and the public.
 Grandparents Raising Grandchildren
Program-Specific Support
 We recognize that every grandfamily is unique, so our program is flexible to support a wide range of situations. Supports may include:
 Camp or after-school program assistance
 Clothing and shoes
 Counseling or support group referrals
 Educational support (supplies, technology, tutoring)
 Food and formula
 Home safety improvements
 Household items and cleaning supplies
 Self-care items (diapers, toiletries, medication assistance)
 Other needs identified through consultation
