PRESS RELEASEFebruary 15, 2022

15, 2022 – [athenahealth, Inc.](https://www.athenahealth.com/) (“athenahealth” or “the Company”), a leading provider of cloud-based enterprise software solutions for medical groups and health systems nationwide, today announced that affiliates of [Bain Capital](https://www.baincapital.com/) and [Hellman & Friedman](https://hf.com/) have completed their acquisition of athenahealth from [Veritas Capital](https://www.veritascapital.com/) and Evergreen Coast Capital for $17 billion.
