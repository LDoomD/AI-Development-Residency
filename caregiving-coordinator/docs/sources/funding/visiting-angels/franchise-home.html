Your Partner in Franchise Success—Where Family Comes First

Since 1998, Visiting Angels has empowered entrepreneurs to positively impact their communities by providing high-quality care to seniors and individuals with disabilities. With over 600 successful franchises across the U.S. and expanding international opportunities available, our business model provides a strong and reliable foundation for growth.

Franchise owners benefit from comprehensive support, including personalized and group training, annual conferences, and valuable partnership programs. Our robust intranet and weekly updates ensure franchisees have continuous access to the information and resources they need to achieve lasting success.

Everything You Need to Know About
Owning a Visiting Angels Franchise

Owning a Visiting Angels franchise means having the tools, resources, support, and guidance to build a thriving business in your community. Our franchisees achieve average annual revenues of around $2 million, with 35–40% gross profit margins.

The rewards go beyond financial success. You’ll build a respected community presence by helping seniors age comfortably at home. With comprehensive lead generation, marketing support, and ongoing guidance, we’ll position you for business growth you can count on and a legacy that matters.
