SAN FRANCISCO, April 7, 2022 /PRNewswire/ -- [Trustworthy](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=3493882-1&h=830715468&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.trustworthy.com%2F&a=Trustworthy), the Family Operating System® where families can protect, organize and optimize their most important information, announced today that it has secured a US $15M Series A Funding round, led by Chicago-based Valor Siren Ventures.
