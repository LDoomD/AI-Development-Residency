Personalized care and support

Caring for yourself and your family shouldn’t be complicated or frustrating. It should be simple and reassuring. Accolade gets to know YOU and helps you get answers, see a doctor that’s right for you, and access benefits or care support that best fits your needs. So, you have better experiences and better health results. 

 

Depending on your employer benefits, you may have access to virtual primary and mental health care, expert medical opinion, or care navigation services. Each is staffed by Accolade’s expert care team, which includes advocates, nurses, doctors, therapists, and specialists.  We also offer benefits from Trusted Partners that help you get the exact care you need — no matter your condition. From digital physical therapy and diabetes care to fertility and behavioral health, Accolade ensures you get high-quality care from thoroughly vetted providers.

 

Connect virtually with a primary care physician or mental health therapist. Get treatment for urgent or ongoing conditions, mental health, preventative care, and more.

Connect with a leading specialist and get a virtual second medical opinion, for a chronic condition, recent diagnosis or ongoing treatment plan 

Connect with a Care Advocate for benefits navigation, claims support, steerage, and referrals to the right providers
