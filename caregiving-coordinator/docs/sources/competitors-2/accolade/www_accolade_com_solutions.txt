A Care Experience that goes beyond the visit An example of how the WayFinding platform gets people where they need to be on their health and care journey. Ask the AI Care Assistant Kara is on vacation and notices some mild wheezing. She opens her app and asks the AI Care Assistant, “I think my asthma is flaring up and I don’t have my inhaler. What should I do?” Instant guidance The AI Care Assistant helps Kara assess her symptoms, evaluates urgency, remembers that she prefers virtual care, and guides her to the appropriate next step based on her symptoms, history, and preferences. Start a visit immediately Within minutes, Kara completes an intake with specific questions related to her concern and is connected to a board-certified clinician who further evaluates her symptoms. Kara gets back to enjoying life Within hours, Kara has answers, treatment, and relief, without having to find or wait for an office visit or navigate multiple services. Being away from home shouldn’t mean that she doesn’t have access to health and care. Support over time WayFinding remembers Kara’s visit, preferences, and care history so future guidance and recommendations feel more personalized. Clinician care Following evidence-based guidelines, the clinician confirms this is likely an asthma flare-up, reviews her medication and history, and electronically sends a prescription for an inhaler to a nearby pharmacy.
