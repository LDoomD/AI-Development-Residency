Our personal password manager provides the features you need to protect your online life from cybercriminals. How do I share passwords with family members using Keeper? Users on a Keeper Family Plan can share passwords and records with other family members once everyone has created a Keeper account. For families, using Shared Folders is often the easiest way to share and manage access to multiple records at once. You can also share a record one time with someone outside your plan, if needed. To share a record with family members: Open Keeper and go to Options > Sharing. Choose the family member(s) you want to share with from the dropdown list, or enter their email address manually. Select the appropriate permission level, then click Add. To share multiple records using Shared Folders: Create a Shared Folder. Add the records you want to share into the folder. Invite family members and set their permissions (view, edit or share). Any updates to records in the folder will automatically sync for all members. For a one-time share: Use One-Time Share to generate a secure, time-limited link to a specific record that you can send to someone without permanently adding them as a shared user. For more details on sharing and other features, see the Keeper Family Plan guide . How safe are password managers? Password management tools like Keeper provide strong 256-bit AES encryption, serving as a defense against cybercriminals since it makes encrypted data virtually impossible to break. AES encryption is the industry standard currently used at the federal level to protect sensitive data. Keeper is a zero-knowledge , zero-trust security product that ensures users have the highest levels of security and privacy. Our autofill feature, KeeperFill® , can prevent cybercriminals from stealing your login details. How do I protect myself from the dark web? BreachWatch is Keeper’s dark web monitoring solution, designed to help protect users from data breach risk. It continuously scans the dark web for stolen credentials and alerts users if their information has been compromised so they can take immediate steps to secure affected accounts. With more than 20 billion stolen usernames and passwords from public breaches circulating on the dark web and traded among cybercriminals, these credentials are often used to take over accounts through credential stuffing and other common attack methods . That’s why BreachWatch monitoring is an essential layer of protection to keep your online accounts secure. If BreachWatch identifies a weak or compromised password, it’s important to update it right away. Watch this short video to learn how to replace a weak password with a strong one in Keeper.
