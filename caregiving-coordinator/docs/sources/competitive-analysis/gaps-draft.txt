GAPS IN THE MARKET AND IN COMPETITOR OFFERINGS — DRAFT from the evidence in the table (ten rows). The conclusions and the wording are the owner's to decide.

WHAT THE EVIDENCE SHOWS (counts are approximate: one reader, one category per review; only reviews where the reviewer is clearly caring for or helping a relative are counted; the apps were chosen by the team)
• Coordination apps (Caring Village, CaringBridge, Lotsa): 109 reviews, 25 describe a problem, 84 describe something that worked. Most common problems: crashes and bugs 11, access or logins for another person 5, other problems 4.
• Monitoring and safety (Lively, Medical Guardian): 156 reviews, 114 describe a problem, 42 describe something that worked. Most common problems: wrong readings or location 42, crashes and bugs 23, a person in danger and the tool did not help 18, missed or late alerts 10.
• Medication reminders (Medisafe): 30 reviews, 18 describe a problem, 12 describe something that worked. Most common problems: relatives cannot see the same information 4, hard for an older person to use 3.
• Each of the 3 coordination apps states in its own words that more than one person can share the same information (the "Who it is for" column).
• Access to accounts or records: across all caregiving-only reviews, 14 of 192 problem reviews are about getting access or logins for another person. No app reviews were collected for Everplans or Epic MyChart, so this group has company claims but no user feedback.
• Correction: earlier drafts used unfiltered review counts (for example 625 problem reviews, 30 about access); those were too high because most of those reviews were not about caregiving.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW
• Whether any competitor lets a relative get access without the account holder's login (not checked).
• Who would pay, or what people would do.
• Anything about companies we did not include. 21 more rows (companies and workarounds) were researched; they are kept as data in docs/sources/competitive-analysis/competitive-data.json and are not in this table.
