GAPS IN THE MARKET AND IN COMPETITOR OFFERINGS — DRAFT from the evidence in the table. The conclusions and the wording are the owner's to decide.

WHAT THE EVIDENCE SHOWS (counts are approximate: one reader, one category per review; the apps were chosen by the team)
• Coordination apps (Caring Village, CaringBridge, Lotsa, ianacare, Cozi, FamilyWall, TimeTree): 543 reviews read, 150 describe a problem, 334 describe something that worked. Most common problems: crashes and bugs 44, other problems 41, missed or late alerts 14, access or logins for another person 11.
• Monitoring and safety (Lively, Medical Guardian, Aloe Care, Life360, Apple Watch, OMRON connect, Withings): 613 reviews read, 392 describe a problem, 181 describe something that worked. Most common problems: wrong readings or location 137, crashes and bugs 75, a person in danger and the tool did not help 32, other problems 32.
• Medication and visit notes (Medisafe, MyTherapy, Kin): 106 reviews read, 35 describe a problem, 60 describe something that worked. Most common problems: relatives cannot see the same information 8, reminders failing 6, other problems 5, crashes and bugs 3.
• 6 of the 7 coordination apps state in their own words that more than one person can share the same information (the "Who it is for" column); the exception is ianacare, where the saved pages had only customer testimonials on sharing.
• Access to accounts or records: in the whole review sample, 30 of 625 problem reviews are about getting access or logins for another person. No app reviews were collected for Everplans, Trustworthy, Keeper or Epic MyChart, and 1Password has no saved page, so this group has company claims but no user feedback.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW
• Whether any competitor lets a relative get access without the account holder's login (not checked).
• Who would pay, or what people would do.
• Anything about companies we did not include.
