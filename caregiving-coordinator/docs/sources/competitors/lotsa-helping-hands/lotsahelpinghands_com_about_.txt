 About Cancer Care 
 Founded in 1944, Cancer Care is the leading national organization providing free, professional support services and information to help people manage the emotional, practical and financial challenges of cancer. Our comprehensive services include counseling and support groups over the phone, online and in-person, educational workshops, publications and financial and co-payment assistance. All CancerCare services are provided by oncology social workers and world-leading cancer experts. To learn more, visit www.cancercare.org or call 800-813-HOPE (4673).
 About Lotsa Helping Hands
 Lotsa empowers people to organize actionable help for someone in need. We believe that people want to help, but often don't know how. Our online care teams organize groups, volunteers and friends to lend a hand during times of medical crisis, the arrival of a new baby, or when family members need additional support. We think that harnessing the strength of the community is the best way to come to the aid of caregivers and families in need.
 Caregivers benefit from the gifts of much needed help, emotional support, and peace of mind, while volunteers find meaning in giving back to those in need.
