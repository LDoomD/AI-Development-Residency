Get reminded of all your intakes thanks to reliable medication alarms

Reliable reminders for your tablets, pills and other medications as well as measurements, doctor’s appointments or symptom checks.

The MyTherapy app keeps an eye on your medications and therapy for you, so you can focus on other important things. 

Comprehensive documentation and personal notes

MyTherapy automatically documents every intake, whether taken or skipped. You can even add notes to each instance, so you never have to face uncertainty regarding past intakes. 

In combination with your health diary, which MyTherapy keeps up-to-date, you will always carry your personal treatment overview with you and have access to it anytime, anywhere.

Always remain in control over your medication supply

Our medication tracker app is your digital brain extension for keeping on top of your supply. MyTherapy will remind you well in advance when it's time for a refill or to get a new prescription.

Use this feature to easily and automatically avoid ever running out of your medication.

Keep track of your symptoms and measurements

MyTherapy enables you to document all your measurements like blood sugar, blood pressure or weight, among many others. Additionally, you can keep track of your headaches, back pain or other symptoms as well as your mood on a daily basis.

You can also program reminders to schedule these checks, so you can build a consistent picture of the development of your treatment and well-being over time.

Be well prepared for your next doctor’s visit

MyTherapy's comprehensive features mean you can be better prepared than ever for your next doctor's appointment.

By having a detailed overview of your therapy, intakes, symptom development, measurements and mood, your doctor can gain a greater insight into your well-being. This can help inform decisions and lead to your treatment being optimized more efficiently.

Quickly generate a health report and medication plan for your doctor

Our app also features an interface for your doctors. By creating a safe and uncomplicated one-time code, you can share your medication plan, intake diary and more with your doctor at the push of a button.

That’s how digital medication support is supposed to work. Easy, smart and secure. MyTherapy App - your reliable companion for better medication and treatment.

3 things that are just as important to us as they are to you:

Data Privacy

Your health is a private matter. Based on this principle, we will not hand over your personal data to third parties without your consent. We conform to the strictest data privacy standards available and keep data processing to a minimum.

Research

We believe that MyTherapy can improve healthcare overall. We work together with reputable research partners. Your data privacy is not compromised in this process and data cannot be traced back to any specific user.

Free availability

MyTherapy always has been and always will remain free to use. In order to make this possible, we cooperate with partners such as pharmaceutical companies and healthcare institutes to whom medication adherence information is vital. Your personal data will never be handed over to our partners or other third parties we cooperate with.
