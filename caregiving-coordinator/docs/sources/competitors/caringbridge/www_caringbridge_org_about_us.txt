CaringBridge is a nonprofit dedicated to building bridges of care and communication, providing love and support on a health journey. Discover our mission and how we ensure no one faces a health challenge alone.
CaringBridge is a no-cost, 501(c)(3) nonprofit health platform that surrounds family caregivers with support while they care for a loved one on a health journey.
A donor-supported nonprofit founded in 1997, the organization offers tools to share and document a health journey, simplify care coordination, and connect caregivers with a supportive community.
In 1997, our founder, Sona Mehring, had the idea for CaringBridge when she offered to support her good friends, JoAnn Hardegger and Darrin Swanson, keep everyone updated after the premature birth of their daughter, Brighid. 
After making emotionally taxing and time-consuming calls, Sona knew there was a better way. She created a website to keep everyone connected and surround JoAnn and Darrin, with support - and CaringBridge was born.
Since that day, CaringBridge has remained committed to surrounding family caregivers with support while they care for a loved one on a health journey.
From the launch of the very first CaringBridge, we’ve been working toward a single vision: a world where no one goes through a health journey alone. 
In order to turn this vision into reality, we’ve made it our mission to build bridges of care and communication providing love and support on a health journey. 
While patients are always at the center of the heath journey, family caregivers often bear the brunt of sharing updates, financial strain, and coordination management during this overwhelming time. 
Feelings of being overwhelmed, isolated, and lonely are prevalent among family caregivers and the loved ones they support on a health journey. CaringBridge addresses those needs by improving emotional health and social support, helping people come together in support of healing.

CaringBridge users from around the country have shared their stories with us to inspire and encourage others. Despite caregiving's universal impact, many of us do not identify as caregivers. 
For over 25 years, CaringBridge has made it simple and safe to offer or ask for support when it’s needed most. We recognize your vital role and surround you with support at every step of the health journey. 
CaringBridge is an inclusive space that empowers individuals to share their unique journeys, connect with others, and find strength in the collective support received for their functional, social, and emotional needs.
