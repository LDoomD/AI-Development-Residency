How do I create a request?
As the Author or Co-Author of a CaringBridge, you can create Requests to ask for and organize specific types of support from your community.
Under Manage my page, select Create a Request. A pop-up window will appear for you to create your Request.
Select what type of request you'd like to create
Childcare – Ask for help watching the kids
Chores – Get help with cleaning, laundry, or home tasks
Errands – Grocery runs, pharmacy pickups, etc.
Meals – Ask for meal drop-offs or delivery support
Pet Care – Request walks, feedings, or vet visits
Transportation – Coordinate rides to appointments or other destinations
Visiting – Schedule supportive visits at the right time
Other – Ask for any other support you need
Add a Title for your request
Add details for your request. Be sure to include dates, times, and details, so your community knows exactly what's needed.
Tip: One person can pick up each request, so if you need more than one person to help or you have more than one date or time, you'll create a request for each slot you need filled.
4. Optionally, add photos.
5. Publish your request.
Your request will be displayed on the All Activity tab as well as the Requests tab
Tips for Effective Requests
Be clear and specific about what you need.
Let people know if flexibility is possible.
Don't hesitate to post multiple requests if your needs change.
