How do I set up Meal Train?
Setting up Meal Train is easy, follow the steps below.
1. Login to your CaringBridge, then click on Ways to get help.
2. Click on Start a Meal Train.
3. You will be directed to the Meal Train website, where you can set up your Meal Train following the prompts on their website.
ProTip: Copy the link to your Meal Train. You will need it to connect your Meal Train to your CaringBridge.
Once you have created your Meal Train, you can connect it to your CaringBridge.
4. Click on Already have a Meal Train?
5. Paste or type in your Meal Train link into the field provided, then click Save and Connect.
6. You will get a green confirmation message that your Meal Train has successfully been connected.
