What are the differences between the different privacy settings on CaringBridge?
There are three privacy settings on CaringBridge that allow you to control who can visit your CaringBridge.
Public - Only signed-in CaringBridge users can comment. What you share may be viewed publicly.
Semi-Private - All signed in users may see and respond to what you share.
TIP: This is our most common privacy setting and is set as the default for new sites.
Private - Only people you add to the Approved Visitors List can view your page.
You can also control how your CaringBridge is found in search and how your CaringBridge is shared.
CaringBridge Search Visibility - Allows other people to find your CaringBridge by searching on CaringBridge.org.
Public Search Engine Visibility - Allows people to find your CaringBridge using a web search engine like Google.
Visitor Sharing - Allows you to control if others can share your Caring Bridge.
You can update your privacy settings at any time by clicking on Page Settings, then clicking on Privacy, then make your selections.
NOTE: CaringBridge no longer offers password protected pages. If you are looking to limit your sites visibility, we recommend updating your Privacy settings to Private.
Access Request - This setting is available ONLY on Private sites. It controls whether people can request access. You can review your pending request in the Pending Access Requests.
TIP: If you toggle this off, you will have to manually add people to your approved visitors list.
