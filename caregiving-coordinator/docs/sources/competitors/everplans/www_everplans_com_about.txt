 About
 Everplans helps you organize, store, and securely share all of your most important information. From your home, to your documents, to your personal decisions.
Everplans was founded in 2012 by Abby Schneiderman and Adam Seifer, both of whom have a strong track record of building and growing ground-breaking technology businesses. In October 2024, Everplans was acquired by Precoa .
