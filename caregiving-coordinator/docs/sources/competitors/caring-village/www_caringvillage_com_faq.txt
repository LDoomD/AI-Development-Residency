 Frequently Asked Questions
 Need help? Be sure to visit our help center  for answers to your questions!
 What is Caring Village? Expand 
 Caring Village is a first-of-its kind online dashboard and mobile app that allows families to easily communicate, collaborate, and coordinate caregiving activities. 
 Users can build an online village for their loved ones and invite family members, friends, neighbors, and professional caregivers to participate in their care.
 The dashboard and complementary mobile app feature a wellness journal, a customizable calendar of events, sharable to do lists with reminders, secure in-app messaging, and caregiver preparedness checklists that make it easier, safer, and less stressful to care for an aging loved one.
 Who is Caring Village For? Expand 
 Caring Village is for both family and professional caregivers. Share caregiving responsibilities with a trusted circle of family members, friends, neighbors, and care professionals. Our goal at Caring Village is to ensure that you, or anyone else who wants to lend a hand, have the most comprehensive set of resources available to provide the best care for your loved one.
 How Do I Create a Village? Expand 
 To create a village, Sign Up with either your e-mail, Apple or Google account. Once you have done so, you will be walked through the process of creating your very own village.
 How Much Does Caring Village Cost? Expand 
 Our Free Plan allows up to two members and is ad-supported.
 If you’d like to add more members or create additional villages and enjoy premium features, you can upgrade to one of our paid plans:
 Circle Plan: Up to 2 villages and 5 members per village , plus access to premium features.
 Village Plan: Up to 5 villages and 25 members per village , plus all premium features.
 It’s always free to join a village as a member , and there’s no limit to the number of villages you can be a member of.
 To learn more about our plans, pricing and premium features, visit our homepage.
 If you're a business looking to use Caring Village for your patients or clients, please contact us .
 How Many Members Can I Invite to My Village? Expand 
 Our free villages can have up to two members. Circle plan subscribers can have up to 2 villages, 5 members per village plus premium features. Village plan subscribers can have up to 5 villages, 25 members per village plus premium features. It is completely free to join a village as a member and there are no limits to the amount of villages you can be a part of. To find out more about our available plans and pricing check out our homepage.
 What Can I Do With My Village? Expand 
 Once created, your personalized village becomes the "Command Center" for all of your caregiving activities. Invite family, friends, neighbors, and care professionals to share in the responsibilities of caregiving. The dashboard and mobile app feature:
 A Personalized Village: Share the care with others, no matter where they live.
 A Wellness Journal: Keep your village updated or check in on the day-to-day status of your loved one
 A Customizable Calendar: Coordinate schedules and avoid missed appointments
 Shareable To Do Lists: Distribute responsibilities and request volunteers
 Secure Messaging: Communicate quickly and effectively with village members
 Preparedness Checklists: Prepare for the unexpected of tomorrow
 Is My Village Secure? Expand 
 Your data is safe and secure with Caring Village. All data stored is fully encrypted and secured through role-based access. Information shared within your village will only be visible to other members of that village.
 We take your personal information security very seriously, which is why we created Caring Village from the ground up to protect your data.
 Can I Create/Join More Than One Village? Expand 
 We welcome everyone to create and/or join as many villages needed to care for their loved ones.
 Once you are in the dashboard, click on "Select Another Village" in the menu and then click on "My Villages". From here, you can view all of your villages, join an existing village, view pending invitations for other villages and create a new village.
 How Do I Download the Mobile App? Expand 
 You can download the Caring Village mobile app by visiting the App Store or Google Play.
 For more info, check out our Mobile App page.
