 OUR STORY
 Built by Caregivers,
 for Caregivers 
 We created Caring Village because we understand first-hand how difficult it can be — emotionally and physically — to provide care for an aging loved one. We've lived through the stress, exhaustion, worry, confusion, and guilt.
 THE CHALLENGE
 Family Caregivers Need a Better System 
 Millions of Americans are caring for an aging parent, a spouse, or a loved one with a chronic condition. Some are juggling work and kids. Some are managing care while navigating their own retirement or health challenges.
 All of them are tracking medications, doctor's appointments, and family updates — often with no system to keep it all together.

 OUR SOLUTION
 One App to Organize It All 
 Caring Village replaces the scattered texts, spreadsheets, and sticky notes with a single command center. Coordinate care plans, track medications, share calendars, store documents, and get 24/7 guidance from Julia, our AI caregiving assistant — all in one place.

 WHAT DRIVES US
 Our Values 
 Empathy First
