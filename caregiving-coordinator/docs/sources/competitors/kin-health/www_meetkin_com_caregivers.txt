 FAQ
 Who is Kin for? 
 Kin is designed for anyone with doctor visits, but can be particularly useful if you have a lot of repeat visits, specialist appointments, or manage care for someone else. Kin is often used during pregnancy check-ups, child well visits, chronic illness checkups and is also great for caregivers. We've also recently introduced Kin for vet visits so you can now use it for your furry friend!
 Do I need my doctor's permission to use Kin? 
 Kin is recommended by doctors across the U.S., but we still recommend you ask your doctor for permission before using Kin at your visit.
 Does Kin support multiple languages? 
 Yes. Kin supports recording visits and reading summaries in both English and Spanish. We’re working to support more languages in the future.
 How much does Kin cost? 
 Kin is completely FREE for users, whether you're a patient or caregiver, you can download the app directly in the app stores today.
 What happens to my data when I use Kin? 
 Kin was founded by doctors and we take privacy very seriously. You can read more about our privacy practices on the privacy page .
 Can I use Kin to manage someone else's doctor visits? 
 Yes! Kin is designed to be shared. While the patient has full control of which visits they share with whom, if you are a caregiver managing someone else's care, you can ask to be added to the patient's Care Circle and see shared visit summaries directly in your own Kin app.
 What can Kin do besides take notes during visits? 
 Kin allows you to sync your calendar to the app so you can get upcoming visit reminders. Kin Chat is available at any point to answer questions about your visits. You can also manage care for multiple dependents directly from your app.
 Does it work with my provider? 
 Yes. Kin works alongside your visit and does not require your doctor or clinic to install anything or change how they practice.
