 How your data is handled
 What is recorded
 Kin records your visit only when you choose to start recording.
 How it’s used
 The recording is used to generate a summary with next steps.
 Who can see your information
 Your summary is private by default. Others only have access if you choose to share it.
 How your data is protected
 Your data is encrypted and secure. Kin uses modern security practices to protect it.
 A note on HIPAA
 Kin is not a covered entity under HIPAA. Because Kin is a tool you use independently for your own purposes, it is not subject to HIPAA in the same way your doctor's office is.
 But our standard is the same: your health information is sensitive, and we treat it that way. 
 You own your data. Kin does not share it, sell it, or access it without your knowledge.
