 We believe no one should go through caregiving alone. 
 Our team is driven by a deeper purpose. We set a new standard for employer-sponsored caregiving benefits that serve employers and their employees through every caregiving journey.
 Our mission
 To help relieve the stress and anxiety felt by all caregivers.
 We know navigating the care of a loved one is confusing and overwhelming. Cariloop provides support that relieves the daily stressors and anxiety felt by caregivers.
 Our vision
 To create the global standard for the way we support caregivers, families and each other. 
 We envision a day when care challenges no longer stand in the way of anyone’s ability to be present with their loved ones.
 Our values
 Service
 We care deeply about the support we provide and meet everyone we work with where they are.
 Empathy
 We seek first to understand before being understood and are mindful of the moments we create for others.
 Innovation
 We believe that being right is less important than doing right as we use technology to enhance human relationships.
 Integrity
 We recognize the fragile moments people trust us with and respond with honesty, transparency and clarity.
 Cariloop is an organization that balances impact on people, profit, and the planet.
 We use business as a force for good by certifying as a B Corporation™ (B Corp™). This certification requires a rigorous audit of how we govern, manage, impact, and operate as a company. We are proud to have met the highest verified standards of social and environmental performance, transparency, and accountability to all of our stakeholders.
 Cariloop's statement of public benefit:
 To create a seamless blend of digital and human-centered products, services, and experiences that transform the way people care for one another. As we grow our membership base and support more families across domestic & global communities, we will then re-invest a portion of our collective time, financial resources, and services into our communities to create a material positive impact on society.
 Cariloop’s 2025 Impact Report
 As part of our commitment as a Public Benefit Corporation and a Certified B Corporation, we are proud to release the Cariloop 2025 Impact Report, which outlines the impact we’ve had on our employees, caregivers, customers, and our communities.
