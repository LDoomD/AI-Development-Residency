The caregiving benefit for every employee

Most employees care for someone. From daycare drop-offs to helping an aging parent behind the scenes, it’s a lot to manage. Our solutions are designed to ease the burden, reduce stress, and bring clarity to the chaos.

Caregiving support for working families

Navigating Care

A new diagnosis. A denied claim. Caregiving can get complicated, fast. We help working caregivers find clarity and direction when challenges arise.

Finding Care

Waitlists. Soaring costs. Reliable care can feel out of reach. We lighten the load by connecting employees with trusted providers for every need.

Managing Care

Coordinating tasks. Tracking medications. The day-to-day juggle can be overwhelming. Our tools help employees stay organized and on top of it all.

Balancing work and caregiving is the new normal

Employees are managing more than just their jobs. Our solutions provide support for the daily logistics and unexpected changes that come with caregiving.

Backup Care

Our reimagined solution gives employees the support they need to manage care gaps without the scramble. And it gives you a simple, flexible way to offer financial help that works for your team and your budget.

  • Expert-guided search for the right care
  • Seamless sourcing from a broad provider ecosystem
  • Customizable coverage to match your goals and budget
  • Flexible subsidies and fast reimbursement for affordable care
