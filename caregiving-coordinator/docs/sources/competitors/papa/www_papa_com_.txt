 Hi, we're
Papa!
 We all need a pal sometimes. Papa connects you and your loved ones with vetted, reliable Papa Pals for help with everyday tasks, transportation, light housework, and friendly companionship. 
 Try it risk-free: $30/hour with zero long-term commitment or contracts. 
 GET STARTED 
 What a Papa Pal can do
 Papa Pals are trusted, background-checked, everyday helpers right from your neighborhood—veterans, retirees, students, former teachers, and caring community members. 
 While they aren’t nurses or medical aides, they are dependable, capable adults here to make daily life easier and bring genuine connection to your door. Here is how a Pal can help you or your loved one: 
 Transportation
 Rides to the doctor, the pharmacy, or just out to lunch—with good company along the way.
 Light housework
 Tidying up, laundry, the dishes, and a little light cooking—done together.
 Companionship
 A walk, a card game, or good conversation over coffee. If it’s good company you need, they’ve got it in spades.
 Errands
 Groceries, the post office, prescription pickups—checked off without the worry.
 Getting organized
 Sorting the mail, clearing a pile, getting back on top of things—no judgment.
 And lots more
 Whatever you need a hand with, just ask—chances are we can help with that, too.

 How scheduling works
 Book, manage, and customize your Papa visits anytime from any device.
 Log in password-free
 Visit the online member portal or use the link in your text reminders to sign in using your date of birth and a quick 4-digit code sent to your phone.
 Schedule and customize your visit
 Pick a date and time, select the services you need—from household tasks to errands and more—and add specific notes or preferences for your Papa Pal.
 Manage visits and connect
 View, reschedule, or cancel visits up to 24 hours in advance right from your account, stay on track with text reminders, and enjoy your time together.

 Safety and vetting
 Trust, before anyone knocks on your door.
 Every Pal passes a comprehensive background check—including criminal, sex-offender registry, and motor-vehicle screening—before they’re ever allowed to accept a visit, plus annual re-checks and continuous monitoring after that.
 Whether it’s your home or your parent’s, inviting someone in is a big deal—we treat it that way.
