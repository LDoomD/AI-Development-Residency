 Members
 Log In Papa offers a hand to help, a shoulder to lean on, and an ear to listen—when, where, and how you need it most. Our Papa Pals serve as companion caregivers, supporting you and your loved ones in person or by phone. 
 Already enrolled? Scheduling your next visit has never been easier. Simply click the button above to log into your new online homepage, where you can view and book upcoming visits—no password needed! 
 Interested in learning more? Check if you’re eligible for Papa through a covered health plan or employer-provided benefit online or by phone, then follow the steps to enroll. Not eligible through a plan? You may be able to purchase Papa services directly; check availability or join our waitlist at get.papa.com . 
 Papa benefits
 No one can do it alone. Not the little ones, aging ones, or even those in between. Papa Pals answer the call when you or your loved ones need support.  
 * Services available through health plans and employers may vary. If you have questions about your eligibility or covered benefits, please contact your health plan or employer. 
 Companionship
 Papa Pals love a good card game, stroll down memory lane, or a new recipe. If it’s good company you need, they’ve got it in spades.
 Transportation
 Need a ride to a doctor’s appointment, help running errands, or picking up groceries? They’ll safely get you where you need to go.
 Everyday tasks
 Keeping up with the house can be overwhelming. Papa Pals offer light cleaning and gardening, meal prep, organizing, pet help, and more.
 Technology assistance
 Our Papa Pals are here to help you set up and learn how to use devices and applications to stay in touch with loved ones, enjoy games, and so much more.
 Child and parental support
 We offer companion caregiving to eligible members. Parents can rely on Papa Pals for homework supervision, after-school snacks, and entertainment.
 Caring for loved ones
 We can’t always be there in person for the ones we love. Papa Pals are available as companion caregivers nationwide to provide a little extra help to aging family members.

 How to get started
 A little support can go a long way. Whether you receive Papa as a covered benefit or choose to access Papa directly, we’ll help you find the right support for your needs. 
 Check if you’re eligible for Papa through a covered health plan or employer-provided benefit online or by phone, then follow the steps to enroll. Individuals not part of a participating plan may sign up directly at get.papa.com . 
 Schedule your first visit over the phone or online and tell us when, where, and how we can help. 
 Let us know how your Papa Pal did so that we can find you the perfect match!
