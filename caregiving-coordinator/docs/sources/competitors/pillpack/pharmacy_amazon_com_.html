    You may be wondering

    NEW: What is the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge Program?
    Amazon Pharmacy is a participating pharmacy in the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge Program. We process Bridge claims, support the prior authorization process, and deliver covered medications directly to your door. Here's what you need to know.

    Q: What is the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge Program? Starting July 1, 2026, Medicare is offering a temporary program that lets eligible Medicare Part D members get certain weight loss medications for a flat $50/month. The program runs through December 31, 2027.

    Q: What medications are covered? Wegovy, Zepbound KwikPen, and Foundayo — for weight loss only. Ozempic and Mounjaro are not included. Only 28- or 30-day supplies are covered; 90-day supplies and pen needles are not available under this program.

    Q: Am I eligible? You may be eligible if you are a Medicare Part D member and your provider prescribes a covered GLP-1 medication for weight loss. You are NOT eligible if you have a diagnosis (such as Type 2 diabetes, heart disease, sleep apnea, or liver disease) that makes your GLP-1 coverable under your existing Part D plan — even if your plan didn't actually cover it.

    Q: My Part D copay is more than $50. Can I use Bridge instead? No. If your Part D plan covers your GLP-1, you are not eligible for Bridge regardless of your copay amount. The program is only for weight loss use where Part D does not provide coverage.

    Q: Will I need a prior authorization? Yes. Your first fill requires a prior authorization from your provider, which is typically approved or denied within 72 hours. If you switch medications (e.g., from Wegovy to Zepbound), a new PA is required. Dose changes within the same drug do not require a new one.

    Q: Does the $50 count toward my deductible or out-of-pocket cap? No. The Bridge program is separate from your regular Part D coverage. The $50 copay does not count toward your deductible or annual out-of-pocket maximum.

    Q: How do I get started with Amazon Pharmacy? Add the Bridge plan to your Amazon Pharmacy insurance profile using:
    • BIN: 028918
    • PCN: MEDDGLP1BR
    • Member ID: Your Medicare number (from your red, white, and blue Medicare card)
    Once added, we will automatically attempt to bill Bridge on eligible fills. You can also call us at 855-745-5725 for help getting set up.

    Q: Can I get a 90-day supply? No. The Bridge program only covers 28- or 30-day supplies.

    Q: Where can I learn more about eligibility? Visit medicare.gov/medicare-glp-1-bridge for full program details.
    Do you take my insurance?
    Amazon Pharmacy accepts most insurance plans. Here’s how to check insurance coverage for your specific plan:

    1. If you are a new customer, check if we accept your insurance plan.

    2. If you already have an Amazon Pharmacy account, you can add your insurance card on the insurance & coupons page. From there, select “Add insurance” and upload a photo of your insurance card, take a picture of your insurance card using your device, or enter your insurance information manually.
    How do I check my medication price?
    To check a medication's price, search for it on Amazon Pharmacy. You'll see an estimated insurance price and a price without insurance.

    The estimated insurance price is based on purchases with insurance over the past two months. Your actual price may differ.

    To get your exact copay price, please contact your insurance provider, as they determine the prices. You can also transfer your prescriptions to Amazon Pharmacy. Once transferred, we'll work with your insurance provider to obtain your copay price before you place an order.
    How do I transfer or add a prescription to Amazon Pharmacy?
    There are 2 ways to send us your prescriptions.

    Ask your prescriber to send your prescriptions here (fastest option):

    • Our name/E-scribe: Amazon.com – Amazon Pharmacy Nationwide Home Delivery
    • Our fax: 512-884-5981
    • Our address: 4500 S Pleasant Valley Road, Suite 201, Austin, TX 78744-2911
    • Our prescriber and pharmacy line: 855-206-3605

    You can also transfer your prescriptions from another pharmacy.

    While we do accept prescriptions by mail, it will be much faster for your prescriber to send us a prescription electronically. Visit Can I mail in my prescription? for instructions.

    If you are not able to find a prescriber when adding a prescription, ask your provider to send us the prescription directly. You can see the prescriber on file for current prescriptions from the medication detail page.

    If your prescriber wants to know more about Amazon Pharmacy, they can visit our Prescribers page.

    Note: Prescriptions for controlled substances can only be accepted directly from your prescriber via E-Scribe.

    Where does Amazon Pharmacy deliver and how fast?
    We deliver to physical addresses in all 50 US states. We also ship to select PO Boxes; however, refrigerated medications and controlled substances must use an alternate delivery address. We cannot ship to APO/FPO/DPO addresses, Amazon Lockers, or other pickup points at this time. You'll be notified at checkout if we're unable to ship specific items to the address you've selected.

    Prescriptions will be reviewed and verified by a pharmacist before they are shipped. After this review, it will be shipped using the delivery method you select. Same-Day Delivery is available in many locations.

    For a personalized delivery time and cost estimate, visit your Pharmacy Dashboard and open up the medication's detail page. All shipping time frames are based on when the order ships.

    If you do not have a prescription on file, you can search for your medication to view its detail page and your personalized delivery estimate.
