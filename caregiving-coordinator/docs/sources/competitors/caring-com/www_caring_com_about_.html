Our Story

Our Story

In 2007, friends who were caring for their aging parents noticed the lack of online resources to help them navigate the complexities of senior living and care. Not finding what they needed, they decided to create the solution in hopes of supporting others in a similar situation. The result was Caring.com.

Today, our company is filled with people passionate about serving seniors and their caregivers. We’ve assisted millions of families across the country since our inception with the help of ever-evolving technology and resources. We continue to invest in ways to improve the search experience through comprehensive directories, hundreds of thousands of consumer reviews, our expert team of Family Advisors, and so much more — all to empower seniors and their loved ones in their search.

Today, our mission is to help as many seniors and caregivers as possible through empathetic, expert guidance, and we take that very seriously.

Meet the Experts

We collaborate with a team of subject-matter experts to create relevant and accurate resources for you and your family.

Dr. Ken Robbins

Geriatric Psychiatry Specialist

Dr. Robbins has spent his entire adult life offering medical and psychiatric advice. He’s served on the clinical faculty of the University of Wisconsin for 35 years, and is board-certified in both internal medicine and psychiatry. Over those 35 years, Dr. Robbins taught forensic psychiatry, collaborative psychiatry, consultation psychiatry, psychopharmacology, and geriatric psychiatry.

Dr. Molli Rothseid

Associate Clinical Social Worker,  PhD, MSW, ASW

Dr. Rothseid is a gerontologist and clinical social worker passionate about improving the quality of life for older adults, specifically those with Alzheimer’s disease, as well as reducing stereotypes surrounding mental illness, aging and dementia.

Gene Altaffer

Masters of Science, Gerontology

Gene works in many avenues along the continuum of long-term care, assisting thousands of clients and their families since 1995. He’s also sold long-term care insurance from numerous carriers while also assisting families with retirement planning.
