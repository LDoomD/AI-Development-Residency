 Medicare GUIDE dementia program Caring for someone with dementia? Medicare now covers your support.
 ianacare is an approved partner organization for the CMS GUIDE program — Guiding an Improved Dementia Experience. A dementia-trained Care Navigator, 24/7 support, and $2,600+ respite care benefit - at no cost to you.
