 WHAT'S INSIDE 
 Your family’s plans, lists, and meals work together in one shared place.
 Cozi is a free family organizer app that puts your shared calendar, grocery lists, to-dos, and meal plans in one place, synced to everyone's phone.
 Shared family calendar
 See who needs to be where at a glance, color-coded by person. Reminders and agenda emails mean fewer missed pickups and double-booked Saturdays.

 Grocery lists & to-do lists
