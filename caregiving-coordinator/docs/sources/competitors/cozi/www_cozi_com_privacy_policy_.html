When you visit the Platforms, we collect certain Personal Information, as well as Non-Personal Information.

“Personal Information” means any information about an identifiable individual, as further defined under applicable laws. We collect Personal Information when you submit it to the Platforms (for example when you sign up for or when you email invite other members). The Personal Information collected may include your name, email address, and IP address. In some cases, if we ask you to provide Personal Information, the provision of that information will be mandatory to participate in a particular activity, service or promotion. If you do not provide this Personal Information, your participation in that activity, service or promotion may be limited or prohibited. For example, if you choose not to provide your Personal Information in a required field when you sign-up for an account, you may not become a customer of Cozi.

“Non-Personal Information” means data in a form that does not include direct or indirect association with an identifiable individual, such as gender, language, zip code, area code, unique device identifier, location and time zone where a Platform is used, and information about customer activities on the Platform, to allow us to better understand customer behavior and improve the services, products and advertising. We collect some of this Non-Personal Information automatically when you visit the Platforms (Ad ID, IDFA, Google Advertising ID).

Where you voluntarily disclose additional personal information without being prompted by us and we are not made aware that it should take special care in handling such information, then we will not be held liable for that information under this Privacy Policy unless and to the extent that we are obliged to use and protect such personal information pursuant to and in accordance with applicable law.

Cookies and Other Technologies

Our websites, services, interactive applications, email messages and advertisements may use “cookies” and other mechanisms such as pixel tags, flash cookies and web beacons.

A "cookie" is a small data file that the Service transfers to your computer for storage to allow our server to “store” or "remember" specific information. These technologies help us to better understand user behavior and use of the Platforms which allow us to analyze trends and also to administer the Platforms. We may use "cookies" and other tracking mechanisms to help support the integrity of registration processes on the Platforms and to track marketing efforts.

We also allow third party service providers to collect information to help us track and aggregate individual use of the Platforms. We treat most information collected by cookies and other technologies as Non-Personal Information. However, to the extent that any information is considered Personal Information by local law, we will treat it as Personal Information.

We and our partners may also use cookies and other technologies to remember Personal Information when you use our Platforms, services and applications, in order to retain changes that you make and to improve your user experience.

We use the following cookies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies. Some cookies are strictly necessary to make our Platforms available to you; for example, to perform your login functionality and for user authentication and security. We cannot provide you with the Services without this type of cookies;
  • Functional cookies. These are used to recognize you when you return to our Website. This enables us to personalize our content for you, greet you by name and remember your preferences (for example, your choice of language or region);
  • Analytical or performance cookies. We also use cookies for analytics purposes in order to operate, maintain, and improve our Platforms. We use third party analytics providers, including Google Analytics and Firebase, to help us understand how users engage with the Platforms. Google Analytics and Firebase use first-party cookies to track user interactions which helps show how users use our Platforms. This information is used to compile reports and to help us improve our services. The reports disclose website trends without identifying individual visitors. You can opt out of Google Analytics without affecting how you visit our Platforms by going to https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout or via Google's Ads settings.

By providing your contact information and information about your device, you are able to register your products with us and we are able to provide you with an improved service. You do not have to choose to accept cookies. In certain jurisdictions, cookies that are not strictly necessary to provide you with services that you have not been explicitly requested, cannot be used without your prior consent. When you choose not to accept all types of cookies, certain service features and functionality may no longer work properly, or at all. It may be possible to disable some cookies by modifying your browser preferences. Please refer to https://www.allaboutcookies.org/ for further information about cookies and how to disable them.
