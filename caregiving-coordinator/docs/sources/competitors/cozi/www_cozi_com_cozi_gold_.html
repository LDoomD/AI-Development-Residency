Cozi Gold

Accessible by everyone in the family on iPhone, Android, & mobile browsers.

Month View on Mobile

See your whole month in one place! Update your settings on iOS & Android to easily access important details. It’s the easiest way to keep your family’s schedule organized on the go.

No Ads

Stay focused on what matters—your family’s plans. With Cozi Gold, you’ll enjoy an ad-free experience and see more of your own calendar, lists, and schedules on every screen.

Calendar Search

Need to find that doctor’s visit or soccer game? Instantly search your calendar to find a specific event.

More Reminders

Need extra nudges? Set up to three reminders for any event so your family never misses a game, appointment, or celebration.

Calendar Change Notifications

Plans change—we get it! Cozi automatically notifies everyone right away so your whole family stays on the same page.

Birthday Tracker

Never forget a special day! Track all your loved ones’ birthdays (and their ages) in one easy spot.

Shopping Mode

Make shopping a breeze! Checked items drop to the bottom, new ones stand out, and your list stays neat and easy to follow while you’re on the go.
