What is Lantern?

Lantern was a digital platform that provided end-of-life planning tools and resources, helping individuals and families navigate everything from wills and healthcare proxies to memorial planning and grief support. In 2023, Lantern was acquired by Wellthy and fully integrated into our platform. Today, Lantern’s trusted content and planning tools live on through Wellthy’s Care Concierge and Care Planning experiences, offering compassionate support for families preparing for and navigating end-of-life decisions.

What are Wellthy’s core values?

Wellthy’s core values guide everything we do—from how we support families to how we operate as a team. Our values include:

  • Be responsive: We respond and take action quickly and thoughtfully.
  • Be persistent: We have a never-take-no, never give up, squeaky wheel mindset.
  • Be collaborative: We always look for ways to help, acknowledge and celebrate one another.
  • Be humble: We're hungry for improvement and comfortable learning from our mistakes.
  • Be curious: We have investigative attitudes, go beyond what’s expected, and always ask "why.”
  • Be purposeful: We live out our mission every day and with every interaction.

These values shape our culture and ensure that we provide thoughtful, personalized, and meaningful caregiving support.
