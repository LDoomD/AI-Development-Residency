 Wellthy is a leading caregiving support solution that helps families navigate complex care needs—from eldercare and childcare to chronic conditions, mental health, and special needs—by connecting them with dedicated care experts. Wellthy has two main products: Care Concierge and Backup Care.
 What is Wellthy?
 When was Wellthy founded?
 Wellthy was founded in 2014 with the mission to make caregiving easier, more affordable, and more accessible for families.
 When was Wellthy founded?
 Who founded Wellthy?
 Wellthy was co-founded by Lindsay Jurist-Rosner, who was inspired by her personal experience caring for her mother with multiple sclerosis for nearly three decades. She co-founded the company with Kevin Roche, who also serves as Chief Technology Officer.
 Who founded Wellthy?

 What is Lantern?
 Lantern was a digital platform that provided end-of-life planning tools and resources, helping individuals and families navigate everything from wills and healthcare proxies to memorial planning and grief support. In 2023, Lantern was acquired by Wellthy and fully integrated into our platform. Today, Lantern’s trusted content and planning tools live on through Wellthy’s Care Concierge and Care Planning experiences, offering compassionate support for families preparing for and navigating end-of-life decisions.
