 Why We Do What We Do 
 In 2012, brothers Omri and Rotem Shor faced a family health emergency. Due to a miscommunication about his medication schedule, their father, a diabetic, accidentally took an extra dose of insulin. It was a mistake which put his life in grave danger. 
 Combining their entrepreneurial skills, the brothers created Medisafe to help patients like their father manage complex prescription schedules, and to provide information and promote collaboration throughout the healthcare industry.

 Frequently Answered Questions 
 How do I create an account to backup my data? Collapse 
 Tap on your avatar on the top left and tap “My Profile”
 Choose an avatar and complete your information
 Tap “Sign Up”
 Add your email address and a password and tap “Sign up”
 Confirm your email and your account will be created
 I forgot my password! Expand 
 In order to reset your password, please follow these steps:
1. Tap on “Log in” (on the first welcome screen, or from the “More” menu inside the app)
2. Tap on “Forgot password?”
3. Enter the email address you used to create an account with Medisafe
If the system doesn’t recognize your previous account, try to use another email, or send us an email to  [email protected] 
 Why did my reminders stop working? Expand 
 The main reason is due to battery saving settings and cleaning apps like Clean Master, 360 Security, CM security, Fast Booster and other similar apps. While cleaning your device, these apps terminate Medisafe’s reminders and put your health at risk.
 How to edit medication times/schedules on iPhone? Expand 
 In order to edit a medication, please follow these steps:
 Tap “Medications” on the bottom menu
 Swipe left on a medication and select “Edit”
 Or tap on the medication and tap “Edit” on the top right
 When you are done editing, tap “Update”
 How to edit medication times/schedules on Android phones? Expand 
 In order to edit a medication:
1. Tap “Medications” on the bottom menu
2. Tap on the medication you want to edit
3. Tap on the pencil icon on the top right
 Alternatively, you can do the following steps:
1. Tap on a dose of the medication on the “Home” screen
2. Tap on the pencil icon on the top right
3. Tap “All future doses”
 How do I send a Medication Status Report? Expand 
  Tap “More” on the bottom menu and then tap “Report”
 Tap “Send”
 Tap “Send Status Report” for a report that includes medication adherence and measurements
 You can then edit the start and end date and the medication to include on the report
 Tap “Send” on the top right
 Alternatively, tap “More” on the bottom menu and tap “Doctors”
 Tap on your doctor and tap “Send status report to doctor”
