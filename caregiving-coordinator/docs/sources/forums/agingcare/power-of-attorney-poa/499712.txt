=== COMMENT 6 (2026-10-03T08:08:53.913-0400) ===
You can resign as the primary but if the secondary doesn't have the willingness or competency to take over... it will be a mess for your Mom and she'll eventually get a court-assigned guardian if the secondary also resigns or doesn't step up.

FYI you won't be able to legally "bounce" to second unless it already has this in your current PoA document as an option. Once you resign, you're out. Does this secondary PoA live near your Mom at least? 

It may be worth it hire a tax attorney or elder law attorney with Medicaid experience (one from her home state) to review the IRA situation and help solve it faster and fully. 

I have been PoA for relatives who lived far away from me. I had to travel there at least twice a year to keep things on the rails. If you work through this issue without resigning you may want to move your Mom to closer proximity to you -- it will make being her PoA easier. 

I'm sorry this is such a stressful situation for you, but it can be solved.
