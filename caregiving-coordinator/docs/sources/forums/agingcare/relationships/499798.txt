=== POST ===
I am a 60 year old, never married, male, only child. There are no siblings, no wife, no ex-wife and no children to assist me. I have a cousin 1200 miles away but she and I are the only 2 "kids" in the family. Everybody else is dead and would be 100 years old. :)
I have been caring for my now almost 99 year old mother for four years. She has vascular dementia. At this point, while I can still talk to her she is not always rational. Medically, she is bedbound unless someone picks her up and moves her, incontinent, and has a feeding tube.
I have not worked for the last year because her anxiety about being at home with a caregiver was making it impossible for me to focus on a job, especially the three days a week I had to go to the office.
This past August, I used one of her short hospital stays to discharge her to a nursing home (a good one). I feel really guilty about doing this but it was becoming too all-consuming for me to have her at home and, quite frankly, medically not really safe for her to be at home. Regardless of these realities, I still feel guilty about placing her in a nursing home. Sometimes she says I "tricked" her into being there.

=== COMMENT 3 (2026-10-16T18:13:28.590-0400) ===
Ativan worked quite well for my mother with vascular dementia living in Memory Care Assisted Living. And a max dose of Wellbutin antidepressant as well. What on earth type of medication is considered a "restraint"??? One that would keep mom sleeping all day? Even that is not a "restraint" but sometimes a merciful way to keep a patient calm. Fwiw, my mother never slept all day with Ativan and Wellbutrin. She was just able to be a bit less ugly and more civilized in nature with those meds being scheduled. Otherwise, she said the most foul things to me you could possibly imagine. After doing my best for 2 very elderly parents for 11 years, as an only child myself. I think if she'd slept all day it'd have been a blessing, tbh. She was convinced I was locking up her deceased family members in the closets at the MC. 

There's no reason to feel guilt for placing her. It takes a team of people working 24/7 to care for elders with dementia. Especially ones like our mothers who have seriously limited mobility. It's unrealistic to do it all yourself, or to expect such a thing from yourself. You did a huge job for 4 years as it is, and that's more than enough. 

Let yourself off the hook now and give yourself some grace. You deserve to.

Your Mom needs to be on medication for her anxiety. This is merciful because dementia robs people of their ability to use reason and logic, robs them of their ability to have empathy for others' stresses and situations, and basically robs them of the ability to be at peace. Therefore, she needs meds first and foremost. What comes out of her mouth is a product of her disease, not her heart. People with dementia often have bouts of paranoia and can be very negative. My 97-yr old Mom can be nasty and was having depression. The lowest dose of Lexapro helped her a lot. 

Never feel guilty to wanting to live your own life. I'm sure your Mom pre-dementia would not want you to be suffering with guilt because of her. Guilt is for felons and immoral people. You've done nothing wrong. 

Also, I hope you're not paying for her care.
