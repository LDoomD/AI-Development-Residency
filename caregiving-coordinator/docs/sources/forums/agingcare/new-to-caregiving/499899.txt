=== COMMENT 2 (2026-10-31T08:47:32.730-0400) ===
If she's not on Medicaid, you would call her insurance which in this case is Blue Cross/Blue Shield and ask someone if the insurance has a paid family caregiver program. Some insurance policies offer them. If they do not have such a program, they don't. If they do, then they will instruct you on what the next steps are for qualifiying your mother for it. If they don't offer a paid family caregiver program, they don't but they may be able to direct you to other resources in your state that can help.

Your mother isn't getting Medicaid with this kind of insurance unless she gets put into LTC and qualifies for it. So a Medicaid-sponsored paid family caregiver program is not going to be an option. Be wary of these programs though. People think the "state" pays for the family caregiver. It does not. It pays up front, but if the person receiving the care has assets like a house, bank accounts, or insurance policies Medicaid gets paid back whatever they paid out to the family caregiver. 

There may be grant programs in your state that help pay for homecare. My state has them, but they get used up fast. Talk to the state's Agency on Aging they can tell you. Or better still, if the town your mother lives in has a senior center, they will know this information.

Primarily, your Mom needs to have a written employment contract with you and pay you from her own funds. That's the only way you can be paid as her exclusive, full-time caregiver.

Medicaid could pay you to do it but she'd need to qualify both medically and financially but you'd never get FT hours and the pay is dismal.

The reason for the contract is so that when the day comes she applies for Medicaid, they won't see a regular transfer of funds and think it's "gifting" which would basically create a penalty phase or disqualify her. 

It'd be worth a consultation with an elder law attorney to get it right. I hope you're her PoA. If you'll be providing her hands-on care, then being her PoA would be a a requirement for me.
