=== COMMENT 2 (2026-10-17T14:42:07.850-0400) ===
Hi [member], I'm sorry you are having trouble getting the help you need.
I'm pretty sure those are legitimate agencies, if you are calling the right number. You may be contacting the wrong agencies for your needs. What is it, exactly, that you are wanting? An in-home care provider? Are you hoping to find volunteers?
Volunteers for in-home care are few and far between, if they exist at all. Someone is paying them, whether it comes out of your pocket or not. 

If the person you care for is eligible for Medicaid assistance, start by calling the local Medicaid office. It would be part of your county health and human services department. 
You can do an online search for Home care providers, and request services, but be prepared, it will cost you $25 to $50 per hour to get someone who can provide help in the home, unless you can get Medicaid assistance.

Part of your trouble is living in a rural mountain town. The farther away you are from a metropolitan area, the harder it is to find services. I have the same challenge - and I'm only 20 minutes outside of a major metropolitan city! There is such a demand for care providers, they can pick and choose the jobs they take. And many "don't want to drive that far".
