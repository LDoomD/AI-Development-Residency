Mandala, NO, it will not get better! It can only get worse!

PLEASE, make a plan for your dad to live independently - you can help him to apply for senior HUD housing which is government subsidized apartment living for seniors and disabled.
Why on earth did your dad move in with you anyway? What was the discussion beforehand? I'm only asking because if you agreed, or if you thought this was an acceptable idea, then he moved in with an expectation that this was agreeable to you. Maybe you thought it would be, until your personalities clashed. It's hard learning to live with anyone after you've lived on your own for years.
And, yes, the shock of this significant change to your lifestyle is probably taking you by surprise. You thought everything would go on as it has, with this other person in your home and life every day?
It's up to you to decide what you are and are not willing to live with.
Maybe you can have a meeting with Dad and make up some house rules that you both abide by. That could work. Remember, this is a transition for him, too. 

You are not obligated to provide your father a place to live. His decisions and life choices have landed him where he is. I think you would feel better if you help him to find somewhere else to live independently and you can get together as often as you want. If he is unable to live independently, then consider Assisted Living, Memory Care, and Skilled Nursing facilities for him. If he has limited income and assets, help him to apply for Medicaid, which will pay for him to have the services he needs and qualifies for.
