=== COMMENT 10 (2026-08-02T13:56:06.000-0400) ===
I would consult with an elder law or estate planning attorney to number one make sure your Mom has all her legal ducks in a row (like assigning a durable PoA and having an Advance Healthcare Directive and Will) and number two, to see if a QIT will help her get her income lower so she'll qualify for Medicaid. A QIT is a Qualified Income Trust. The money she earns that exceeds the Medicaid limit goes into the trust. At the end of her life if she used Medicaid, the money in that trust is part of the Medicaid Recovery, not inheritance. 

My 97-yr old Mom is on Medicare and has BCBS supplemental gap insurance. Her Internist ordered in-home PT and OT. See if your Mom's primary care doc can do this so you don't have to take her to appointments.
