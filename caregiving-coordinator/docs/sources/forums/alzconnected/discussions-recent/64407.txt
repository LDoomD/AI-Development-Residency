Common Abbreviations

People

ADLO = Loved One with Alzheimer's disease

BF = Boyfriend

BIL = Brother-in-Law

DD = Dear/Darling Daughter

DH = Dear Husband

DS = Dear/Darling Son

DW = Dear/Darling Wife

FIL = Father-in-Law

GF = Girlfriend

GLO = Greetings to Loved Ones

GMIL = Grandmother-in-Law

GFIL = Grandfather-in-Law

LO = Loved One

MIL = Mother-in-Law

NOK = Next of Kin

OH = Other Half

PWD/PLWD = Person with Dementia/Person Living with dementia\

SIBs = Brothers and Sisters or Siblings

SIL = Sister-in-Law

SO = Significant Other

Popular Online Acronyms

AKA = Also known as

BBL = Be back later

BTW = By the way

BRB = Be right back

IMCO = In my considered opinion

IMHO = In my humble/honest opinion

IOW = In other words

J/K = Just kidding

LOL = Laugh out loud

OMG = Oh my gosh!

OT = Off topic

TIA = Thanks in advance

TTT = “to the top”

TTYL = Talk to you later

WDYT = What do you think

Types of Dementia

AD or ALZ = Alzheimer's disease

ADRD = Alzheimer's disease and related dementias

EO or EOAD = Early onset Alzheimer's disease (diagnosis before the age of 65)

FTD = Frontotemporal dementia

FTLD = Frontotemporal lobar degeneration

LBD or LB = Lewy Body disease or dementia

MD = Mixed dementia

VD = Vascular disease or dementia

TBI = Traumatic brain injury

CTE = Chronic traumatic encephalopathy

NPH = Normal pressure hydrocephalus

MCI = Mild Cognitive Impairment

YOAD = Younger onset Alzheimer’s disease (another way to say diagnosis before age 65)

Stages of Dementia

ES = Early stage of Alzheimer's disease (in the beginning of the disease progression)

LS = Late Stage of Alzheimer's

MS = Middle Stage of Alzheimer's

Care Places

AL or ALF = Assisted living facility

ADC = Adult day care

CCC / CCRC / LPC = Continuing care community, continuing care retirement community, or life plan community (a facility which offers independent living, assisted living and nursing home residences)

LTC / LTSC = Long-term care / skilled care

MC = Memory care

NH = Nursing home

RCF = Residential care facility (group home, personal care home, adult family home)

SNF = Skilled nursing facility/nursing home

Medical

CNA = Certified nursing assistant

Delusion = false ideas that are firmly believed in spite of contradictory proof

DNR = Do not resuscitate

DON = Director of nursing

DVT = Deep vein thrombosis

DX = Diagnosis

ER = Emergency room

G-Tube = Gastrostomy tube

Hallucination = false perceptions of objects or events involving the senses; seeing, hearing or tasting things that are not there

LPN = Licensed practical nurse

LVN = Licensed vocational nurse

MMSE = Mini mental state exam

OT = Occupational therapy

PCP = Primary care physician

PEG Tube = percutaneous endoscopic gastrostomy tube (feeding tube inserted direct into the abdomen)

PT = Physical therapy

RN = Registered nurse

SLT – Speech/language therapy

TIA = Transient ischemic attack (mini-stroke)

UTI = Urinary tract infection

Legal

APS = Adult protective services

CELA = Certified elder law attorney

DPOA = Durable power of attorney

HIPAA = Law Health Insurance Provider Accountability Act

LTCO = Long term care ombudsman

LW = Living will

NAELA = National Academy of Elder Law Attorneys

POA = Power of attorney
