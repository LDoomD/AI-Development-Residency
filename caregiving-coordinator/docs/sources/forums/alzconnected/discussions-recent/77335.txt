=== COMMENT 2 (2026-10-13T19:59:28+00:00) ===
Her hallucinations are as real to her as the hand before your face.    Will you pay believe a person who tells you it is not there?

You will not be successful trying to distract her.  You MAY be successful in agitating her over them.    

If she is fearful of them or cause her distress, there are certain meds that can help.  Otherwise, I would just not worry about them.
