After participating in many support groups and hearing how many caregivers have gone in to therapy, I asked them a few questions and asked if therapy had been helpful.  The overwhelming response was YES!  They don't know what they would do without a therapist.  My husband has mild to moderate dementia, but the bigger problem for me is his daughter and exwife that have given me more grief than anyone else.  They aren't supportive, they degrade me (we've been married 23 years so you think they would be over it) and all their worries are about themselves, me, me, me.   I start with the therapist Tuesday and I can't wait, someone I can talk to without judgement and willing to work WITH me to help with my severe anxiety and depression.  I am so longing to listen to someone to help support a caregiver during this hard journey.
