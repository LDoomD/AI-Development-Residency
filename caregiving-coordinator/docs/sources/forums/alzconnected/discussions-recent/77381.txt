I am sorry you and your wife are going through this. Figuring out the right medications is very difficult. My mother had a negative reaction to a couple of medications which was making things worse initially and we did not know it. One caused delirium. How quickly is Hopsice changing the medicine. These are antipsychotics. They have serious side effects. They need to be tapered carefully. While one medication is getting out of the system, it will have an impact. Then the new medicine needs time to get to an effective level. Some of the medications will not become effective until taken for weeks or months. Switching medications quickly will not be helpful and will have a negative impact. A very slow, careful approach needs to be taken. The sedatives may be creating issues as well.

When you say vocalizing, is it tolerable? What exactly is she doing? Are you able to manage the situation? Are you both safe? If you are, I would continue to keep her in your home and figure it out. I would not send her anywhere.
