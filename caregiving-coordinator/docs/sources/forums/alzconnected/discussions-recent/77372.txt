=== COMMENT 5 (2026-10-20T22:43:08+00:00) ===
DH's and mom's cardiologists and my PCP are particular about precision. They recommend the Omron @[member] likely uses.  It measures about 4 x 4 x 6" out of the box and is Bluetooth enabled. Wrist and watch apps are not as accurate. The Omron is as reliable as a professional cuff and stethoscope; DS is a nursing student, so we have both in the house at the moment. 

You can manually enter the data on the iPhone Health app. I'm trialing my 8th BP medication so I take it daily. My PCP put a BP chart in the myChart or I can send her a screenshot of my weekly readings.
