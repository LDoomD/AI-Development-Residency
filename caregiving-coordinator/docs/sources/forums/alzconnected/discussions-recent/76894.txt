=== COMMENT 2 (2026-08-20T15:11:11+00:00) ===
Oh my gosh, this is giving me flashbacks and shivers. My mom was (and still a little bit) obsessed with her cell phone. While I was the one she called and texted incessantly, she often made repeated calls and sent strange texts (sometimes mean) to her friends. It was horrible and I wanted to "accidentally" run over the phone with my car.

But then she said something that made me pause…she said it was her last lifeline to the "outside" world and, without it, she felt so lonely and isolated (partly due to language barriers). She also was using the messaging as a way to work out her thoughts and feelings. She would hit send without realizing or understanding that we were actually receiving every rambling, sometimes awful, thought she was having.

I used the block feature almost all of the time (because she would call 10x-plus in a row while at work and I needed my phone to be on) and I asked her friends to do the same. As her everything (cognitive, coordinating the litte teeny phone keys - which also led to issues around calling) declined, I got her a memory phone, which has really helped. It's photo-based so it's clear who she is calling and there are features that can be set - like a long button hold to start the call (instead of instant touch), quiet time setting (with a voice feature), etc. The phone doesn't do much, but neither does my mom. It can call, video call, and text. And I have full control of the her phone remotely (and she doesn't know). And I can set reminders, including when to charge her phone.

IDK. I think I would be lost without my phone because she's right - it's her only communication to the former life and outside world. At least now I can control just how much access she has (like her AL home and their exit doors).

(I guess the other plus is I don't have siblings to contradict me…I am guessing from above that whoever has the POA is the final decision-maker?)

=== COMMENT 5 (2026-08-20T21:25:30+00:00) ===
I am my mom's only, so I went through the phase of her calling me multiple times per day, many of them back to back. I had to select which calls to accept and which to let go to vm. If I spoke to her within the hour, I ignored all the follow-up calls. I told her to write down whatever came to mind in the interim, and we would discuss the next time we spoke. I got it to the point of no more than 3 times per day; always a morning and bedtime check-in, along with a random call.

Their boredom and frequent calls will drive you insane. If your sister who is paying for the phone does not mind the calls, let her accept them all. If you have a Samsung phone, each caller can get their own ringtone. Select a "Silent" or a "Single beep" ringtone for mom's number.
