=== COMMENT 2 (2026-10-30T20:07:53+00:00) ===
As you've found logic, explanations, trying to rephrase requests won't get you anywhere. At this stage you can either work around her -hiding & securing things , consider adult day care or care givers in the home to give you a break or consider placement. 

If you haven't already seeing an elder law attorney will give you guidance on the legal papers you'll need and options for getting help with placement costs.

Doing the kind of care a PWD requires in a home setting 24/7 requires mental and physical stamina . Just like some people have the patience to run pre-schools some can handle it ,some caregive their LO after placement - the work doesn't end with placement .  

Both of you need to be safe- keeping you healthy is a part of it too. 

Have you talked to a social worker at the Alz Assoc— they can help  at no charge .
