just because a feeding tube  is mentioned doesn’t mean one is needed… or for that fact, wanted.  Nor are you required to agree to it. 

For now -try offering shakes such as Boost or Ensure or actual milkshakes. Ice cream or other high calories foods that she likes.  Her taste buds could be declining - the last ones to shut off are the one for sweet tastes.

=== COMMENT 13 (2026-10-25T14:48:53+00:00) ===
My wife lost a lot of weight and got down to 86 pounds. they said it was "failure to thrive". We got her on hospice and was preparing for the end. She for some reason started eating better. That was a year ago and she is holding her own weight and still on hospice. I had decided to always make sure she had food that she liked available for her but if she was able to eat and just didn't want to to let it go. The way the hospice nurse put it is to listen to her body. I think if she is as comfortable and happy as can be then trying to force her to eat is only going to upset her and not change the outcome.
