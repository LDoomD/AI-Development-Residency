Do the questions differ day to day or do they usually involve the same concern? If the questions are different, it is best to just answer the question each time. You may also try to then interest her in doing something else which may stop the repeated questions for a time. If the questions involve the same concern, you may be able to address the underlying concern which may improve the situation.

=== COMMENT 6 (2026-10-20T20:34:00+00:00) ===
https://alzconnected.org/discussion/comment/284565#Comment_284565
    

If it's early in the progression, having a calendar or message board may be helpful. If it's later and that won't help, I would simply answer with whatever you are doing at the moment. Like, right now we are going to have lunch and then discuss what's being made, etc. Or, right now we are going to watch this movie, it's about…., right now we are going do a little laundry, can you help fold these… I think you have to answer the questions and try to start a conversation or activity to help keep her busy with something for a while.
