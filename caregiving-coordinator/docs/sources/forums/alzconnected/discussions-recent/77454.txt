=== COMMENT 5 (2026-10-30T14:30:58+00:00) ===
@[member]

I am so sorry this is where you are right now. 

Some things to consider for your shopping list—

A bag for the car with your supplies and a complete change of clothing down to the shoes. You might consider an extra outfit for yourself as well. 

A trashcan than contains odors unless it's easier to buy smaller bags (for Depends, gloves, wipes) and run out to your outdoor trash. 

Shaving cream— old school foam rather than gel. The dollar store is a great place to get this stuff.

Odoban to add to laundry to eliminate odors. IME, this is the best product out there for this. I've gotten it at Lowe's. 

A bidet can be really helpful for cleanup. IME, the heated seat/water makes it more pleasant to use. 

Side note: That 25lb weight loss in 6 months is worth watching. This can be a sign of progression if it's the result of either or both poor digestion/appetite. Dad lost about that amount in the 6 months before he went into MC and then lost another 25lb in his last 2 months despite us plying him with calorie dense foods daily. In retrospect, we should have had him evaluated for hospice once we lost 10% of his weight. We didn't; mom was new in the area and could have used the support.

Hi,  So sorry about your weekend…

Regarding hospice—You can always ask a hospice company for an evaluation, but for straightforward dementia the person generally has to be into stage 7 for Medicare coverage to kick in.

Here's a good summation:

    
        https://www.cgsmedicare.com/hhh/education/materials/pdf/hospice_terminal_prog_dementia_alzheimers.pdf
    

Autoship on Amazon for the briefs or supplies is helpful.  We used the Tena briefs for my mom as she was in her mid-90's and the knit material on the sides was more forgiving on her skin.

You may want to buy disposable chucks as well--Amazon has many choices. The larger disposable ones are a nice backup to the washable ones, and smaller ones are good during emergency changes if you need to sit your person down on something clean while you gird your loins for the clean up, or to throw on the floor as a landing spot for soiled things.

As a 'just in case' precaution I took off the covers to my upholstered cushions and wrapped the seats in trash can liners, then put the covers back on (I left the liners open loosely at the back when I zipped them back up, so air could escape when people sat down). I also put chucks down on the car and sofa seats and covered them with a washable covers so it looked nicer.  Amazon has some nice washable covers.
