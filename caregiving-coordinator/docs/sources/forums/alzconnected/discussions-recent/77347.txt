Hospice update: Well I thought it was the right decision in this world of trying to do right by our spouse. Hospice was a bust. We requested care for midday to afternoon visits and to have English-speaking caretakers. The very first day, a lady showed up at 8:00am thinking she was going to give me, rather than my wife a shower. She spoke no English and used a phone translator to "communicate" with me. I sent her away. Later that day a nurse arrived and without assessing my wife said she was prescribing a laxative for her. My wife surely does not need a laxative! We swere promised a doctor visit but that never happened. 

I soon cancelled the Hospice service and went back to the private service for daily care for my wife. In the past, we had used Hospice for my mother and for my father-in-law, for which we received great care. Times have changed, and not for the better.
