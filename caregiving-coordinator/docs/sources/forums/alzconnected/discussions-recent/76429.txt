Sorry you have reasons to join this great group. I was diagnosed with mild alzheimers July 14, 2025. I have not experienced many changes until now. I have started seeing the following changes: Word finding ( cannot come up with simple words), Apathy ( loss of interest, can just sit in the recliner all day without much motivation or interest, but try not to do this, losing train of thought at times, etc.  I wish you the best on this journey. My plan is to "try" to live in the present, "try" to get some exercise on most days, eat healthy on most days, continue to encourage myself to stay busy. The TRY part is getting to be somewhat more of a challenge, but I'm not giving in to this. My next neurology appt. is in Sept. I declined the infusions after a lot of stress with the entire infusion program. I tred aricept, but didn't see improvement, only some side affects. Take care of yourself, and my prayers for all of us dealing with this diagnosis.
