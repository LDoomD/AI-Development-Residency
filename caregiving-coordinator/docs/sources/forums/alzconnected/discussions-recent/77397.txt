My mom is the full time caregiver for my dad with Alzheimers. He been diagnosed with it for over a year. I have been trying to give Mom support. She wasn't ready to accept it.  Now she is coming to me for help. We did a caregivers support group last night.  I was able to call the Alzheimers hot line to give her resources. === COMMENT 2 (2026-10-24T01:10:05+00:00) ===
I know it is easy to say 'don't feel guilty', but maybe if mom knows she is really, seriously, helping dad by removing the driving, it might help her. You simply can't allow an unsafe driver to drive. You don't want them to hurt themselves or an innocent person. If there is a diagnosis, insurance may not pay a dime should something happen. Just something to keep in mind.

A few folks have replaced a credit card with either a prepaid one with a very low balance, or a used Visa gift card. I looked at it as: you can't expect someone who can't reason, to reasonably use a credit card.

Sorry you and mom are dealing with 'this'. I hope the group helped you both. Because, yeah, 'this' horrid thing is difficult.

=== COMMENT 5 (2026-10-24T17:20:51+00:00) ===
Can you encourage your mom to ask the keys question in a support group meeting. She may listen to others more than she listens to her child. 

Another option, and perhaps more appropriate as the keys should be removed right away, can you get on a conference call with Alz and your mom. Have the Alz person explain why the keys need to be removed now.

When you are on the phone with Alz alone, ask the trained person whether it may make sense for you to tell your mom that if she wants you to help, that she is going to need to respect your perspective, meaning car and credit cards go away. I'm not trained in this but I would think you would explain to your mom that you love her and respect her, but to help, you need to be fully part of the care team.

The car is a big deal—your dad, others, can get hurt or worse.

For the keys, the approach that worked for us is that we didn't want our mother to get hurt or hurt anyone else. That worked. Hopefully, your mother understands that. Also, if the keys should be taken now and are not, if something were to happen, she would most likely be liable. We made sure our mother got where she wanted to go in exchange.

For the credit card, can your mom get online access and monitor it? Is there a reason to take it away right now?

Hang in there and take care of yourself. It's a difficult journey and you are at the beginning.
