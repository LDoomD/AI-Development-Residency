=== COMMENT 13 (2026-10-29T20:27:50+00:00) ===
Give the tester a chance.  She needed to present a certain personality to get your LO to  cooperate and feel comfortable.  I was in the room when my step-dad was tested with the 2-3 hour test.   It was pretty comprehensive.  I think the time varies with the amount of questions they get right. For example, if they can’t  count  backwards to a specific number, then they skip the next x number of questions. The report we got a few  weeks later was very detailed. Included his reading level ( he never could read much), his type of dementia symptoms, recommendations etc. The tester was a neuropsychologist who did the testing but didn’t treat my step-dad afterwards.  My step-dad also refused to go to a neurologist - which if you  read below was really not  a big loss.

My mom never did the two hour testing.  She became the patient of a neurologist who did the 10 minute test every visit, watched her walk ever time, asked a few questions every visit, did a CT scan after the initial visit, prescribed certain medications. After a few years, I switched the medications to the family doctor because seeing the neurologist wasn’t needed because nothing else was ever suggested. Mom was frustrated at the number  of doctors  and appointments she had. 

The keys thing is an accurate statement.  We all misplace our keys because we are distracted, but we don’t put them in the freezer, or forget what they are, where they normally are, or what they are for.
