=== COMMENT 3 (2026-10-25T16:16:51+00:00) ===
I found Leqembi "effective" in the sense that after 49 infusions over about 2 years, my amyloid level was at 9 Centiloids. This is considered "amyloid clear", meaning that Leqembi removed much of the amyloid plaque that had accumulated in my brain over the preceeding decade or so. That is the prime function of both Leqembi & Kisunla - amyloid plaque removal.

Many patients are more concerned with how the drugs impact cognitive decline associated with Alzheimer's. That is a much more complex topic. It is unclear to me how I would determine my reductions in cognition without Leqembi infusions, and then compare that with my reductions in cognition I've experienced with treatment. The Phase 3 trials with Leqembi/Kisunla demonstrated they slowed cognitive decline associated with Alzheimer's, when comparing patients receiving treatment against a group receiving a placebo. This is great for the FDA in determining if they should approve the drug, but not so useful to individual patients who want to know how the drug might impact their particular situation.
