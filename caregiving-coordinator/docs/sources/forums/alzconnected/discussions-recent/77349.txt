Does anyone’s loved one keep their eyes closed a lot? My mom has entered a phase where she will “talk” to me with her eyes closed. I usually have to repeatedly ask her to try to open them. I’m not sure if this is common or just unique to her.

=== COMMENT 1 (2026-10-15T23:50:43+00:00) ===
It's common. However, do rule out any type of medical issue or infection (UTI). Sometimes my mother would do that if she was sick. When better, that behavior stopped.

But it is also a common behavior. I think to shut out too much stimulation.

I wouldn't ask her to open her eyes unless it is necessary for some reason. That's one of those things that is bothering you - not her. I say that with complete understanding as I have been in this situation. These are the types of things we have to let go of.

=== COMMENT 3 (2026-10-17T13:37:36+00:00) ===
My mom started doing this towards later stage 6. But it may have been part of withdrawing into herself as the disease progressed.  She would open her eyes and respond and interact, but if she was just sitting in her wheelchair she'd rest an elbow on the arm of the chair and cover her eyes with her hand. Maybe there's only so much processing the brain can do and reducing outside stimuli feels less overloading.
