I live in a world that is lonely. So much has been taken- my happiness, my freedom, my love of life of learning growing doing. I live in a word where I feel helpless I can’t stop this demon from taking my husband. My heart is aching it breaking with each decline. I’m living in a world where those I held near and dear are “ too busy” to lend a hand, give me a call, or ask how I’m doing. I am tired of doing it all - cooking, cleaning, daily laundry, paying bills, grocery shoping,  doctors appointments, I’m even tired of crying everyday… you all know the drill. I’m tired of trying to reason with a person who can’t! Please shower please shave please change the clothes you’ve been wearing for 5 days! Six years since his dementia diagnosis and I know there is a long road ahead . I have got to find a way to cope with the world I’m living in. Thanks all for listening 💕

=== COMMENT 6 (2026-11-01T22:32:14+00:00) ===
Does misery really love company?  I’m not sure, but I know that you have plenty of it with the caregivers who frequent AlzConnected.  You’ve expressed what so many of us feel at times.  Then, we are able to find something that helps make our lives easier.  Often, that’s getting respite help.  Look for local resources—if eligible through the GUIDE Program or Veterans Administration.  There may also be help through Senior Services or other agencies in your area.  And it could pay off to ask family and friends for specific help.  There’s no doubt you need help.  You need to take care of yourself.  Make it a priority to carve out time for activities like exercise, hobbies, or just time alone to relax.  Try to live in the moment and find something positive in each day—even if that seems difficult some days. Others who have experienced what you’re going through will respond with good ideas.  We care.
