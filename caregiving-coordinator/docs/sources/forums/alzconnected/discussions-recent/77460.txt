My dad died. My mom who has AD saw him after he died and attended the funeral but doesn’t remember. 

At first we were telling her that he died but that would cause a meltdown every time and it was heartbreaking. 

She wanders the house trying to find him and is constantly asking where he is. They were inseparable for 60+ years. 

We have tried fibbing and then diverting her attention but that’s not  working. She’s angry, very agitated and depressed. 

She’s never going to remember he died but she’s also not going to stop asking about him. 

I feel like I can’t even call her (I don’t live nearby) because I will just be a reminder of my dad. 

I can’t take this and really don’t know what to do.  Any advice?

=== COMMENT 4 (2026-10-31T01:46:44+00:00) ===
This is hard on everyone, as you have to experience your own grief while you try to shield your mother from upset. And you don't get to share your grief with your mom, which is so hard.  I agree that talking with her healthcare providers with an eye to prescribing something to reduce her background anxiety and depression may help.  Those may help reduce her focus on trying to locate him.  It will be hard for her to let go of having him around if they were inseperable but, if there are visual cues reminding her to look for him, you might try removing them for a bit. Unfortunately the name of the game is keeping her calm, so the fibs and diversions will have to continue.
