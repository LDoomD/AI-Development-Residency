The adjustment is so hard. One moment can be 'relatively' peaceful, seem happy and then it's like someone threw a switch and the mood will turn dark, sad, angry, mean…It's the Alzheimer's brain taking over, or has taken over. There are no filters anymore. My LO just says whatever and I have to roll with it. The constant criticism, being blamed for everything…it goes on and on. And, yet there will be a moment when she'll talk about what a wonderful life, we have and how much she loves me. Makes me cry when I write this. So sad.

Currently she still recognizes me but is having a real challenge with recognizing our home and how long we've lived here. Is this our house? Where do I sleep? Where should I brush my teeth? Etc…

Terrible, ugly disease. I know each case presents itself differently. But what comes after this phase.  Of course, more ugliness I know that is the reality.

=== COMMENT 5 (2026-10-20T12:45:08+00:00) ===
DW and I went through similar in the early stage. Hours long arguments that made no sense whatsoever. Day after day. After I got the hang of not arguing back as much, yes just rolling with it and medications, things eased up. That stage for us has passed thank God. Seemed to take forever. I think my DWs issues came from anxiety knowing something was wrong but not being able to figure things out, You might want to talk to her Doctors about medications. Neither of us were big on medications in general. However, after I thought about how bad it must be on her to be in such a volatile state and not know why most of the time, I changed my mind. I hope it gets better for you. Try to remember every time your DW tells you she loves you. That was also something that helped me when she was blowing my hair back in an argument.
