=== COMMENT 3 (2026-10-23T12:42:52+00:00) ===
24/7 Helpline at 800.272.3900

An IRL support group can be valuable for area specific help, but IMO this site is better in that it's open 24/7 and has a lot of members with compassion and experience to support you. Having both was great for me.

I found my local group here-

Get Involved with Your Local Chapter

There were some evening meetings and there are also virtual ones. You could also check the doctor's office if they have a social worker, an area hospital and your county's Area Agency on Aging.
