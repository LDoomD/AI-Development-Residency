My mom has recently become wheelchair bound and keeps attempting to stand or walk and falls.  Does anyone have suggestions on how to help keep her in the wheelchair?

=== COMMENT 1 (2026-10-20T01:37:55+00:00) ===
Hi Jean - welcome to 'here', but sorry for the reason.

Could you maybe put the feet up on it? Or use a removable tray as a 'reminder' barrier not to get up. Possibly a wedge type seat cushion that tilts the knees higher than the hips - this makes it a little harder to actually stand up. Maybe put her walker right in front of the wheelchair, so she would about have to use it if she does get up?

There are also wheelchair seat alarms available, so it will sound if the weight is off the seat. 

I know that attempting any type of seatbelt is considered a restraint, so I think you cannot use that unless it is doc ordered.

Sorry you are dealing with that
