=== COMMENT 1 (2026-10-30T18:53:04+00:00) ===
MIL had food issues on and off for a while. Sometimes she would simply forgot that she already ate, and other times I really think the mind wasn't communicating with the stomach whether it was full or not. I think this is fairly common. We put a type of lock on the pantry, but left out snacks she could eat at any time. We put fruit or sometimes yogurt at her eye-level in the fridge, so that would be what she went for. 

The handle cover you are using is a great idea!  and no, you are not 'just being a chicken'. This stuff can get on your nerves quickly.
