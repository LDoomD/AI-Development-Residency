     Support Your Employees 
   
 
 
 

 
 


 
 

 
 
		 

 

   
     
       How we help employees 
               Employees' care needs are complex, personal, and ever-changing. Here are a few of the top needs Cariloop Coaches help with: 
           
    
     
      
         
           
           
             Infant & Childcare 
             Parents' top request is for help finding reliable, affordable childcare.
 
           
         

      
         
           
           
             Mental Health 
             Support for loved ones, including Veterans, for emotional and behavioral challenges. 
           
         

      
         
           
           
             Elder Care 
             Employees struggle to find affordable, quality care for aging loved ones. 
           
         

      
         
           
           
             Chronic Illness Support 
             Employees need guidance for tough conversations after a serious diagnosis. 
           
         

      
         
           
           
             LGBTQIA+ Support 
             Families want inclusive mental health and affirming LGBTQIA+ care. 
           
         

      
         
           
           
             Neurodiversity Support 
             Employees need help navigating care for neurodivergent loved ones. 
           
         

      
         
           
           
             Navigating Health Insurance 
             Families need help understanding rising health costs and insurance options. 
           
         

      
         
           
           
             Legal & Financial Resources 
             Families look for legal and financial help before or during a care crisis. 
           
         

      
         
           
           
             Women’s Care 
             Women need support through family planning, postpartum, and menopause.
