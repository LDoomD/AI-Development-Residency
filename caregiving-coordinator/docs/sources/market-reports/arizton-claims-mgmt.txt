A health insurance claim or a medical insurance claim is a request raised by a policyholder for compensation of expenses incurred during medical treatment. Based on the type of claim procedure opted by a client, the insurance company verifies the claim to either clear the bills directly with the hospital or reimburse the amount spent. The high adoption rate of health insurance policies among patients for financial assistance is driving the growth of the global healthcare claims management market. Artificial Intelligence (AI) has transformed various industries across the globe and radically can alter the healthcare field. With growing advancements in the healthcare industry, AI has become an efficient, faster, and inexpensive choice for claims management. The shift toward value-based solutions is encouraging healthcare companies to move toward automated medical claims management solutions. Medical billing is one of the significant parameters in healthcare revenue cycle management. The medical billing and their respective coding contribute to establishing and categorizing patient information that dictates the billing of Medicare and insurance companies. Moreover, increased digitalization has accelerated the adoption of automation in the medical billing process. Most organizations are turning to automation to streamline and error-proof their billing systems. The significant advantage of medical billing process automation is making comprehensive data reporting. The growing health insurance coverage, the rising importance of medical denials , demand for automated medical claims processing systems, and the rise in the number of private insurance companies contribute to the healthcare claims management market growth.
