What Is Covered Under Benefits Navigation Platform Market? Benefits navigation platforms refer to digital platforms that assist members in exploring benefits and enable employers to track usage and engagement. The benefits navigation platforms provide personalized solutions to help members navigate their benefits, allow employers to understand utilization, and improve employee engagement. The main types of benefits navigation platforms are for employers, and for employees. Employer refers to a person, business, or other entity that hires and compensates individuals to work for them. The various solution is software, and services, that are applied to large enterprises, and small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) used in retail, healthcare, financial services, manufacturing, IT and telecom, public sector, and others. The growing adoption of remote working is expected to propel the growth of the benefits navigation platform market in the coming years. Remote working is an employment arrangement in which workers do not go to their primary place of work, such as a workplace, but instead do their job activities from a location outside of the usual office environment. Benefits navigation platforms can help to bridge this gap by providing employees with easy-to-access information about their benefits, including eligibility requirements, enrollment instructions, and how to use different benefits. For instance, in February 2023, according to the Office for National Statistics, a UK-based government department, between September 2022 and January 2023, 16% of working adults reported exclusively working from home, while a higher 28% reported a combination of working from home and commuting to work. Therefore, the growing adoption of remote working is driving the benefits navigation platform industry.
