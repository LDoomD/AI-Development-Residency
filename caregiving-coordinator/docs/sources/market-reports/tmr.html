Analyst Viewpoint

Rise in drug addiction is propelling the medication adherence packaging market development. Compliance-prompting packaging can help combat drug abuse and addiction. They can also reduce total healthcare costs by directly managing medications for complex chronic conditions.

Surge in geriatric population is also driving the medication adherence packaging market trajectory. Specialized medication packaging for the elderly is becoming increasingly relevant as medical care is more frequently administered at home or in day clinics.

Key players operating in the global medication adherence packaging market are developing smart labeling and packaging solutions for the pharmaceutical sector. These solutions employ Near-field Communication (NFC) or Bluetooth to help wirelessly monitor the use of medicines.

Market Introduction

Medication adherence packaging helps improve patient compliance with prescribed drugs. It can positively impact medication-taking behaviors in patients. Medical packaging has aided the development of effective medication management solutions, especially for the elderly, children, and those challenged by cognitive, physical, or functional impairment.

It uses new technologies to address reasons for non-adherence, increase patient confidence in medication management, and improve their perceived quality of life.

Plastics, paper and paperboard, aluminum, and various types of composite materials are used to manufacture compliance packaging. Polyethylene terephthalate (PET), High-density Polyethylene (HDPE), and Polypropylene (PP) are some of the plastics employed in medication adherence packaging.

Sachets, cartons, and blister pack backing are commonly used paper-based materials for packaging solid dosage forms such as tablets and capsules.

Aluminum protects medicines from light, oxygen, and moisture. It is gaining traction in the medication adherence packaging market landscape to ensure longer shelf life for medications and higher security for patients.

Paper-plastic composite materials and bio-composite materials that are renewable and biodegradable are also in high demand. Green Packaging made of biodegradable composite materials is presently generating a lot of attention in the pharmaceutical sector.

Rise in Drug Addiction Augmenting Medication Adherence Packaging Market Demand Market

Drug addiction has proven to be a challenge in many countries as evidenced by the opioid crisis. The opioid crisis has particularly hit the U.S. and Canada, affecting mostly young and middle-aged adults. In the U.S., over 115 million pills containing illicit fentanyl were seized by law enforcement in 2023.

Thus, government regulatory bodies such as the FDA are encouraging stakeholders in the pharmaceutical sector to develop innovative medical packaging designs to combat drug abuse and addiction. This is boosting the medication adherence packaging market size.

Blister packs help patients follow drug regimens as they make it less likely for a patient to double dose, miss a dose, or take the wrong dose. This has led to the R&D of intelligent blister packs that can store the time and date of dosage removal from the package.

These packs monitor the patient’s medication intake digitally through NFC or Bluetooth. In 2023, Schreiner MediPharm, a manufacturer of smart labeling and packaging solutions for the healthcare sector, developed its Smart Blister Card, which enables digital medication adherence monitoring.

Hence, R&D of intelligent blister packs is expected to spur the medication adherence packaging market growth in the near future.

Surge in Geriatric Population Propelling Market Progress

Compliance-prompting packaging helps improve medication adherence, patient safety, and patient health outcomes, especially in adult patients. Many countries are recording rapid growth in the elderly population. People aged 60 years or above are at risk of various chronic diseases.

They are also prone to non-adherence to several medications. By 2050, the world's population of people aged 60 years and older is projected to reach 2.1 billion. Moreover, the number of persons aged 80 years or older is expected to reach 426 million by 2050. Thus, rise in elderly population is fueling the medication adherence packaging market revenue.
