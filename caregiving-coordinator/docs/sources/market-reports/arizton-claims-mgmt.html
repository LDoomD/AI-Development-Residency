The healthcare claims management market size is projected to reach USD 12 billion by 2027, growing at a CAGR of 5% from 2021 to 2027. The market revenue in 2021 was USD 8.9 billion. A health insurance claim or a medical insurance claim is a request raised by a policyholder for compensation of expenses incurred during medical treatment. Based on the type of claim procedure opted by a client, the insurance company verifies the claim to either clear the bills directly with the hospital or reimburse the amount spent. The high adoption rate of health insurance policies among patients for financial assistance is driving the growth of the global healthcare claims management market.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has transformed various industries across the globe and radically can alter the healthcare field. With growing advancements in the healthcare industry, AI has become an efficient, faster, and inexpensive choice for claims management. The shift toward value-based solutions is encouraging healthcare companies to move toward automated medical claims management solutions. Medical billing is one of the significant parameters in healthcare revenue cycle management. The medical billing and their respective coding contribute to establishing and categorizing patient information that dictates the billing of Medicare and insurance companies.

Moreover, increased digitalization has accelerated the adoption of automation in the medical billing process. Most organizations are turning to automation to streamline and error-proof their billing systems. The significant advantage of medical billing process automation is making comprehensive data reporting. The growing health insurance coverage, the rising importance of medical denials, demand for automated medical claims processing systems, and the rise in the number of private insurance companies contribute to the healthcare claims management market growth.

GLOBAL HEALTHCARE CLAIMS MANAGEMENT MARKET SEGMENTS

This research report includes a detailed segmentation by

Component - Healthcare claims management software provides significant benefits to insurance organizations across a comprehensive range of key business areas. Claims management software is a powerful tool that ensures swift and successful medical claims processing. The global healthcare claims management software market is projected to witness the highest CAGR of approximately 5% during the forecast period.

Type - An integrated claims management solution includes workflow automation tools that can improve claims handling by driving informed decisions and productivity and contributing to rapid, cost-effective claims closure. The integrated segment in the healthcare claims management market is expected to reach over 5% during the forecast year.

Delivery mode - The demand for web & cloud-based medical claims management systems is likely to increase in the coming years due to several benefits and advanced features. In 2021, the global web & cloud-based healthcare claims management solutions market was valued at USD 6.80 billion and will witness growth in the upcoming years.

End-users –By end-user, the market is segmented into payers and providers, of which the providers are expected to witness the highest CAGR of 5.21% during the forecast period. Payers in the industry are change leaders who make effective changes to health plans and overall care services. However, payers also use existing technologies to digitize portions of the healthcare claims management process, which can enable them to gain significant value and competitive advantages.

GEOGRAPHICAL ANALYSIS

North America: Medical expenses in the US are much higher compared to other countries in the region. Various reasons contribute to the high medical inflation rate in the region. In 2021, the North American healthcare claims management market was valued at over 3 billion and will grow significantly in the upcoming years.

Europe: The rise in the number of surgeries performed in European countries every year drives the demand for health coverage. Moreover, in Europe, insurance companies that offer the best-in-class customer experiences are growing faster and gaining positive brand recognition. The healthcare claim management market is expected to grow in the region.

APAC: APAC is anticipated to emerge as the fastest-growing regional market due to the growing awareness of medical claims and the rising cost of medical treatments. Also, the increasing government investments in advancing the medical IT infrastructure are creating vast opportunities for claim management providers and contributing to the industry's growth.

Latin America: Most Latin American countries have a unique medical system. Also, medical services tend to be much cheaper in Latin American countries than in other parts of the world, thereby gaining traction in the industry.

The Middle East and Africa: With the growth in medical tourism, countries in the Middle East and Africa are gaining popularity due to their cost-effective surgical procedures. The Middle East & Africa (MEA) healthcare claims management market is expected to grow at a CAGR of 3.72% during the forecast period.

COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS

The healthcare claims management market is dominated by Cerner, McKesson, UnitedHealth Group, eClinicalWorks, and Cegedim. Major players are competing with the other prominent players through acquisitions, advanced product lines, and broader availability. Established companies are acquiring or collaborating with smaller vendors offering complementary products, technologies, or services to expand their presence in the marketplace. For instance, Cerner acquired AbleVets LLC, a health IT engineering and consulting company specializing in cybersecurity, cloud, and system development solutions for federal organizations, to enhance resource capabilities and growth opportunities within its federal business. Several key players are indulging in mergers and acquisitions to remain competitive and expand their reach through inorganic growth strategies.
