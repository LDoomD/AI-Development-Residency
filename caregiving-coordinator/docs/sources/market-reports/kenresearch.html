CHAPTER 1 - MARKET SUMMARY

Market Overview

The Global Digital Legacy Market converts personal digital records, online identities, encrypted credentials, financial assets and stored memories into organized instructions that can be accessed or administered after incapacity or death. Commercial demand is supported by 6.0 billion internet users in 2025, compared with approximately 4.7 billion in 2020, widening the population accumulating transferable digital property.

North America remains the principal revenue hub, accounting for approximately 38.14% of the market in 2025. High internet penetration, mature cloud infrastructure, established estate-planning channels and early integration by insurers and wealth platforms improve customer acquisition economics. The region also benefits from institutional crypto activity, including USD 2.3 trillion in transaction value received between July 2024 and June 2025.

Future Outlook

The Global Digital Legacy Market is projected to rise from USD 25,000 million in 2025 to USD 54,299 million by 2031, reflecting a forecast CAGR of 13.80%. Expansion will be supported by greater digital asset ownership, mainstream digital estate planning, platform-native legacy controls and institutional distribution. Revenue growth is expected to moderate from the 16.60% historical CAGR recorded during 2020-2025 as the market moves from early adoption toward scaled integration within insurance, wealth management, legal technology and employee-benefit ecosystems. Cloud delivery will remain the principal architecture, while hybrid models gain share among regulated institutions requiring tighter control over identity, consent and access records.

Managed legacy profiles are forecast to increase from approximately 410 million in 2025 to 774 million by 2031. Value growth will remain higher than profile growth because providers are adding beneficiary verification, AI-assisted document classification, probate support, crypto inheritance and institution-grade compliance features. The strongest profit-pool migration is expected toward B2B licensing, embedded financial-service distribution and transaction-linked estate administration. Asia Pacific should provide the fastest regional expansion, while North America remains the largest revenue pool. Downside risks include fragmented post-mortem privacy rules, weak consumer planning behavior, cybersecurity exposure and difficulty proving death, authority and beneficiary identity across borders.

Historical Market Performance (2020-2025)

The market added USD 13,400 million between 2020 and 2025, with 2025 representing the highest annual revenue level and 2020 the lowest. Managed profiles nearly doubled from 215 million to 410 million, while implied revenue per profile rose from USD 54.0 to USD 61.0. The widening spread between value and volume growth indicates greater monetization of verification, legal documentation, encrypted vault capacity and institutional workflows. The triangulated 2025 confidence range is USD 22,000-28,000 million, equivalent to a margin of error of approximately 12%, with service-scope definitions representing the largest uncertainty.

Forecast Market Outlook (2026-2031)

Forecast revenue reaches USD 54,299 million in 2031 as the market records a 13.80% CAGR. Managed profiles rise to 774 million, implying a volume CAGR near 11.10%, while revenue per profile increases to USD 70.2. Growth is expected to become more institution-led as banks, insurers, employers, law firms and funeral networks embed legacy services into existing customer relationships. The bull case exceeds USD 60,000 million if cross-border standards and embedded distribution accelerate, while the constrained case remains below USD 48,000 million if privacy fragmentation and consumer reluctance delay adoption.

Key Segmentation Takeaways

Comprehensive analysis across all extracted segmentation dimensions providing insights into market structure, consumer preferences, and distribution patterns.

Solution Type

Digital vault and asset inventory services generate the broadest revenue pool because they provide the foundational record layer required by wills, executor workflows and beneficiary access. The dominant proposition is shifting from passive document storage toward continuously updated inventories that link accounts, credentials, ownership evidence, consent instructions and named recipients within one secure environment.

Deployment Model

Hybrid deployment is the fastest-growing architecture as financial institutions and regulated service providers combine scalable cloud applications with private identity, consent and document repositories. Growth is led by configurations that keep sensitive authorization records under institutional control while using cloud services for customer onboarding, workflow automation, beneficiary communications and non-sensitive content preservation.

CHAPTER 8 - INDUSTRY ANALYSIS

Growth Drivers, Challenges & Opportunities

Comprehensive analysis of key factors shaping the Global Digital Legacy Market, including growth catalysts, operational challenges, and emerging opportunities across platform development, institutional distribution and consumer adoption.

Growth Drivers

Expansion of the Global Digital Asset Base

  • Internet use increased from 60% of the population (2020, global) to 74% in 2025, adding approximately 1.3 billion people who now create digital records requiring long-term administration.
  • High-income economies reached 94% internet penetration (2025, global high-income group), creating a commercially attractive concentration of users with cloud accounts, financial assets and higher willingness to purchase planning services.
  • The Americas and Europe recorded 88%-93% internet penetration (2025, regional), supporting low-cost digital onboarding and explaining their early leadership in subscription and institution-embedded legacy services.

Platform-Native Legacy Controls

  • Apple permits users to appoint one or more Legacy Contacts (2026, global service), creating a standardized access-key and death-certificate workflow that raises awareness of formal digital succession.
  • Google allows users to designate up to 10 recipients (2026, global service) through Inactive Account Manager, demonstrating demand for differentiated data-sharing instructions across beneficiaries.
  • Google may delete personal accounts after two years of inactivity (policy effective from 2023, global), increasing the economic value of advance inventories, reminders and automated preservation workflows.

Legal Recognition of Digital Property and Fiduciary Access

  • Revised UFADAA covers four fiduciary categories (current, United States): executors, guardians, agents under powers of attorney and trustees, expanding the institutional users requiring compliant digital-asset workflows.
  • The Property (Digital Assets etc) Act received Royal Assent in December 2025 (United Kingdom), clarifying that crypto-tokens and similar assets can attract property rights and strengthening inheritance-planning use cases.
  • GDPR Recital 27 states that the regulation does not apply to deceased persons, leaving 27 EU member states (current, European Union) able to develop national rules and creating demand for jurisdiction-aware compliance engines.

Market Challenges

Fragmented Post-Mortem Privacy and Succession Rules

  • UFADAA distinguishes communication content, communication catalogues and other digital assets across three access categories (current, United States), requiring granular permissions rather than a single executor credential.
  • European rules permit national treatment of deceased-person data across 27 member states (current, European Union), increasing localization costs and limiting the scalability of one standardized beneficiary workflow.
  • Apple requires both an access key and proof of death, representing two principal verification documents (2026, global service); comparable platform-specific processes create operational complexity for executors managing multiple accounts.

Cybersecurity, Fraud and Identity Exposure

  • The FBI received 1,008,597 complaints (2025, United States reports), demonstrating the scale of threat activity affecting digital identities, financial accounts and personal information stored by legacy platforms.
  • Cryptocurrency-related complaints were associated with USD 11.367 billion in losses (2025, United States reports), raising liability and verification requirements for providers offering wallet and token inheritance services.
  • Personal data breaches generated USD 1.315 billion in reported losses (2025, United States reports), making encryption, key segregation, audit trails and minimal-data architectures critical entry barriers.

Low Planning Completion and Persistent Digital Divide

  • Approximately 55% of Americans (2025, United States survey) have no estate plan, indicating that awareness does not automatically convert into completed digital directives or paid subscriptions.
  • About 2.2 billion people remained offline (2025, global), constraining demand in low-connectivity markets and limiting the effectiveness of digital-only beneficiary and executor processes.
  • Internet penetration was only 23% in low-income countries (2025, global low-income group), requiring assisted channels, simplified products and financial-inclusion partnerships before large-scale commercialization becomes viable.

Market Opportunities

Embedded Legacy Planning for Financial Institutions

  • A USD 25 million Series C round (2025, Trust & Will) supports expansion of SaaS tools for advisors, attorneys, institutions and member organizations, demonstrating a monetizable white-label and enterprise-licensing thesis.
  • FutureVault supports 4,000 partner professionals (2025, global operations), showing that advisors, banks, insurers and family offices can become scalable acquisition and retention channels.
  • FutureVault partner firms represent more than USD 600 billion in assets under management (2025, global operations); realizing this opportunity requires API integration, institutional controls and advisor-ready client workflows.

Crypto and Token Inheritance Infrastructure

  • North America represented 26% of global crypto transaction activity (2024-2025 period), creating a substantial addressable pool for secure instructions, multi-party recovery and executor verification.
  • India ranked first globally in crypto adoption (2025, Chainalysis index), indicating that future inheritance demand will extend beyond established Western estate-planning markets.
  • The United Kingdom recognized digital assets through legislation enacted in 2025 (United Kingdom); scalable commercialization requires equivalent legal clarity, beneficiary identity standards and custody-provider integration in additional jurisdictions.

AI-Assisted Curation and Legacy Administration

  • AI-related complaints generated USD 893 million in reported losses (2025, United States reports), creating a premium market for provenance controls, consent records and protection against unauthorized digital replicas.
  • Trust & Will plans to expand AI using insights from more than one million users (2025, United States), supporting personalized plan reviews, missing-asset detection and real-time recommendations for families and advisors.
  • FutureVault provides more than 1,000 API endpoints (2026, global platform), illustrating the infrastructure required to connect document intelligence, private AI, advisors and regulated record systems.

CHAPTER 12 - FAQ

FAQs

The Global Digital Legacy Market was valued at USD 25 billion in 2025. The estimate covers provider revenue from digital vaults, digital wills and directives, account memorialization, posthumous communications, probate workflows and enterprise platform licensing. The market's commercial base includes approximately 410 million monetized or institutionally managed legacy profiles, implying average revenue of about USD 61 per profile. North America represented the largest regional revenue pool, supported by mature estate-planning channels, widespread cloud adoption and clearer fiduciary-access frameworks than many emerging markets.

The Global Digital Legacy Market is forecast to reach USD 54,299 million by 2031, expanding at a CAGR of 13.80% during 2026-2031. Managed profiles are expected to rise to 774 million, while implied revenue per profile increases to approximately USD 70.2. Growth should be led by hybrid platform deployment, institutional distribution, digital-asset inheritance and higher-value verification services. The forecast assumes continued internet adoption, broader recognition of digital property and gradual integration of legacy planning into wealth, insurance, employee-benefit and probate ecosystems.

Profit pools will move from standalone consumer subscriptions toward B2B licensing, white-label platforms, advisor distribution and transaction-linked estate services. B2B2C channels represented approximately 39.0% of market revenue in 2025 and are projected to approach 48.0% by 2031. Institutional partners reduce customer acquisition costs, provide trusted access to affluent users and create opportunities to retain assets across generations. Providers with APIs, compliance controls, advisor dashboards and beneficiary workflows can generate higher contract values and lower churn than undifferentiated storage applications.

The most material constraint is the interaction between fragmented laws and cybersecurity exposure. Provider workflows must reconcile user consent, platform terms, fiduciary authority, privacy, intellectual property and beneficiary verification across jurisdictions. At the same time, the FBI recorded USD 20.877 billion in reported internet-crime losses during 2025, including USD 11.367 billion associated with cryptocurrency. A security failure involving account credentials or private documents could create direct liability, regulatory scrutiny and irreparable trust damage for a digital legacy provider.

North America offers the strongest current market position, while Asia Pacific provides the fastest growth outlook. North America generated approximately USD 9,535 million in 2025, equivalent to 38.14% of global revenue. Asia Pacific is forecast to expand at about 15.70% annually through 2031, supported by rising connectivity, digital finance and crypto adoption. Europe remains an important mature market, although differences in national treatment of deceased-person data create additional compliance requirements for providers operating across borders.

The accumulation of digital accounts and assets across a growing online population will have the greatest structural impact. Approximately 6.0 billion people, or 74% of the global population, used the internet in 2025, compared with 60% in 2020. Each additional connected consumer creates potential demand for account inventories, access instructions, memory preservation and digital-asset transfer. Adoption will accelerate when these capabilities are bundled into trusted products that consumers already use, including banking, insurance, employer benefits and estate-planning services.
