CHAPTER 8 - INDUSTRY ANALYSIS Growth Drivers, Challenges & Opportunities Comprehensive analysis of key factors shaping the Global Digital Legacy Market, including growth catalysts, operational challenges, and emerging opportunities across platform development, institutional distribution and consumer adoption. Growth Drivers Expansion of the Global Digital Asset Base Internet use increased from 60% of the population (2020, global) to 74% in 2025, adding approximately 1.3 billion people who now create digital records requiring long-term administration. High-income economies reached 94% internet penetration (2025, global high-income group), creating a commercially attractive concentration of users with cloud accounts, financial assets and higher willingness to purchase planning services. The Americas and Europe recorded 88%-93% internet penetration (2025, regional), supporting low-cost digital onboarding and explaining their early leadership in subscription and institution-embedded legacy services. Platform-Native Legacy Controls Apple permits users to appoint one or more Legacy Contacts (2026, global service), creating a standardized access-key and death-certificate workflow that raises awareness of formal digital succession. Google allows users to designate up to 10 recipients (2026, global service) through Inactive Account Manager, demonstrating demand for differentiated data-sharing instructions across beneficiaries. Google may delete personal accounts after two years of inactivity (policy effective from 2023, global), increasing the economic value of advance inventories, reminders and automated preservation workflows. Legal Recognition of Digital Property and Fiduciary Access Revised UFADAA covers four fiduciary categories (current, United States): executors, guardians, agents under powers of attorney and trustees, expanding the institutional users requiring compliant digital-asset workflows. The Property (Digital Assets etc) Act received Royal Assent in December 2025 (United Kingdom), clarifying that crypto-tokens and similar assets can attract property rights and strengthening inheritance-planning use cases. GDPR Recital 27 states that the regulation does not apply to deceased persons, leaving 27 EU member states (current, European Union) able to develop national rules and creating demand for jurisdiction-aware compliance engines. Market Challenges Fragmented Post-Mortem Privacy and Succession Rules UFADAA distinguishes communication content, communication catalogues and other digital assets across three access categories (current, United States), requiring granular permissions rather than a single executor credential. European rules permit national treatment of deceased-person data across 27 member states (current, European Union), increasing localization costs and limiting the scalability of one standardized beneficiary workflow. Apple requires both an access key and proof of death, representing two principal verification documents (2026, global service); comparable platform-specific processes create operational complexity for executors managing multiple accounts. Cybersecurity, Fraud and Identity Exposure The FBI received 1,008,597 complaints (2025, United States reports), demonstrating the scale of threat activity affecting digital identities, financial accounts and personal information stored by legacy platforms. Cryptocurrency-related complaints were associated with USD 11.367 billion in losses (2025, United States reports), raising liability and verification requirements for providers offering wallet and token inheritance services. Personal data breaches generated USD 1.315 billion in reported losses (2025, United States reports), making encryption, key segregation, audit trails and minimal-data architectures critical entry barriers. Low Planning Completion and Persistent Digital Divide Approximately 55% of Americans (2025, United States survey) have no estate plan, indicating that awareness does not automatically convert into completed digital directives or paid subscriptions. About 2.2 billion people remained offline (2025, global), constraining demand in low-connectivity markets and limiting the effectiveness of digital-only beneficiary and executor processes. Internet penetration was only 23% in low-income countries (2025, global low-income group), requiring assisted channels, simplified products and financial-inclusion partnerships before large-scale commercialization becomes viable. Market Opportunities Embedded Legacy Planning for Financial Institutions A USD 25 million Series C round (2025, Trust & Will) supports expansion of SaaS tools for advisors, attorneys, institutions and member organizations, demonstrating a monetizable white-label and enterprise-licensing thesis. FutureVault supports 4,000 partner professionals (2025, global operations), showing that advisors, banks, insurers and family offices can become scalable acquisition and retention channels. FutureVault partner firms represent more than USD 600 billion in assets under management (2025, global operations); realizing this opportunity requires API integration, institutional controls and advisor-ready client workflows. Crypto and Token Inheritance Infrastructure North America represented 26% of global crypto transaction activity (2024-2025 period), creating a substantial addressable pool for secure instructions, multi-party recovery and executor verification. India ranked first globally in crypto adoption (2025, Chainalysis index), indicating that future inheritance demand will extend beyond established Western estate-planning markets. The United Kingdom recognized digital assets through legislation enacted in 2025 (United Kingdom); scalable commercialization requires equivalent legal clarity, beneficiary identity standards and custody-provider integration in additional jurisdictions. AI-Assisted Curation and Legacy Administration AI-related complaints generated USD 893 million in reported losses (2025, United States reports), creating a premium market for provenance controls, consent records and protection against unauthorized digital replicas. Trust & Will plans to expand AI using insights from more than one million users (2025, United States), supporting personalized plan reviews, missing-asset detection and real-time recommendations for families and advisors. FutureVault provides more than 1,000 API endpoints (2026, global platform), illustrating the infrastructure required to connect document intelligence, private AI, advisors and regulated record systems. 2025? The Global Digital Legacy Market was valued at USD 25 billion in 2025. The estimate covers provider revenue from digital vaults, digital wills and directives, account memorialization, posthumous communications, probate workflows and enterprise platform licensing. The market's commercial base includes approximately 410 million monetized or institutionally managed legacy profiles, implying average revenue of about USD 61 per profile. North America represented the largest regional revenue pool, supported by mature estate-planning channels, widespread cloud adoption and clearer fiduciary-access frameworks than many emerging markets. What is the market forecast through 2031? The Global Digital Legacy Market is forecast to reach USD 54,299 million by 2031, expanding at a CAGR of 13.80% during 2026-2031. Managed profiles are expected to rise to 774 million, while implied revenue per profile increases to approximately USD 70.2. Growth should be led by hybrid platform deployment, institutional distribution, digital-asset inheritance and higher-value verification services. The forecast assumes continued internet adoption, broader recognition of digital property and gradual integration of legacy planning into wealth, insurance, employee-benefit and probate ecosystems. Where will the principal profit pools shift? Profit pools will move from standalone consumer subscriptions toward B2B licensing, white-label platforms, advisor distribution and transaction-linked estate services. B2B2C channels represented approximately 39.0% of market revenue in 2025 and are projected to approach 48.0% by 2031. Institutional partners reduce customer acquisition costs, provide trusted access to affluent users and create opportunities to retain assets across generations. Providers with APIs, compliance controls, advisor dashboards and beneficiary workflows can generate higher contract values and lower churn than undifferentiated storage applications. What is the most material market constraint? The most material constraint is the interaction between fragmented laws and cybersecurity exposure. Provider workflows must reconcile user consent, platform terms, fiduciary authority, privacy, intellectual property and beneficiary verification across jurisdictions. At the same time, the FBI recorded USD 20.877 billion in reported internet-crime losses during 2025, including USD 11.367 billion associated with cryptocurrency. A security failure involving account credentials or private documents could create direct liability, regulatory scrutiny and irreparable trust damage for a digital legacy provider. Which region offers the strongest market position and growth? North America offers the strongest current market position, while Asia Pacific provides the fastest growth outlook. North America generated approximately USD 9,535 million in 2025, equivalent to 38.14% of global revenue. Asia Pacific is forecast to expand at about 15.70% annually through 2031, supported by rising connectivity, digital finance and crypto adoption. Europe remains an important mature market, although differences in national treatment of deceased-person data create additional compliance requirements for providers operating across borders. What demand factor will have the greatest impact on adoption? The accumulation of digital accounts and assets across a growing online population will have the greatest structural impact. Approximately 6.0 billion people, or 74% of the global population, used the internet in 2025, compared with 60% in 2020. Each additional connected consumer creates potential demand for account inventories, access instructions, memory preservation and digital-asset transfer. Adoption will accelerate when these capabilities are bundled into trusted products that consumers already use, including banking, insurance, employer benefits and estate-planning services.
