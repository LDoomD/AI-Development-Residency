Headspace Premium Pricing in 2026: Is It Worth It? Oct 7, 2026 Headspace Premium is the paid subscription that unlocks the full Headspace library: guided meditations, sleep content, focus music, and movement sessions. The cost of Headspace runs $12.99/month on the monthly plan, or about $5.83/month when billed annually at $69.99/year. Student and family options bring the price lower. This breakdown covers every plan, who qualifies for each discount, and whether the subscription makes sense for your situation. Prices verified October 2026. What Is Headspace Premium? "Headspace Premium" is the common shorthand for any paid Headspace subscription, as opposed to the limited free tier. There is no plan officially named "Premium": Headspace sells monthly and annual subscriptions, plus Student and Family options. App stores also list a higher tier called Headspace Plus at $94.99/year, which adds mental health coaching and therapy features to the core meditation library. All paid plans unlock the full meditation library, sleep tools, focus music, and movement sessions. The differences come down to billing cycle, price, and whether you qualify for a discount. Pricing details are in the section below. Key Takeaways The monthly plan at $12.99 is expensive for a meditation app. The annual plan at $69.99/year ($5.83/month) is the better deal if you plan to stick with it. Students get a significant discount: $9.99/year, which is by far the cheapest way in. A 14-day free trial on the annual plan gives you enough time to evaluate the library before committing. Headspace Pricing Plans at a Glance Headspace offers four main pricing tiers. Here is what each one costs and who it's designed for. Monthly Plan: $12.99/month Full access to all content, billed monthly. Includes a 7-day free trial. This is the highest per-month cost and makes sense only if you're not sure you'll stick with it and want to try before committing annually. Annual Plan: $69.99/year (~$5.83/month) The most common choice. Billed once per year, this cuts the monthly cost by more than half. Comes with a 14-day free trial, which is the longest free window Headspace offers. If you're already reasonably confident you'll use it, the annual plan pays off within your sixth month. Student Plan: $9.99/year The best deal Headspace offers: roughly 86% off the standard annual price. Requires verification through SheerID. You'll need to be 18+, enrolled in a post-secondary program, and based in the US, UK, Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Portugal, or Spain. Acceptable documents include enrollment letters, class schedules, or tuition receipts. Re-verification is required annually. Family Plan Up to six accounts under a single annual subscription. One account manages billing and invites. Check headspace.com for current family pricing, as this plan is updated periodically. Is There a Free Version of Headspace? Yes, with limits. The free tier gives you access to a handful of introductory meditations, a few sleepcasts, and basic breathing exercises. It's enough to get a sense of Headspace's style and production quality, but not enough to build a sustained practice. The 14-day free trial on the annual plan is the more useful way to evaluate the full library. You get access to everything, no restrictions, and can cancel before being charged if it doesn't click. If you're on the fence, this is the path to take. Headspace is also eligible for HSA and FSA spending, which means you can pay with pre-tax dollars through a Health Savings Account or Flexible Spending Account. If you have one available, that effectively reduces the after-tax cost by 20-30% depending on your tax rate. What You Get with Headspace Headspace's library has grown considerably over the years. A paid subscription includes: Over 500 guided meditations, organized by theme (stress, anxiety, sleep, relationships, focus, and others) Sleep content: sleepcasts, wind-down routines, soundscapes, and sleep music Movement and yoga sessions Focus music designed for concentration and flow Mental health coaching as an optional add-on (additional cost) The production quality is high. Sessions range from quick two-minute exercises to longer thirty-minute practices. The body scan meditation format they use is particularly well-suited to the app. For beginners, the structured course approach is easier to follow than an open library. Teachers in the US, UK, Canada, and Australia get free access if they qualify under Headspace's educator program, and it's worth checking if you work in education. Is Headspace Worth the Price? It depends on how you'll actually use it. A few scenarios where it makes clear sense: The annual plan is worth it if you've already tried meditation before and know you'll use it consistently. At $5.83/month, it's priced in the same range as a streaming service and delivers more direct mental health value than most. Practicing noting meditation or working through a structured anxiety course is genuinely useful content if you return to it regularly. The student plan is worth it for almost anyone eligible. $9.99/year is less than a single coffee per month. Even occasional use justifies the cost at that price. The family plan is worth it for households of three or more, where the per-person cost drops well below any individual plan. The monthly plan is hard to justify unless you're genuinely unsure you'll stick with it. At $12.99/month versus $5.83 on the annual plan, you're paying a significant premium for flexibility. If you want to test before committing, use the 14-day free trial on the annual plan and cancel if it doesn't work. How Headspace Compares Headspace's biggest competitor is Calm , which runs around $69.99/year as well. The content styles differ: Calm leans more into sleep and ambient audio; Headspace is more structured and course-based. Both are well-made; the choice usually comes down to personal preference for tone and format. If you're specifically looking for health coaching apps with more personalization, there are options with human coaches rather than recorded content, though they come at a higher price point. Headspace's coaching add-on exists but isn't the core product. If sleep tracking and biometric data alongside mindfulness is what you want, a circadian rhythm app or a wearable paired with a lighter meditation practice might cover the same ground more specifically. The Headspace app is free to download, with a limited free tier. Unlocking the full library costs $12.99/month on the monthly plan or $69.99/year on the annual plan. Students pay $9.99/year. Those prices are verified as of October 2026 via Headspace's official listings. How much does Headspace cost? $12.99/month if you pay monthly, or $69.99/year (about $5.83/month) on the annual plan. The student plan is $9.99/year for eligible students. If you qualify for HSA or FSA reimbursement, the after-tax cost is effectively 20-30% lower depending on your tax bracket. Is there a Headspace lifetime subscription? No. Headspace does not offer a lifetime subscription. The longest commitment available is the annual plan at $69.99/year. There is no one-time purchase option for permanent access. What is the cheapest way to get Headspace? The student plan at $9.99/year is the cheapest option by far, provided you qualify. If you don't qualify, the family plan is cheaper per person when shared among multiple members. For individuals without those options, the annual plan at $69.99 is considerably cheaper than monthly billing. Does Headspace have a free trial? Yes. The monthly plan comes with a 7-day free trial. The annual plan comes with a 14-day free trial. There's also a limited free tier with a small selection of content if you want to get a sense of the app without signing up for a trial. The 14-day annual trial is the best way to evaluate the full library before committing. Can I use HSA or FSA funds for Headspace? Yes. Headspace is HSA and FSA eligible, which means you can pay for it with pre-tax health savings dollars. Depending on your tax bracket, this can reduce the effective cost by around 20-30%. Is Headspace cheaper than Calm? At the standard annual level, they're priced similarly. Calm's annual plan is typically around $69.99/year as well. Headspace's student plan is notably cheaper than Calm's. For most adults comparing standard plans, price isn't the differentiating factor. What happens when a student plan expires? When you graduate or fail re-verification, Headspace offers a 1-year alumni subscription at 40% off the standard annual price, then transitions you to regular pricing. You'll need to re-verify your student status each year to maintain the $9.99 rate. Does Headspace work on all devices? Headspace is available on iOS and Android, with a web version for desktop. A single subscription covers all your devices. There are also integrations with some wearables and health platforms, depending on your region and device.
