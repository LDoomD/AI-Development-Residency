CARILOOP FAQS
Benefit-eligible faculty and staff now have access to Cariloop©, a new caregiving benefit option provided by Michigan State University.
How much does Cariloop cost?
As part of Michigan State University’s commitment to helping employees on their caregiving journey, this benefit is provided at no cost to MSU employees. If an employee would like to receive professional coaching beyond the 30 continuous days provided, employees have the option to extend the coaching for $249/month.
