## The answer (approved)

**What market segment has the largest opportunity? Why?**
Helping worn-out caregivers get relief (segments 11 and 12).
- **The biggest need:** exhaustion is the #1 problem for both audiences, adult children and spouses.
- **Big enough:** the adult day care market alone is $19.3B worldwide (2025).
- **The need isn't met:**
  - Caregivers want breaks far more than they get them. Of 341 posts about respite, 128 had gotten a break, 78 couldn't get one and 81 were still trying.
  - Nationally, 39% of caregivers say a break would help, but only 13% have ever used respite (AARP/NAC 2025, PDF pp.26-27).
- **No one solves it:** there's no consumer app for getting relief. Burnout and meditation apps crowd the market, but caregivers almost never use them.
- **What stops caregivers:** the cost, a parent who says no, paperwork and rules to qualify, and family who won't help.
- **What the opportunity is not:** the care itself is a physical service. Our opportunity is helping caregivers reach relief, not providing the care.
