## EPIC 1: Remove the barriers that keep caregivers from getting a real break.

| Feature | Tasks |
|---|---|
| Getting real breaks they can afford *(A1)* | 1. Having family or paid help that steps in 2. Coping with guilt and grief, and knowing next steps 3. Setting limits without guilt or judgment |
| Getting real breaks and a life outside caring *(A2)* | 1. Having people who understand to lean on 2. Getting through the paperwork to qualify for help 3. Feeling close to their partner again |

## EPIC 2: Make sure caregivers don't carry the care alone, with relatives sharing the work.

| Feature | Tasks |
|---|---|
| Having siblings share the care and costs *(A1)* | 1. Having relatives agree and follow through 2. Being informed, heard and able to visit 3. Getting the parent the care doctors advised |
| Having relatives who support, not criticize *(A2)* | 1. Getting real hands-on help and someone to call 2. Being listened to without being judged |

## EPIC 3: Increase how often a parent or partner accepts outside help.

| Feature | Tasks |
|---|---|
| Getting the parent to accept doctors or help *(A1)* | 1. Seeing the parent take care of their health 2. Getting the parent to accept a safer move 3. Finding next steps the parent will accept |
| Bringing in outside help the partner accepts *(A2)* | 1. Planning together without causing anger 2. Settling who handles care tasks and money |

## EPIC 4: Make personal, ongoing guidance reachable without an employer or a high hourly bill.

| Feature | Tasks |
|---|---|
| Calming outbursts and calls, and staying calm themselves *(A1)* | 1. Handling hard talks about final wishes 2. Knowing if a change is the illness, and what's next 3. Being treated with respect, not accused |
| Understanding the behavior and how to respond *(A2)* | 1. Having hard talks without blame or yelling 2. Calming agitation and keeping their partner busy |

## EPIC 5: Make the money, papers and accounts manageable, even when nothing was prepared ahead.

| Feature | Tasks |
|---|---|
| Having legal authority and papers sorted in time *(A1)* | 1. Understanding and getting Medicaid and benefits 2. Controlling bills and stopping scams and overspending 3. Finding and affording the care the parent needs |

## EPIC 6: Protect caregivers' own health, job and money while they care.

| Feature | Tasks |
|---|---|
| Protecting their money and avoiding debt *(A2)* | 1. Keeping a home, food and healthcare 2. Keeping needed coverage and cutting unused costs 3. Getting paid and claiming credits for caregiving |
