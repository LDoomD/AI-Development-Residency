# Market analysis, part 1: Top caregiver problems (DRAFT for the owner, 2026-11-05)

**What this shows:** the problems family caregivers write about most, from the most common to the least. Every problem shows up for both audiences: adult children caring for a parent, and spouses or partners.

| # | The problem | Seen in | What caregivers run into |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Caregivers are worn out, guilty and alone.** | 260 posts and threads | • Carry the care alone while family stays away<br>• Can't get a break, even when help exists<br>• No one around who understands<br>• Guilt and grief over hard decisions |
| 2 | **Money, accounts and legal papers pile up.** | 156 | • Banks won't let them into a parent's accounts<br>• Sign-in codes go to the parent's phone<br>• Power of attorney missing or rejected<br>• Care costs outrun savings and insurance |
| 3 | **Caregiving takes over their own job, money and health.** | 143 | • Juggling care with a job, school or family<br>• Their own health, sleep and savings suffer |
| 4 | **Dementia behavior makes daily life and talks hard.** | 139 | • Anger, accusations or violence<br>• New confusion or strange behavior<br>• Hard talks about final wishes or car keys<br>• Nonstop anxious calls and questions |
| 5 | **Choosing, arranging and paying for a care home is hard.** | 125 | • Deciding where and when the parent should move<br>• A hospital stay forces a fast move<br>• Finding a place they can afford |
| 6 | **Family won't help or share the work.** | 125 | • Siblings refuse to help, even when asked<br>• Updates get lost in group chats<br>• Relatives fight over decisions |
| 7 | **Getting answers from doctors and records takes too long.** | 111 | • Months of waiting for tests or a diagnosis<br>• Too many patient portals, and family access blocked<br>• Doctors' offices don't share information |
| 8 | **They can't tell if the parent is safe.** | 105 | • Falls at home with no one there<br>• Wandering, or being left alone<br>• No way to know the parent is OK |
| 9 | **The parent or partner refuses help.** | 95 | • Says no to doctors, medicine or moving<br>• Turns down paid help or alert devices<br>• Won't give up the car |
| 10 | **Medicines are hard to manage.** | 74 | • Medicine changes aren't shared with family<br>• The parent forgets or refuses medicines<br>• Little training for nursing tasks |
| 11 | **Hands-on personal care falls on them.** | 17 | • Toileting, bathing and wound care<br>• Lifting someone who can't move<br>• Finding equipment and supplies |

**Where the numbers come from:** 529 caregiver posts and threads read in full: a random sample of 412 Reddit posts, plus 117 caregiving forum threads. "Seen in" counts the posts where the problem comes up at all. Row 11 counts only posts where it's the main topic, so its number is lower. The relief research (831 posts, 2026-11-05) confirms that row 1 is the top problem for both audiences.

**National numbers that agree:**
- 24% of caregivers feel alone, and 30% want help managing stress (AARP/NAC 2025, PDF p.53).
- 59% of dementia caregivers report high emotional stress (Alzheimer's Association 2026, Figure 12, printed p.52).

**Limits:** people who post online lean toward hard moments, so these numbers show patterns, not national rates.
