## 1. EPIC: Remove the barriers that keep caregivers from getting a real break. (Business goal 1 · KPI: % of caregivers who get a real break)
- **1.1 FEATURE (Audience 1): Getting real breaks they can afford**
  - 1.1.1 Having family or paid help that steps in
  - 1.1.2 Coping with guilt and grief, and knowing next steps
  - 1.1.3 Setting limits without guilt or judgment
- **1.2 FEATURE (Audience 2): Getting real breaks and a life outside caring**
  - 1.2.1 Having people who understand to lean on
  - 1.2.2 Getting through the paperwork to qualify for help
  - 1.2.3 Feeling close to their partner again

## 2. EPIC: Make sure caregivers don't carry the care alone, with relatives sharing the work. (Business goal 2 · KPI: % of caregivers whose relatives share the work)
- **2.1 FEATURE (Audience 1): Having siblings share the care and costs**
  - 2.1.1 Having relatives agree and follow through
  - 2.1.2 Being informed, heard and able to visit
  - 2.1.3 Getting the parent the care doctors advised
- **2.2 FEATURE (Audience 2): Having relatives who support, not criticize**
  - 2.2.1 Getting real hands-on help and someone to call
  - 2.2.2 Being listened to without being judged

## 3. EPIC: Increase how often a parent or partner accepts outside help. (Business goal 3 · KPI: % of caregivers whose parent or partner accepts outside help)
- **3.1 FEATURE (Audience 1): Getting the parent to accept doctors or help**
  - 3.1.1 Seeing the parent take care of their health
  - 3.1.2 Getting the parent to accept a safer move
  - 3.1.3 Finding next steps the parent will accept
- **3.2 FEATURE (Audience 2): Bringing in outside help the partner accepts**
  - 3.2.1 Planning together without causing anger
  - 3.2.2 Settling who handles care tasks and money

## 4. EPIC: Make personal, ongoing guidance reachable without an employer or a high hourly bill. (Business goal 4 · KPI: % of caregivers who get personal, ongoing guidance without an employer or a high hourly bill)
- **4.1 FEATURE (Audience 1): Calming outbursts and calls, and staying calm themselves**
  - 4.1.1 Handling hard talks about final wishes
  - 4.1.2 Knowing if a change is the illness, and what's next
  - 4.1.3 Being treated with respect, not accused
- **4.2 FEATURE (Audience 2): Understanding the behavior and how to respond**
  - 4.2.1 Having hard talks without blame or yelling
  - 4.2.2 Calming agitation and keeping their partner busy

## 5. EPIC: Make the money, papers and accounts manageable, even when nothing was prepared ahead. (Business goal 5 · KPI: % of caregivers who get the money, papers and accounts in order)
- **5.1 FEATURE (Audience 1): Having legal authority and papers sorted in time**
  - 5.1.1 Understanding and getting Medicaid and benefits
  - 5.1.2 Controlling bills and stopping scams and overspending
  - 5.1.3 Finding and affording the care the parent needs

## 6. EPIC: Protect caregivers' own health, job and money while they care. (Business goal 6 · KPI: % of caregivers who keep their own health, job and money while caring)
- **6.1 FEATURE (Audience 2): Protecting their money and avoiding debt**
  - 6.1.1 Keeping a home, food and healthcare
  - 6.1.2 Keeping needed coverage and cutting unused costs
  - 6.1.3 Getting paid and claiming credits for caregiving
