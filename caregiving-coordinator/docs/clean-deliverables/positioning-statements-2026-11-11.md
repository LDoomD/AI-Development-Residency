## Box 1. Target audiences of your product or service (Approved 2026-11-11)

- Adult Children (or children-in-law) caring for a parent
- Spouses or partners caring for a husband, wife or partner

Source: Target Audiences stickies, vision board `PQFzfyqTUQhAgEEe1P6ygs`, node 1:487 (Approved; written by the owner).

## Box 2. What problems are you trying to solve for audiences? (Approved 2026-11-11)

**Adult children caring for a parent**
1. The parent says no to doctors, help, moving or giving up the car.
2. Adult children carry the care, often alone, and can't get a break even when help exists.
3. A parent's anger, confusion and anxiety make visits, calls and big talks hard.
4. Care costs, benefits and legal papers pile up.
5. Relatives stay away, refuse to help or fight over decisions.

**Spouses or partners**
1. The partner denies needs and refuses help or changes.
2. Spouses carry the care, often alone, and lose health, sleep, closeness and breaks.
3. The partner's confusion and mood swings turn daily life and talks into fights.
4. They worry about income, insurance and treatment costs.
5. Family and friends criticize, doubt or drift away.

Source: the MVP #1 top 5 problems per audience, copied word for word from `vision-and-scope-boards-2026-11-10.md` (Approved 2026-11-10). The owner chose the MVP #1 top 5 over all MMP problems (2026-11-11).

## Box 3. Which other competitors are solving the same problems? Where are the gaps? (Approved 2026-11-11)

**Direct competitors**

| Competitor | What it is | Fully meets | Biggest weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caring Village | A shared family care app | None | Loses entries, shows medical details to friends, can't get relatives to join |
| Everplans | An online vault for documents and wishes | None | Useless unless the parent filled it in |
| Cariloop | A personal care coach | Knowing what to do | Only through employers or insurers |
| A Place for Mom | A free placement advisor | None | Nonstop sales calls; advisors know little about the places |
| Geriatric care managers | A local professional who manages the care | Knowing what to do; a real break | Cost; services vary |
| Care.com | A marketplace to hire aides | None | Aides don't show up or quit; vetting is on the family |

**The gaps in the market today**
1. No one handles a parent who refuses help, or gets relatives to share the work.
2. Personal, ongoing guidance is locked behind an employer or a high hourly bill.
3. Help with money and accounts works only if the parent prepared ahead of time.
4. Relief exists, but caregivers can't reach it.

Sources: competitive master report, tables 3, 4b and 4c, and section 5 "The 4 gaps" (Fact / Approved). Prices and counts were removed (no research numbers on the board). The template asks for "top 5 to 7 direct competitors"; the competitive analysis has exactly 6 direct ones.

Limits:
- Gaps 1 and 3 were measured for parents only. The Audience 2 statement leaves out the parent-only parts (same rule as the vision boards, Approved 2026-11-10).
- Respite and adult day services and VA caregiver programs fully meet "a real break" but are indirect, so they aren't in the table. Gap 4 covers them. The owner chose not to add a line for them (2026-11-11).

## Box 4. What impact will you have on the audience's problems? (Approved 2026-11-11)

**Adult children caring for a parent**
1. Getting the parent to accept doctors or help
2. Getting real breaks they can afford
3. Calming outbursts and calls, and staying calm themselves

## Box 5. How is your impact different from competitors' impact? (Approved 2026-11-11)

| Today, competitors… | Our impact is different: we aim to… |
|---|---|
| Fully meet a real break only for families who can pay or get in: care managers, respite and the VA | Remove the barriers that keep caregivers from getting a real break. |
| Can't get relatives to join; no one fully meets relatives sharing the work | Make sure caregivers don't carry the care alone, with relatives sharing the work. |
| Help with a parent who refuses, but nothing works once the parent says no | Increase how often a parent or partner accepts outside help. |
| Fully meet "knowing what to do" only through an employer (Cariloop) or for families who can pay (care managers) | Make personal, ongoing guidance reachable without an employer or a high hourly bill. |
| Help with money and papers only if the parent filled it in ahead of time (Everplans) | Make the money, papers and accounts manageable, even when nothing was prepared ahead. |

## Box 7. Write your product positioning statement (one per audience) (Approved 2026-11-11)

**AUDIENCE:** Adult Children (or children-in-law) caring for a parent

**Our product solves the following problems:**
- The parent says no to doctors, help, moving or giving up the car.
- Adult children carry the care, often alone, and can't get a break even when help exists.
- A parent's anger, confusion and anxiety make visits, calls and big talks hard.
- Care costs, benefits and legal papers pile up.
- Relatives stay away, refuse to help or fight over decisions.

**Other businesses in the space:** Care managers, respite and the VA give real breaks, but only to families who can pay or get in. Cariloop gives guidance only through an employer. Caring Village can't get relatives to join. Everplans is useless unless the parent filled it in. Nothing works once the parent says no.

**Unlike other businesses, we** focus on getting caregivers to the real breaks that already exist; getting a parent to accept help and relatives to share the work; personal, ongoing guidance without an employer or a high hourly bill; and the money, papers and accounts, even when the parent didn't prepare ahead.
