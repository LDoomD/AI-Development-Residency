## How the instructor organizes it (Figma [2199:2624](https://www.figma.com/board/HcDDdEGAnikTImO8v7UPMp/?node-id=2199-2624))

Separate sections side by side, left to right. Each section has a title, a one-line definition, then one table or answer:

1. **About market research** and **AI prompts** (teaching material)
2. **Possible market segments:** one table (#, segment, who it groups, problem it addresses, example organizations, sector)
3. **Market size:** one table (market, segments, audience fit, size today, growth, top companies, source, notes)
4. **Market opportunity:** one table (segment, big enough?, few competitors?, unmet need?, verdict), plus the question "What market segment has the largest opportunity? Why?" and its answer
5. **Audience segmentation definition:** bullet facts about the audience
