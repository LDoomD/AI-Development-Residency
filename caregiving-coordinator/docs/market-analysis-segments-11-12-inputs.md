## Questions the reading pass answers

From the earlier exhaustion research, not new:
1. What do caregivers use for relief: respite, adult day, therapy, support groups, or any of the 10 offerings?
2. What actually helps them?
3. **What stops them from using it?** Tests AARP's reasons (inflexible programs, too few providers, hard applications, trust, shame, quality) against caregivers' own words, plus cost, waitlists and the parent refusing.
4. Who provides the help they name?
5. Who pays, and what does it cost?
