# Market Research: FigJam Template Prompts 1 and 2, Run for Caregiver Coordination

**Prepared:** 2026-10-29. Draft for review, not yet placed on the FigJam board.
**Method:** the board's two prompts, adapted to this product (wording in `figjam-market-research-review.md`). Every number is from a named source. Where sources disagree, the range is shown.

**Idea summary used in Prompt 1:** an experience that helps several family members coordinate care for an aging or ill relative, so appointments, medications, refills and doctor and pharmacy details are known and acted on.

## Prompt 2: Channels, competitors, size, growth

The table gives, per segment: channels, direct competitors, indirect competitors, size, growth. "Not found" means my searches did not surface it.

### 1. Family caregiver coordination apps
- **Channels:** app stores, direct download; employer and health plan resale; caregiver communities.
- **Direct:** Caring Village, Jointly, ianacare, Carely, plus about 15 new App Store entries (HuddleCare, Elderella, Hyey, CircleCare, FairNest, Idyll and others, mostly fewer than 10 ratings).
- **Indirect:** group chats, shared calendars, spreadsheets, Google Docs.
- **Size / growth:** $4.80B in 2026, $16.90B by 2034, 16.9% a year (StrategyMRC). Narrower matching platforms: $0.64B, 16.9% (Fact.MR).
- **Funding signals:** ianacare $12.1M (date not shown); Helpful $7.5M seed (2023); Hera $27M Series A (June 2026, senior care coordination).
- **Evidence on quality:** 17 of 54 recent Caring Village App Store reviews were 3 stars or below (calendar sync, tasks failing, sign-up screen blocked, member lockout).

### 2. Medication management apps
- **Channels:** app stores, pharmacies, health plans.
- **Direct:** Medisafe (101,530 App Store ratings), Hero (10,750), MyTherapy, CareClinic, Max, EveryDose.
- **Indirect:** phone alarms, paper pill boxes, Apple Health.
- **Size / growth:** $9.11B in 2026, 12.45% a year (Fortune Business Insights). Alternate: $6B, 17.9% (Research Nester). Consumer apps only: $3.5B (Verified Market Research).
- **Evidence on quality:** 64 of 100 recent Medisafe reviews were 3 stars or below (reminders repeat after a dose is logged, one-month history limit, data wrong after a device change).

### 3. Online and mail-order pharmacies
- **Channels:** health plan benefits, retail chains, direct web and app.
- **Direct:** CenterWell (Humana), Amazon Pharmacy, PillPack, Capsule, Express Scripts, retail pharmacies such as CVS.
- **Indirect:** doctors' offices, patient portals.
- **Size / growth:** mail-order $151.7B in 2026, 18.2% a year (Business Research Company). Other firms: $130B to $177B, 12% to 19%.
- **Process fact:** CenterWell says renewal is manual: you press Renew, and it contacts your prescriber, who may take up to 6 days to respond. Its auto refill covers existing refills only.
- **Not found:** company-level data for Amazon Pharmacy, PillPack, Capsule.

### 4. AI visit recording and notes
- **Channels:** app stores (patient-side), health system contracts (clinician-side).
- **Direct, patient-side:** Kin (4.9 stars, 1,071 ratings, released April 2026), AlignCare, Neatly, VisitNotes, Hoy.
- **Indirect:** clinician-side scribes inside health systems, notes apps, general voice recorders.
- **Size / growth:** AI medical scribing $1.82B in 2026, 19.6% a year to 2040 (one firm). Others: $1.67B, 20.48%; ambient documentation 28.8%; US only $621.66M, 25.12%. These are mostly clinician-facing.
- **Evidence on quality:** Kin reviewers raise questions about who controls the visit summary and whether recordings go to outside AI providers.
- **Not found:** a patient-side market size.

### 5. Patient portals and proxy access
- **Channels:** sold to health systems; patients get them through their providers.
- **Direct:** Epic MyChart, athenahealth, Oracle Health, eClinicalWorks.
- **Size / growth:** $7.77B in 2026, 18.4% a year (Mordor Intelligence). Others: $2.64B base at 6.6%, up to 18.7%.
- **Gap fact:** some hospitals do not offer proxy accounts for adult patients' caregivers, and staff may suggest sharing the patient's password. A shared login exposes the full record. Policies vary by health system.
- **Not found:** reliable market-share figures.

### 6. Employer-sponsored caregiver benefit services
- **Channels:** employers, health plans, benefits brokers.
- **Direct:** Wellthy, Cariloop, Carallel, Maven (expanding beyond maternity), Care.com Care@Work, Homethrive, Grayce.
- **Indirect:** employee assistance programs, ianacare.
- **Size:** no reliable figure found. A 2016 AARP estimate put the broader caregiver market at $42.9B, projected to reach $72B by 2020 (stale).
- **Scale signals (conflicting):** Cariloop 1.7M lives, Wellthy 2.0M, Carallel 0.4M. Carallel raised $8.2M (Series A).
- **Role:** these use human coaches and are paid by employers. Channel to consider: employers pay, not families.

### 7. Care journals and update-sharing
- **Direct:** CaringBridge (19,469 App Store ratings, 4.9 stars, nonprofit), Lotsa Helping Hands (free sign-up calendar).
- **Evidence on quality:** CaringBridge reviews cite repeated donation prompts, confusing navigation, and forcing invitees to make an account.
- **Size:** not found as a separate segment.

### 8. Smart pill dispensers
- **Direct:** Hero ($99.99 start plus $29.99 a month per one review), MedMinder, Pillo, Livi ($130 plus about $99 a month), Spencer Health.
- **Size / growth:** $3.7B in 2026, 6.7% a year (Global Market Insights). Others: $3.62B at 7.24%; smart segment 11.9%.
- **Evidence on quality:** Hero reviews mention billing that continued after a death and AI-only support.

### 9. Geriatric care managers
- **Direct:** local practices, Aging Life Care Association members.
- **Price:** about $75 to $250 an hour (consumer sites).
- **Size:** no segment-specific figure. Broader geriatric care services: about $1.4T to $1.475T, 5.1% to 6.25% a year (two firms).

### 10. Insurance and benefits navigation
See `caregiving-market-research-report.md` (section 3): $12.25B in 2026, 7.52% a year (Mordor Intelligence).

## Gap map (hypotheses, not conclusions)

Marks: **Yes** = a source says it exists. **Partial** = exists with limits. **Not found** = my searches did not surface it. **Unverified** = I did not check.

| What a family needs | Closest existing | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Shared place for siblings: tasks, calendar, notes | Caring Village, Jointly, ianacare | Yes, but reviews cite bugs and sync problems |
| Claim-a-task sign-up for appointments | Lotsa Helping Hands (volunteer help calendar) | Partial: built for volunteers, not a sibling team |
| Announce and claim by text message | None surfaced | Not found |
| One shared fact sheet (doctors, pharmacy hours, medications, auto-refill source) | Caring Village documents; Medisafe lists medications | Partial. Doctor and pharmacy directory in one place: unverified |
| Visit notes from a recording | Kin and four newer apps | Yes. Trust and data-handling concerns in reviews |
| Medication list and dose reminders | Medisafe, Hero | Yes |
| Watching refill counts and chasing renewals before they run out | Pharmacies' own Renew buttons (CenterWell) | Partial and manual. App-level watching: not found, unverified |
| Showing where a renewal request got stuck (pharmacy, doctor, delivery) | None surfaced | Not found |
| Travel time and leave-by time tied to the appointment | General calendar apps | Unverified |
| Shared access to portals without sharing a password | Proxy accounts, uneven by health system | Partial |
| Human coaching and navigation | Wellthy, Cariloop, Carallel | Yes, paid by employers |

### What this suggests (for the founder to test, not proven)
1. **Visit notes are crowded.** Do not lead with them.
2. **Family coordination apps exist but have weak reviews.** Reliability is an opening.
3. **Two items were not found at all:** announce-and-claim by text, and visibility into where a refill renewal got stuck.
4. **Employers are the buyers in the one segment with real money flowing**, which raises the question of who pays.

### Unchecked
- Google Play ratings and installs.
- Whether Medisafe, Caring Village or Jointly track refills or doctor contacts.
- How many caregivers have a refill problem. So far that rests on one founder's account and one forum thread.
- Primary sources for funding rounds and market reports.
