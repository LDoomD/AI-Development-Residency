# ADR-NNNN: Short title of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decided by:** name

## Context

What situation forced a decision? Two to five sentences. Include the
constraints (budget, time, data sensitivity, team size).

## Decision

What was decided, stated as one clear sentence, then any detail needed.

## Options considered

1. **Chosen option.** Why it won.
2. **Other option.** Why it was rejected.
3. **Another option (if any).** Why it was rejected.

## Consequences

- **Good:** what this makes easier.
- **Bad:** what this makes harder or what risk is accepted.
- **Enforcement:** the `arch-gate.config.json` rule name, or "cannot be
  checked by search because ...".
