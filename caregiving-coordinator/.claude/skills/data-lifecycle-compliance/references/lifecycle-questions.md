# Lifecycle questions

**Collect**
- Is each field needed for a stated purpose? Remove the rest.
- Are users told what is collected and why, before it is collected?
- Is permission asked where it is needed, and can it be withdrawn?

**Use**
- Is the data used only for the stated purpose?
- Is it ever used to train or improve a model? By whom?

**Share**
- Which third parties receive it (hosting, analytics, email, AI services)?
- What exactly do they receive, and what do their terms say about keeping or
  training on it?
- Is it sent outside the user's region?

**Keep**
- How long is each kind kept, and who decided that?
- Is it encrypted in storage and in transit?
- Who can read it (roles, staff, support), and is access logged?
- Is it in logs, backups, analytics, or error reports as well?

**Delete**
- Can a user delete their data and their account?
- Does deletion reach backups, logs, and third parties, and how long does that
  take?
- Can a user export their data?
- What happens to data when a user leaves a shared group?

**Incident**
- If this data leaked, who would be told, in what time, and by whom?
- Is there a record of who accessed what?
