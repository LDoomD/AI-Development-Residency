# Requirements

1. Choose a specialization area
2. Write a research paper about the specialization with the following topics:

   1. Introduction to the subject
   2. Why it’s relevant to your career
   3. Background about the specialization
   4. How does it work in the context of building products?
   5. What does it include?
   6. What activities does this specialization area involve that people must do on teams?
   7. What tools and skills do you nee to learn to get good at the specialization?
   8. What kind of app will you build that uses this specialization?
3. Cite sources in APA style with a bibliography at the end of the paper. Place in-line citations. DO NOT PLAGERIZE.
4. You can write the paper with Claude, but make it polished an human readable. Evaluate the output.
