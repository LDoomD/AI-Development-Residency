# Week 2 PREMIUM RESIDENCY - Write a Research Paper and Proposal for Your Specialization Project

This is a **separate, Premium-only assignment** from the Week 2 "Develop Product Specifications"
coding challenge in `week-2-3.md`. Premium students do both, as two distinct parts of Week 2:

1. **This page** — a research paper on the chosen specialization area, written first, since it's
   where the specialization area actually gets chosen and justified.
2. **The other Week 2 coding challenge** (`week-2-3.md`) — audience segmentation, market analysis,
   vision/scope, etc. for the specialization app itself, done after this paper.

## Standard Residency Objective

STANDARD RESIDENTS DO NOT COMPLETE THIS ASSIGNMENT.

## Premium Residency Objective

You are not building an app for people with ADHD to budget — you are building the app you chose
based on your specialization. Your objective is to choose a specialization area and write a
research paper about the subject to learn about it deeply. Propose the vision for your
application that will be the project you build for the Premium Residency.

## Tools

1. Claude Code

## Specialization Areas

1. AI ethics
2. AI Design
3. AI Automation
4. Content management systems
5. Local AI models
6. Advanced information security
7. Advanced agentic workflows
8. Web performance
9. Advanced data engineering
10. Conversational design
11. Something you choose (the instructor must approve)

## Requirements

1. Choose a specialization area.
2. Write a research paper about the specialization with the following topics:
   1. Introduction to the subject
   2. Why it's relevant to your career
   3. Background about the specialization
   4. How does it work in the context of building products?
   5. What does it include?
   6. What activities does this specialization area involve that people must do on teams?
   7. What tools and skills do you need to learn to get good at the specialization?
   8. What kind of app will you build that uses this specialization?
3. Cite sources in APA style with a bibliography at the end of the paper. Place in-line citations.
   DO NOT PLAGIARIZE.
4. You can write the paper with Claude, but make it polished and human readable. Evaluate the
   output.
